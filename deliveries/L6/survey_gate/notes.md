# Survey gate layout and light (L6 candidate)

- `outside.png`: a closed underground shopping arcade, shutters on both sides and at the back, blank signboards, attached ceiling pipes, dusty dry terrazzo. The F-grade slit stands against the rear concrete pier between two shutters. No people or readable signs.
- Cool-white ceiling fluorescents are the key, alternating lit/unlit fixtures. The gate has a black-violet centre and a thin pale cold white-cyan rim. Keep its light small; the selected second attempt removes the first attempt’s broad wet floor reflection.
- `key.png`: a ruined Far Side granary. Two round silos flank a narrow lower passage; another cylinder stands behind. The foreground entry landing descends by shallow steps; linked narrow wall stairs turn up between silo walls at the rear. Grain spills at bases and on the lower floor.
- Warm amber dusk enters slit windows at picture-left/high outer walls and throws oblique bands across stone and grain; shadows stay cool slate-violet. This is sunset on stone, not Nib’s magical amber glow.
- Two tiny generic adults, one paused near the left silo and one farther along the passage, give 1.78 m scale. Nominal cylinders about 6 m tall/diameter; stairs 18 cm rise, passage about 1.5 m, stone parapets about 1.1 m. Keep the curved walls, connected steps and common floor plane coherent.
