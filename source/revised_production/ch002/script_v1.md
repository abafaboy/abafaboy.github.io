# My Summons Go First
## Chapter 2: A Place to Sit

**Scope:** The first several minutes after Nib arrives. One room, two frightened people, a misunderstanding, and a small agreement. No registration, assessment, hunt, power test, or offscreen progress.

**Continuity:** Begins in the exact final positions of Chapter 1. Taeo is beside the apartment door, one empty hand raised. Nib stands in the space Taeo cleared. The folding table is against the wall; the laundry rack has been pushed back. Taeo's phone and used meal cup are on the kitchenette counter.

**Room geography for all panels:** The entrance is at the near end of the room. Facing inward from it, the kitchenette runs along the left wall; the narrow bed sits against the right wall. The window is at the far end. Nib initially stands between the bed and kitchenette. Taeo initially stands between Nib and the only exit. Preserve those positions through changes of camera angle. The folded table and laundry rack remain outside the central walking space. The room is cramped, not a maze.

**Visual direction:** Grounded vertical-scroll manhwa. Clear adult anatomy and consistent character models. Nib is a small, lean adult goblin, not a child, mascot, or chibi. Carry emotion through posture, gaze, fingers, distance, and interrupted movements. Reaction panels should change the emotional reading, not repeat it. No additional interface panels are needed. Taeo's brief account of what he saw is a fallible description, not a new system rule.

## Scene 1 — An answer, please

**Panel 1**

Continue directly from Chapter 1. A two-shot establishes the gap between them.

Nib's hand hovers beside his sheathed scouting blade. Taeo's raised palm has gone loose.

**TAEO:** You can talk.

**Panel 2**

Nib glances at the closed door behind Taeo, then back at his face.

**NIB:** Where am I?

The repeated question is firmer this time.

**Panel 3**

Taeo lowers his hand slowly.

**TAEO:** My apartment.

Nib does not look reassured.

**TAEO:** My room. I live here.

**Panel 4**

Nib looks around without moving his feet: the bed, the clothes drying overhead, the small window.

His thumb touches the edge of his belt pouch.

**NIB:** How did I get here?

**Panel 5**

Taeo's gaze catches on the blade.

**TAEO:** Could you move your hand away from that?

**Panel 6**

Nib looks at Taeo's hand, now hanging by his side, then at the door.

**NIB:** Move away from the door.

**Panel 7**

Taeo looks behind himself.

Only now does he notice where he is standing.

**Panel 8**

Wide view. Taeo edges sideways toward the kitchenette, leaving a clear path from Nib to the entrance.

He stops short of the counter. Both hands stay visible.

**TAEO:** All right.

**Panel 9**

Nib takes his hand off the blade.

He does not relax the rest of his body.

A narrow silent beat holds the now-open line to the door.

## Scene 2 — Who did this?

**Panel 10**

Taeo looks at the empty patch of floor beneath Nib's feet.

**TAEO:** I think I brought you here.

**Panel 11**

Nib's face tightens.

**NIB:** You think?

**Panel 12**

Taeo begins to gesture, realizes how abrupt it looks, and stops.

**TAEO:** There was something in front of me. Words. A button.

**TAEO:** I pressed it. Then you appeared.

**Panel 13**

Nib studies the air between them.

There is nothing there for the reader to inspect. Do not imply that Nib can or cannot see an active interface; none is presently displayed.

**NIB:** Why?

**Panel 14**

Taeo opens his mouth.

A silent beat before he answers honestly.

**TAEO:** I wanted to see what would happen.

**Panel 15**

Nib stares at him.

Taeo looks down first.

**TAEO:** I didn't know it would be someone.

**Panel 16**

Nib's fingers close around his own wrist, well away from the blade.

**NIB:** Send me back.

**Panel 17**

Taeo looks up.

**TAEO:** I don't know how.

**Panel 18**

Nib takes one quick step toward him.

Taeo backs into the edge of the kitchenette counter.

A spoon beside the meal cup rattles against it.

SFX: **TIK.**

**Panel 19**

Both freeze at the sound.

Nib sees Taeo gripping the counter behind him. Taeo sees that Nib has still not drawn the blade.

**Panel 20**

Nib steps back to the edge of the cleared floor.

**NIB:** Then find out.

**Panel 21**

Taeo releases the counter one finger at a time.

**TAEO:** I'll try.

Nib keeps watching him. The answer is not enough to make this all right.

## Scene 3 — Names

**Panel 22**

Nib moves toward the window along the foot of the bed, keeping Taeo in the corner of his eye.

Taeo remains at the kitchenette. He does not follow.

**TAEO:** Nib—

**Panel 23**

Nib turns sharply before reaching the window.

**NIB:** How do you know my name?

**Panel 24**

Taeo points at the space where the result label had appeared, then lets the gesture drop.

**TAEO:** It was written beside you. When you arrived.

**Panel 25**

Nib looks at that empty space.

His mouth presses into a thin line.

**NIB:** What else?

**TAEO:** Goblin scout. That's all I saw.

**Panel 26**

Nib checks his belt pouch by touch. The familiar knot is still tied.

He looks back at Taeo.

**NIB:** Your name.

**TAEO:** Taeo.

**Panel 27**

Nib repeats it quietly, fixing the sound in his memory.

**NIB:** Taeo.

Taeo gives a small nod. No smile or bonding flourish.

**Panel 28**

Nib reaches the window and lifts only the edge of the curtain.

Outside: the opposite apartment building, a lit window, ordinary city traffic below.

Keep the view from Nib's eye level; he can see out without climbing on the bed.

**Panel 29**

Close on Nib's hand holding the curtain.

His grip tightens.

No dialogue. Leave enough vertical space for the unfamiliar view to land.

**Panel 30**

From the kitchenette, Taeo can see only Nib's back.

**TAEO:** Do you recognize anything?

A beat.

**NIB:** No.

**Panel 31**

Nib releases the curtain. It settles slowly.

He stays near the far wall, choosing distance from both Taeo and the window.

Taeo starts to ask another question, but stops when he sees Nib's face.

## Scene 4 — Water

**Panel 32**

Nib rubs a thumb over his dry lower lip.

Taeo notices. His eyes move to a clean cup beside the sink.

**TAEO:** Do you want water?

**Panel 33**

Nib looks at the cup, then Taeo.

**NIB:** From where?

**Panel 34**

Taeo gestures toward the tap instead of suddenly turning it on.

**TAEO:** Here.

**Panel 35**

Nib says nothing. Taeo opens the tap a little.

A thin stream runs into the sink.

Nib watches the water emerge, his head tilted slightly. Curious, still guarded; no exaggerated comedy.

**Panel 36**

Taeo fills the clean cup and shuts off the tap.

He turns slowly, holding it where Nib can see.

**TAEO:** Where should I put it?

**Panel 37**

Nib points to the floor near the foot of the bed, halfway between them.

Taeo looks at the spot, then nods.

**Panel 38**

Taeo crouches and sets the cup down.

He returns to the kitchenette before Nib moves.

Show the complete exchange in a wide panel. No unexplained teleporting across the room.

**Panel 39**

Nib crouches beside the cup but does not pick it up.

His gaze returns to Taeo.

**NIB:** You first.

**Panel 40**

Taeo pauses, then moves toward the cup.

Nib immediately rises and backs away to the far end of the bed.

Taeo stops until Nib has made the distance he wants.

**Panel 41**

Taeo picks up the cup and takes a normal swallow.

His hand trembles enough to make a small ring of water move against the rim.

Nib watches his throat, not the cup.

**Panel 42**

Taeo replaces the cup at the same spot and retreats to the kitchenette again.

Nib waits another beat.

**Panel 43**

Nib lifts the cup with both hands and drinks.

One cautious sip. Then a longer one.

Taeo looks away to give him a little privacy.

**Panel 44**

Taeo's phone vibrates against the counter.

SFX: **BRR.**

Nib jerks. Water spills over his fingers.

Taeo's own shoulders jump.

**Panel 45**

Taeo places his open hand beside the phone without picking it up.

**TAEO:** It's mine. Just a message.

The screen is angled away; no readable notification or new phone UI is needed.

**Panel 46**

Nib looks from the phone to the water on the floor.

His ears tilt back with irritated embarrassment.

**NIB:** I wasn't asking.

Taeo does not challenge him.

**Panel 47**

Taeo takes a dish towel from its hook, then pauses.

**TAEO:** May I?

His gaze indicates the spill, not Nib.

**Panel 48**

Nib moves his feet out of the wet patch.

Taeo kneels and wipes it up, staying out of arm's reach.

Nib holds the cup, watching this ordinary, unhurried act.

## Scene 5 — Not an order

**Panel 49**

Taeo returns the damp towel to the sink. He stays there rather than closing the distance again.

**TAEO:** I'm sorry I brought you here.

**Panel 50**

Nib looks down into the cup.

**NIB:** You still did.

**TAEO:** I know.

No reassurance follows. Let the admission remain uncomfortable.

**Panel 51**

Nib glances toward the door. The route is still clear.

**NIB:** What's outside that?

**TAEO:** A hallway. Stairs down to the street.

**Panel 52**

Nib considers the answer.

**NIB:** More people like you?

**TAEO:** Yes.

**Panel 53**

Taeo shifts his weight off one tired leg. He seems about to say more, then chooses something smaller.

**TAEO:** You can sit down. If you want.

He indicates the bed, but does not approach it.

**Panel 54**

Nib looks at the mattress.

Then he lowers himself to the floor beside the foot of the bed instead, back to the bed frame, facing Taeo and the entrance.

His blade remains sheathed and accessible.

**NIB:** Here.

**Panel 55**

Taeo takes that in without correcting him.

He sits on the floor against the kitchenette cabinet, across the cleared space. He lowers himself slowly; his knees have been stiff with tension.

At last, neither is standing over the other.

**Panel 56**

Wide, quiet panel.

Two people at opposite sides of a small room. Nib has the cup. Taeo rests his empty hands on his knees.

The door remains visible between them in the near background.

**TAEO:** When I said “stay”…

**Panel 57**

Nib looks up immediately.

**TAEO:** I thought you'd only understand simple words.

**NIB:** I understood you.

**Panel 58**

Close on Taeo. He hears the distinction.

**TAEO:** Right.

A pause, with no thought caption explaining it.

**Panel 59**

Taeo looks at the door, then back at Nib.

**TAEO:** Would you rather wait here for a while?

**TAEO:** While we work out what to do.

**Panel 60**

Nib turns the cup slowly between his hands.

He considers the room, the closed curtain, and finally Taeo. Give this decision room to breathe.

**NIB:** For a while.

**Panel 61**

Taeo nods once.

He does not grin, offer a handshake, or treat the agreement as a victory.

**TAEO:** All right.

**Panel 62**

A low angle across the floor. The cup is nearly empty.

Nib holds it out a little, staying seated.

**NIB:** More water.

**Panel 63**

Taeo begins to stand, then checks the distance between them.

Nib sets the cup on the floor and slides it partway across.

This time he does not retreat.

**Panel 64**

Final tall panel. Taeo fills the cup at the kitchenette, turned just enough that Nib can still see his hands.

Nib sits by the bed frame in the same guarded position. His shoulders have lowered a fraction.

The apartment is unchanged. The second person in it is not.

No dialogue. No system reward, loyalty meter, new threat, or teaser replacing the ending.

**END CHAPTER 2**

## Brief causal and pacing audit

- **The previous cliffhanger receives a real answer.** Nib's repeated question forces Taeo past surprise and into an explanation. The chapter spends its full length on the consequence of bringing a person into his home.
- **Each defensive response has a visible cause.** Taeo fears the blade; Nib sees an unknown person blocking the exit. Taeo creates an escape route before Nib removes his hand from the weapon. Neither becomes fearless.
- **Taeo owns his mistake without becoming unusually wise.** He admits he wanted to see what would happen. He cannot explain the mechanism or promise a return. His apology does not erase Nib's grievance.
- **Nib has agency without becoming an exposition source.** He asks questions, requests to go back, challenges Taeo, refuses the offered seat, chooses his own position, and agrees only to wait. He provides no cosmology, perfect tactical deduction, or instant devotion.
- **The name discovery produces a believable misunderstanding.** Taeo uses the displayed name without realizing how threatening that knowledge could seem. The exchange establishes names and preserves the limited Chapter 1 result text without adding a character sheet.
- **The first trust act is small and observable.** Nib chooses where the water goes and asks Taeo to drink first. Taeo complies without crowding him. The spill and cleanup make their fear physical. On the refill, Nib no longer retreats; that is the entire gain.
- **Dialogue changes their next action.** Questions determine spacing, explanation, water placement, and whether Nib sits. Silence carries hesitation, the unfamiliar city, and the small shift in trust. No narrated emotions duplicate the acting.
- **The baseline remains meaningful.** Taeo's initial command came from his ordinary-summon expectations. Nib makes clear that understanding an order is different from consenting to it. This establishes the relationship through behavior, without inventing a control mechanic.
- **The endpoint is bounded.** “For a while” is temporary shelter, not a contract, loyalty pledge, or combat partnership. Neither agrees to enter a dungeon. There is no registration call or assessment hidden between panels.
- **No new power canon is asserted.** Return, dismissal, persistence, language, telepathy, resurrection, costs, and summon control remain unresolved. Taeo's inability to explain something is not proof of a system limitation.
- **Geography supports the emotion.** The open exit line, far window, two sides of the cleared floor, shared cup position, and final seated positions remain consistent. The water exchange is deliberately blocked in full rather than solved with camera cuts.
- **Panel count serves performance.** The 64 panels include brief beats that can share a production page or scroll section. If trimming is necessary, combine adjacent silent reactions; preserve the door negotiation, honest admission, unfamiliar-window pause, water test, apology without forgiveness, and change from command to invitation.
