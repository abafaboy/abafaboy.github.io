# My Summons Go First
## Chapter 1: One More Shift

**Scope:** One ordinary workday, a nearby gate incident, Taeo’s awakening, and his first summon. The chapter ends before Taeo and Nib have their first real conversation.

**Provisional choices:** Taeo works at a small neighborhood grocery store. His awakening happens during the gate incident, but this chapter does not establish that proximity to gates causes awakening. The interface wording is provisional. For staging, the gate is an opaque, vertical dark-blue aperture with a restrained, unstable edge. Its final visual design remains an art decision.

**Backstory:** Taeo is an employed young orphan. His younger sibling lives with their grandparents elsewhere. This informs his behavior; the chapter does not stop to explain his biography.

---

## Scene 1 — Before the rush

**Panel 1**

Early evening. A small neighborhood grocery store.

Through the window, Taeo stacks packets beside the register. His work vest is slightly too large.

A customer waits with a basket.

**CUSTOMER:** These were two for one.

**Panel 2**

Taeo looks from the two drinks to the promotion sticker.

The tiny date at its bottom ended yesterday.

**TAEO:** That ended yesterday.

The customer’s mouth tightens.

**Panel 3**

Before the customer answers, Taeo reaches toward a neighboring shelf.

**TAEO:** These still are. Same price.

**CUSTOMER:** Those are fine.

**Panel 4**

Taeo swaps the drinks, scans them, and slides the basket forward.

Behind the customer, a short, heavy stone porter stands directly in front of the automatic door. A stack of delivery crates fills its arms.

The door tries to close around a crate.

SFX: **DING. DING.**

**Panel 5**

The delivery worker pulls a receipt from his pocket without looking up.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** Back.

The porter takes precisely one step backward.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** Wait.

It becomes still.

**Panel 6**

Taeo approaches the crates.

**TAEO:** On the counter, please.

The porter does not move. Its blank face remains pointed straight ahead.

**Panel 7**

The delivery worker looks up.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** You have to tell me.

He points to the counter.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** There. Put down.

The porter obeys, dropping the crates the last few centimeters.

SFX: **THUNK.**

Taeo catches a packet before it tumbles off the top.

**Panel 8**

Taeo presses the escaped packet back into place.

**TAEO:** Can it do “gently”?

**DELIVERY WORKER:** Still working on that.

He signs the delivery sheet.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** Carry, follow, stop. That’s most of it.

**Panel 9**

The worker leaves. The porter follows silently, matching his steps.

Taeo watches them through the glass.

His coworker leans out from the back room.

**COWORKER:** Your turn to eat.

**Panel 10**

Taeo checks the wall clock.

Then the unfinished crates.

**COWORKER:** They’ll still be there.

**TAEO:** I know.

He puts down the next packet anyway.

## Scene 2 — Dinner, elsewhere

**Panel 11**

A cramped break area.

Taeo sits on an upturned crate with a heated meal balanced on his knees. His phone rests against a tissue box.

An incoming video call: his younger sibling.

He wipes his mouth before accepting.

**Panel 12**

On the phone, his sibling holds the camera much too close.

**SIBLING:** Are you eating?

**TAEO:** Are you?

**SIBLING:** I asked first.

Taeo tilts his meal toward the camera.

**Panel 13**

The sibling pulls the phone back. A dinner table appears behind them.

Their grandmother’s hand enters the frame and deposits another piece of meat into the sibling’s bowl.

The sibling looks sideways at the hand.

**SIBLING:** She’s doing it again.

**Panel 14**

For the first time, Taeo’s shoulders loosen.

**TAEO:** Grandma. I saw that.

**GRANDMOTHER, OFF PHONE:** Good. Tell this one to eat.

**SIBLING:** I am eating.

Their mouth is visibly full.

**Panel 15**

Taeo laughs quietly.

He takes a proper bite of his own dinner while the sibling begins describing something from school.

The sibling talks with both hands. The camera wobbles.

No dialogue needs to be legible here.

**Panel 16**

The sibling settles down.

**SIBLING:** Are you coming this Sunday?

Taeo glances at the staff schedule pinned beside the door.

His name occupies the Sunday column.

**Panel 17**

A closer view of Taeo. The smile remains, but smaller.

**TAEO:** I’ll ask about swapping.

**SIBLING:** You said that last time.

A beat.

**TAEO:** I know.

**Panel 18**

The sibling looks down, then quickly lifts their bowl.

**SIBLING:** Save me some of those seaweed snacks.

**TAEO:** The spicy ones?

**SIBLING:** Obviously.

**Panel 19**

The call ends.

Taeo remains looking at the dark screen for a moment.

Then he opens his banking app.

A familiar recipient: his grandmother.

He sends his usual support payment. The remaining balance is visibly modest, without requiring the reader to study exact figures.

**Panel 20**

He sets the phone down and eats the last few bites.

From the shop:

**COWORKER, OFF PANEL:** Taeo?

Taeo looks toward the door.

**COWORKER, OFF PANEL:** Come look at this.

## Scene 3 — The aisle

**Panel 21**

Taeo steps onto the shop floor.

His coworker stands near the register, looking toward the middle aisle.

A hanging promotional sign moves slightly.

Everything else is still.

**TAEO:** What?

**Panel 22**

Between two shelving units, a thin vertical dark line hangs in empty air.

A customer extends a hand toward it.

**TAEO:** Don’t touch that.

**Panel 23**

The line widens.

An opaque dark-blue aperture opens across part of the aisle. Nothing can be seen beyond its surface.

The customer jerks their hand back.

SFX: **VMMMM.**

**Panel 24**

Taeo stops.

His fingers tighten around the edge of his work vest.

The aperture is now taller than he is.

No explosion. No collapsing shop. The fluorescent lights continue humming.

**Panel 25**

His coworker grabs the store phone.

**COWORKER:** Everyone outside. Leave your baskets.

A customer near the register looks down at their shopping.

**CUSTOMER:** But I already—

**COWORKER:** Outside. Please.

**Panel 26**

Taeo remains still for one more beat.

An older customer stands farther down the shop aisle, still on the store side of the gate. The aperture blocks their direct route toward the register; the adjacent aisle remains clear.

They stare at the dark surface instead of moving.

**Panel 27**

Taeo looks at the gate.

Then at the clear route around the next shelving unit.

He makes himself move.

**TAEO:** This way.

His voice comes out too quietly.

**Panel 28**

He approaches along the clear aisle, keeping a shelving unit between himself and the gate.

Louder now:

**TAEO:** Sir. This way.

The customer finally turns.

**Panel 29**

The customer starts toward him, still carrying a basket.

Taeo takes the basket and puts it on a shelf.

**TAEO:** Leave it. We’ll keep it here.

**Panel 30**

At the end of the shelf, the customer glances back.

Taeo briefly touches their elbow and points toward the front door.

**TAEO:** Keep going.

His other hand is shaking.

**Panel 31**

The customer exits.

Taeo follows, then turns his head for one last check of the shop.

Empty aisles.

His half-finished delivery crates beside the register.

The gate occupies the place where an ordinary aisle should be.

**Panel 32**

Something appears directly in Taeo’s field of view.

**[AWAKENING COMPLETE]**

He flinches.

**Panel 33**

The words remain when he turns his head.

A second line appears.

**[CLASS: SUMMONER]**

**TAEO:** What—

**Panel 34**

His coworker takes his sleeve from outside the door.

**COWORKER:** Taeo!

Taeo stumbles onto the pavement.

**Panel 35**

Wide exterior.

People retreat from the storefront. One person is already filming. Another pulls them farther back.

Taeo and his coworker stop beside the neighboring building.

The coworker is speaking into the phone.

**COWORKER:** Inside the store. Everyone’s out.

**Panel 36**

Taeo leans forward, palms on his knees.

His breathing is too quick.

The floating text follows him into the composition.

**[FIRST SUMMON AVAILABLE]**

He closes his eyes.

**Panel 37**

Black for a narrow beat.

Then his eyes open.

The words are still there.

He looks at his coworker, who is occupied with the emergency operator.

Taeo says nothing.

## Scene 4 — Waiting outside

**Panel 38**

Later.

The street in front of the shop is cordoned off. Responders keep pedestrians moving. The storefront is intact.

Taeo sits on a low wall outside the cordon. His coworker hands him bottled water.

**COWORKER:** You okay?

**TAEO:** Yeah.

**Panel 39**

Taeo attempts to unscrew the cap.

His hand slips.

His coworker notices, takes the bottle, loosens the cap, and hands it back without comment.

**Panel 40**

Two hunters arrive at the cordon.

One listens to the responder’s briefing. The other checks a fastener on their equipment.

They behave like people arriving for dangerous work, not performers making an entrance.

Taeo watches.

**Panel 41**

A nearby onlooker addresses a responder.

**ONLOOKER:** How long until we can go back?

**RESPONDER:** After they clear the boss and the gate closes. We don’t have a time yet.

**Panel 42**

Through the storefront’s wide glass window, Taeo can see the cleared central aisle and the gate.

The hunters enter the store. One disappears into the opaque aperture.

The second follows.

Taeo’s fingers crease the water bottle.

**Panel 43**

His coworker glances at a message from the manager.

**COWORKER:** He says go home. He’ll deal with the store.

Taeo nods.

**COWORKER:** You sure you’re okay to get back?

**TAEO:** I can get home.

**Panel 44**

Taeo walks away from the cordon.

A few people remain with their phones raised toward the shop.

He does not look back.

The summon notification rests at the edge of his vision.

## Scene 5 — The room

**Panel 45**

Taeo unlocks his apartment.

A small room: narrow bed, folding table, kitchenette, drying laundry.

He steps inside and shuts the door.

**Panel 46**

He stands with his forehead against the closed door.

His keys hang loosely from his hand.

Silence.

**Panel 47**

His phone vibrates.

A message from his sibling:

**SIBLING, TEXT:** Grandma says thank you. She also says buy yourself actual food.

Below it, a badly framed photograph of their grandfather asleep in front of the television.

**Panel 48**

Taeo looks at the photograph.

He types:

**TAEO, TEXT:** Tell her I ate.

He considers adding something else.

Doesn’t.

**Panel 49**

He sits at his folding table.

On the wall nearby is an ordinary family photograph, kept carefully despite the cheap frame. It includes Taeo, his sibling, and their parents.

The picture is background, not an explanatory insert.

Taeo opens an official hunter-registration information page on his phone.

One brief section is readable:

**NEWLY AWAKENED?**

**Contact us to book an ability assessment.**

**Panel 50**

He opens the short summoner introduction on the same page.

**Most summons follow simple commands. They aren’t expected to speak.**

Taeo saves the assessment office’s contact, then sets down the phone. No booking or assessment has happened.

**TAEO:** I’ll call in the morning.

**Panel 51**

A memory-sized insert: the stone porter staring blankly ahead while Taeo asks it to put down the crates.

No repeated dialogue.

**Panel 52**

Back in the room.

Taeo looks at his own hands.

Then at the small amount of floor between the table and the bed.

**TAEO:** I’m not going into a dungeon.

A beat.

**TAEO:** I just want to see.

**Panel 53**

A wider view as Taeo folds the table against the wall and pushes the laundry rack back.

His phone and meal cup sit on the kitchenette counter. A small rectangle of floor is now clear.

He keeps checking the space while he works.

**Panel 54**

Over Taeo’s shoulder: he stands near the door, facing the cleared floor. The exit remains behind him.

He brings the notification into focus.

The interface opens:

**[AVAILABLE DRAWS: 1]**

**[DRAW]**

**Panel 55**

Close on his hand hovering before **DRAW**.

His eyes shift toward the phone on the counter, then back to the empty space.

He has not pressed it yet.

**Panel 56**

A small, quiet panel: his finger completes the selection.

**DRAW** dims beneath it.

**Panel 57**

A wider panel returns to the whole cramped room.

A point of light appears just above the floor and stretches into a low, uneven silhouette. Taeo steps back involuntarily, his shoulder nearly touching the door.

No long rarity ladder, catalogue, or rules explanation.

**Panel 58**

Low close-up.

Bare, narrow, callused feet touch the floor. Worn equipment hangs above wiry legs as the light fades.

One foot shifts to find balance.

**Panel 59**

An adult goblin stands in Taeo’s room.

Small and lean, with an angular face, old nicks along one ear, a worn belt pouch, and a short scouting blade.

This is Nib.

He is neither an adorable child nor an imposing warrior. He looks like someone accustomed to making do with very little.

A simple result label fades beside him:

**[NIB — GOBLIN SCOUT]**

**Panel 60**

Nib sways once as the last light disappears.

He catches his balance.

His eyes move to the ceiling light, the window, then Taeo standing near the door.

Taeo remains perfectly still.

**Panel 61**

Taeo raises one empty hand.

Awkwardly:

**TAEO:** Stay.

**Panel 62**

Nib’s eyes narrow.

He does not take the expected obedient stance.

One hand hovers near his belt, without drawing the blade.

**Panel 63**

Close on Nib.

His mouth moves clearly.

**NIB:** Where am I?

**Panel 64**

Close on Taeo.

His raised hand has gone slack.

No dialogue.

**END CHAPTER 1**

## Brief causal and pacing audit

- **The ordinary life has its own small actions:** Taeo handles a customer, eats during a shift, talks to family, struggles to arrange a visit, and sends support. These scenes establish responsibility without a biographical speech.
- **The baseline precedes the exception:** The porter is nonverbal, responds to its summoner’s basic commands, and cannot independently interpret Taeo’s request. A short official introduction confirms that simple commands and absent speech are the ordinary baseline in this fictional world, rather than merely traits of a stone creature. Nib’s understandable question then has immediate significance.
- **Taeo remains cautious:** During evacuation he uses a clear route rather than approaching or entering the gate. He freezes, acts when another person needs direction, and experiences the physical reaction afterward.
- **The gate does not cause arbitrary destruction:** It obstructs an aisle. People evacuate; responders establish a cordon; hunters enter. The boss-and-closure rule appears in response to an ordinary practical question.
- **Awakening and accepting his power are separate beats:** The notification interrupts the incident. Taeo gets home, checks registration information, considers the power, clears space, hesitates, and chooses one limited experiment.
- **The chapter spends its ending on arrival:** Nib receives no instant loyalty, genius demonstration, personality monologue, or command test. Chapter 2 owns the meeting.
- **Nothing is completed offscreen to accelerate the plot:** Registration, testing, preparation, and the first hunt remain future events. Taeo reads the assessment instructions and saves a contact to call in the morning. He does not book, attend, or complete an assessment.
- **The panel count is adjustable:** The 64 panels represent acting and transitions, not a target quota. Compress adjacent physical actions if needed; preserve the family conversation, evacuation hesitation, post-incident reaction, and decision before the draw.
