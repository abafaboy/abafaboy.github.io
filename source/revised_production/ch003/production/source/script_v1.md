# My Summons Go First
## Chapter 3: Both of Us

**Status:** Script draft for review. No illustration or publication authorized by this document. Chapters 1 and 2 remain unchanged.

**Scope:** The end of the first evening, the following morning, and arrival at a booked ability assessment. Taeo follows through on his promise to seek help. Nib comes to ask his own question. The initial measurement establishes low output, and Nib's independent responses lead to a joint assessment. Neither registration nor their larger problem is resolved.

**Immediate goal:** Get someone qualified to examine what happened to Nib.

**Visual continuity:** Nib is a wiry, thigh-height adult with angular features, weathered hands, old ear nicks, a worn belt pouch, and a short sheathed scouting blade. Never use child proportions or a mascot performance. Taeo remains visibly young, tired, and careful. Preserve the established apartment geography. A clear evening-to-morning transition is required. No magical UI is needed in this chapter; phone text and ordinary signs are the only readable graphics.

## Scene 1 — Someone to ask

**Panel 1**

Continue from Chapter 2's final position, without repeating the water exchange. The filled cup now rests beside Nib. Taeo picks up the phone from the kitchenette.

The message that interrupted them earlier is from his coworker:

**COWORKER, TEXT:** The hunters are out. Nobody hurt. I'll let you know about tomorrow.

Taeo lets out a breath he had been holding.

**Panel 2**

He replies while Nib watches from beside the bed.

**TAEO, TEXT:** Home now. Thanks. Let me know when you get back.

**NIB:** What is that?

**TAEO:** A phone. I can talk to people with it.

**Panel 3**

Nib looks toward the window, then back at the object.

**NIB:** People who aren't here?

**TAEO:** Yes.

**NIB:** Then ask someone.

**Panel 4**

Taeo opens the assessment contact he saved in Chapter 1.

**TAEO:** There's an office for people who wake up with abilities. I found it before you arrived.

**TAEO:** I'll call when they open.

No new app interface is necessary beyond an ordinary saved contact.

**Panel 5**

Nib sits a little straighter.

**NIB:** They know how to send me back?

**TAEO:** I don't know. They know more than I do.

**Panel 6**

Nib considers that inadequate but usable answer.

**NIB:** Wake me when you call.

**TAEO:** All right.

This is a plan for tomorrow, not renewed permission to remain or an agreement to work together.

**Panel 7**

A little later. Taeo takes a spare folded blanket from beneath the bed and lays it on the mattress, within Nib's reach. He then moves back to the kitchenette to open a packet of crackers.

**TAEO:** There's this, if you're hungry.

Nib examines the blanket's loose edge. He does not answer immediately.

**Panel 8**

Time has passed. The packet sits open between their separate places. A cracker is in Nib's hand; Taeo eats one against the cabinet. No second poisoning test or feeding performance.

Nib has pulled the blanket onto the floor and folded it beneath himself.

**NIB:** You sleep up there?

He indicates the narrow bed.

**TAEO:** Usually.

**Panel 9**

Nib looks at the bed, then at Taeo's long legs folded awkwardly on the floor.

**NIB:** Then go on.

Taeo looks at him, surprised by the practical instruction.

**Panel 10**

Night. The light is off. Streetlight outlines the closed curtain.

Taeo lies on the bed, fully clothed. Nib sits on the folded blanket near its foot, back against the frame and blade sheathed at his side. The path to the entrance stays open.

Neither is asleep yet.

No dialogue.

## Scene 2 — The call

**Panel 11**

Morning, clearly marked by daylight and an ordinary phone alarm.

Taeo reaches down from the bed and stops the alarm. Before he can sit up, Nib is already looking at him.

**NIB:** Are they open?

**Panel 12**

Taeo sits at the edge of the bed with the phone. He turns on the speaker and sets it beside him where both can hear.

**TAEO:** I think so.

He calls the saved assessment number. Let the ringing occupy one small beat.

**Panel 13**

**STAFF, PHONE:** Ability assessment office.

**TAEO:** I awakened yesterday. The class said summoner. I'd like an assessment.

Nib gets to his feet.

**Panel 14**

**STAFF, PHONE:** Have you summoned anything yet?

**TAEO:** Yes. He's here.

A beat. Taeo looks at Nib.

**TAEO:** He talks. He wants to go back.

**Panel 15**

Nib steps closer to the phone, without touching it.

**NIB:** Can you do that?

Taeo's mouth closes around the explanation he was about to add.

**Panel 16**

**STAFF, PHONE:** Was that the summon?

**TAEO:** Yes.

A brief pause while the staff member considers what to do, rather than a shocked reaction montage.

**Panel 17**

**STAFF, PHONE:** I'll note that for the assessor. We need to see you in person.

**STAFF, PHONE:** Can you come at eleven? Bring your identification, and bring him with you.

**TAEO:** Eleven is fine.

**Panel 18**

**STAFF, PHONE:** I'll send the address. Don't try anything new before the appointment.

**TAEO:** Okay. Thank you.

The call ends. Nib is still looking at the phone.

**NIB:** They didn't answer.

**TAEO:** No.

**Panel 19**

Taeo opens the address message, then looks up.

**TAEO:** It's a walk from here.

**NIB:** How far?

**TAEO:** About twenty minutes.

**NIB:** Show me.

No route tutorial, commuting montage, or guarantee that the office can reverse the summoning.

## Scene 3 — Outside

**Panel 20**

Later that morning. Taeo puts his identification and phone into his jacket pockets. His work vest remains hung in the room.

A message from the coworker is briefly readable:

**COWORKER, TEXT:** Manager says come in at four.

Taeo types **Okay**. Work still exists after this appointment.

**Panel 21**

At the entrance, Taeo puts on his shoes. Nib stands beside him, travel gear already on, bare feet planted on the floor.

Taeo's gaze catches on the scouting blade.

**TAEO:** Keep that in its sheath outside.

**NIB:** It is in its sheath.

**Panel 22**

Taeo glances at the blade again. It has been sheathed throughout their meeting.

**TAEO:** Right.

He opens the door.

Nib goes through first, after checking the hallway himself.

**Panel 23**

The stairwell. Taeo holds the stairwell door while Nib descends ahead of him, one hand near the wall.

Their height difference reads naturally. Nib navigates ordinary steps without being carried or turned into a child on an obstacle course.

**Panel 24**

At street level, Nib steps through the building's open entrance.

A broad view from just behind his shoulder: moving cars, tall facades, people passing, overhead wires. The city has been there all night; now it is close and loud.

Taeo is one pace behind him.

**Panel 25**

A delivery scooter passes on the road with a sudden engine whine.

Nib turns quickly toward it. A pedestrian walks across his nearer line of sight at the same time.

He backs into the recessed entrance, making room rather than drawing his weapon.

**Panel 26**

Taeo stops beside the recess, keeping out of the way of the next pedestrian.

**TAEO:** We can wait a minute.

Nib looks up at the unfamiliar buildings. There is no place here that he recognizes as safer than the place he just left.

**Panel 27**

A worker crosses the far side of the street. A long-limbed wooden porter follows with two strapped cases.

The worker stops at a crossing. The porter stops behind them, silently.

Nib watches. No fresh lecture about ordinary summons.

**Panel 28**

**NIB:** They're not looking at it.

**TAEO:** People see summons here.

A passerby glances at Nib and continues walking. Acknowledgment, not universal indifference or a gathering crowd.

**Panel 29**

Nib looks at the path Taeo is facing.

**NIB:** Which way?

Taeo points along the pavement.

**TAEO:** This way.

Nib steps out of the recess.

**Panel 30**

They walk. Taeo starts at his usual pace. After two strides he notices Nib falling behind and shortens his steps.

Nib notices the adjustment but says nothing.

Their movement carries the scene out. Do not add a second reassurance conversation.

## Scene 4 — People who come back

**Panel 31**

Exterior: a modest regional assessment building. Ordinary people enter; no palace, armed welcome party, or elite waiting for Taeo.

Taeo checks the address once, then opens the door. Nib follows him inside.

**Panel 32**

Reception, handled in one panel.

Taeo presents his identification.

**TAEO:** Eleven o'clock. I'm Taeo. This is Nib.

The receptionist checks the booking and adds Nib's name to its note, then indicates a nearby waiting area.

**RECEPTIONIST:** Take a seat. The assessor will call you.

**Panel 33**

Taeo sits at the end of a bench. Nib chooses the open space beside it, where he can see both doors.

A television on the opposite wall is showing a news interview. Taeo's attention goes to it immediately.

**Panel 34**

On the television: Han Seoryun in used field clothing, standing beside a rescue vehicle. Another crew member moves equipment behind her.

One simple lower-third:

**HAN SEORYUN · S-RANK HUNTER**

**REPORTER, TV:** Will your team go back in today?

**SEORYUN, TV:** The next team is already there. Mine are going home to sleep.

No new dungeon rules or current disaster subplot need to be explained.

**Panel 35**

Taeo watches with a small, unguarded smile. His posture has changed before he realizes it.

Nib follows his gaze.

**NIB:** You know her?

**TAEO:** No. I've watched her for years.

**Panel 36**

On the screen, Seoryun steps aside as a tired teammate passes her a drink. She takes it while listening to another question. Do not make her pose for admiration.

**TAEO:** There was a rescue on the news when I was younger. She kept going back for people.

**Panel 37**

Closer on Taeo. He speaks to Nib, not to the television now.

**TAEO:** They showed the families waiting outside. Then people started coming out.

A short quiet beat.

**TAEO:** I liked that part.

No flashback to his parents' death, no claim that Seoryun has never lost anyone, and no heroic slogan.

**Panel 38**

Nib studies Taeo's face, then looks once more at the screen.

The interview has continued without them. A crew member is putting equipment into the vehicle.

No dialogue. Nib has learned something about Taeo that was not an apology or a promise.

**Panel 39**

A woman in ordinary assessment-office clothes opens the inner door with a folder in her hand.

**ASSESSOR:** Taeo?

Taeo stands too quickly, then checks that Nib is beside him.

**TAEO:** Yes.

## Scene 5 — The person who was there

**Panel 40**

Inside a plain assessment room: desk, visitor chairs, a marked patch of open floor, and equipment set back against the wall. Keep unfamiliar equipment background-only; it is not a promise of a fight.

The assessor sets down the folder.

**ASSESSOR:** I'm Chae. I'll be doing your assessment.

**CHAE:** The call note says your summon spoke.

**Panel 41**

Taeo sits. Nib remains standing beside the other visitor chair, his head below the desk's upper edge.

**TAEO:** Yes. Straight away.

Chae looks toward Nib, locating him properly rather than continuing to address empty air above him.

**CHAE:** Nib?

**Panel 42**

**NIB:** That's my name.

Chae pauses with her pen just above the paper.

One contained reaction. The phone report was real. No alarm, higher-rank reveal, or instant diagnosis follows.

**Panel 43**

Chae moves her chair out from behind the desk. Now she can see Nib without making him climb onto furniture.

**CHAE:** Have a seat wherever you're comfortable.

Nib stays standing.

She continues without repeating the invitation.

**Panel 44**

**CHAE:** Tell me what happened yesterday.

Taeo puts his hands together to stop fidgeting.

**TAEO:** A gate opened at work. After we got outside, I saw the class message.

**Panel 45**

A small close view of his fingers tightening.

**TAEO:** At home there was one draw. I used it, and he appeared.

**CHAE:** Anything after that? Another message?

**TAEO:** His name. Goblin scout. Nothing else that I saw.

No repeated awakening or summon flashback is necessary.

**Panel 46**

Chae notes the limited account.

**TAEO:** I couldn't bring it back up. I tried last night.

**CHAE:** We'll look at that together. Don't force it now.

The ordinary appointment has begun. She has not established a power limit or pronounced the interface broken.

**Panel 47**

Chae slides a palm-sized assessment sensor across the desk.

**CHAE:** Put your hand flat on this. Keep it there until I say.

Taeo follows the instruction. Nib moves to a position where he can see the hand and device clearly.

**Panel 48**

The instrument settles to a steady indication near the low end of its physical scale.

Do not invent a numeric stat, rarity label, diagnostic tree, or floating system window. This is an office instrument taking one measurement, not Taeo discovering how to activate a skill.

**Panel 49**

Chae checks the reading, then indicates that Taeo can remove his hand.

**CHAE:** Your output is low.

Taeo looks at his hand as he withdraws it.

**TAEO:** What does that mean?

**Panel 50**

**CHAE:** That's one measurement. I'm not assigning your rank yet.

She looks at Nib.

**CHAE:** His responses don't match what I'd expect from an ordinary summon. I need to assess you together.

**Panel 51**

Nib looks from the instrument to Chae.

**NIB:** Does that tell you where I came from?

**CHAE:** No. It only measures his output.

The small device has supplied a real result and has a clear limit. It cannot answer their main question.

**Panel 52**

Nib steps into the clearer space beside the desk.

**NIB:** Can you send me back?

Chae puts the pen down.

**CHAE:** I don't know yet. Where would you be going?

**Panel 53**

Taeo starts to help, too quickly.

**TAEO:** I think he came from a dungeon. He didn't recognize—

**NIB:** You didn't ask me.

Taeo stops.

**Panel 54**

Taeo looks at Nib.

He remembers the question he almost asked at the window and never finished. No replay panel needed.

**TAEO:** No. I didn't.

The admission is small and embarrassed. Do not expand it into another apology speech.

**Panel 55**

Chae turns fully toward Nib.

**CHAE:** Then let's start with you. What were you doing before you arrived here?

The question is ordinary. The change in who gets to answer is the turn.

**Panel 56**

Nib glances at Taeo, making sure he is going to let the answer stand.

Taeo leans back instead of filling the silence.

**NIB:** Keeping watch.

**Panel 57**

**CHAE:** Alone?

**NIB:** Yes. Someone was coming to take over.

Nib's hand closes around the familiar knot on his belt pouch.

**Panel 58**

The two humans listen.

**NIB:** He'd find my place empty.

A beat.

**NIB:** He'd go looking.

No image of the other person, fantasy landscape, secret crest, or looming enemy. We have Nib's account and its immediate personal consequence.

**Panel 59**

Taeo looks down at his phone in his lap. The coworker thread is still open from checking his shift. His earlier reply, **Home now**, is visible just above the latest message.

A quiet visual contrast. He was able to say he got home. Nib cannot.

Do not add a thought caption explaining the contrast.

**Panel 60**

Taeo locks the phone and puts it away.

**TAEO:** What do you need us to do?

The question is directed to Chae. He is no longer asking only what his new class is.

**Panel 61**

Chae sets the folder aside and moves to the marked floor. She shifts a visitor chair clear of the space as she goes.

**CHAE:** First, I'll check whether I can detect a connection between you. It may help us understand how he got here.

**CHAE:** Taeo, that mark. Nib, over here.

She indicates two positions a few paces apart. They can still see and speak to each other.

**Panel 62**

Final tall panel: Taeo stands on his mark. Nib walks to the other one and turns to face him. Chae stands to the side, able to watch both.

For the first time, the two of them are deliberately preparing to do something together.

Nib meets Taeo's eyes. Taeo straightens a little.

No dialogue. The examination has changed course; the next step is a controlled test in this room, not a dungeon.

**END CHAPTER 3**

## Causal and pacing audit

- **Goal:** Taeo seeks qualified help for Nib. The promise is translated into a completed call, a specific appointment, a journey, and the beginning of an actual examination. The chapter does not merely promise that registration will happen later.
- **Obstacle:** The phone contact cannot explain an unprecedented report remotely. Nib must enter an unfamiliar city to be heard. At the office, Taeo's own unexamined assumption briefly gets in the way of Nib's account.
- **Decision:** Nib asks to hear the call, addresses the staff himself, and chooses to leave the entrance recess and continue to the appointment. Taeo accepts that he cannot explain Nib's origin for him and asks what both of them can do next.
- **Emotional change:** Chapter 2's temporary tolerance becomes one shared action with separate motives. Taeo wants an explanation and to correct his mistake; Nib wants to return to an existing life. Nib sees a sincere piece of Taeo through his admiration for a rescue hunter. Neither has pledged loyalty or agreed to dangerous work.
- **Concrete assessment result:** Taeo's first measured output is low. Chae explicitly withholds a final rank because the assessment is incomplete. Nib's independent behavior warrants a separate investigation; the script establishes no causal link between output and intelligence. The consequence is visible: she sets aside the individual reading and moves both of them to a controlled floor test. No new power is activated or awarded.
- **Concrete new information:** Nib had a watch duty and expected a relief before being transported. Someone may be searching for him. This is Nib's recollection and expectation, not omniscient confirmation of the other person's actions. His origin remains unknown.
- **Forward pull:** The initial output measurement has a definite result. Nib's independent responses require a separate investigation, so the examiner moves them to a joint test to look for a detectable connection. Chapter 4 owes the reader that test and useful consequences, not another appointment or interview-preparation chapter. Nib's missing relief supplies a personal reason to keep seeking an answer.
- **Practical continuity:** The interrupted phone message is read; the saved contact is used; a night visibly passes; bedding and a little food are handled economically; the ordinary job supplies a four-o'clock obligation. The continued employment is not quietly replaced by a hunter career. Family contact is not repeated solely to restate Chapter 1.
- **Public baseline:** A silent porter makes the city intelligible to Nib. Passing humans can notice him without treating all humanoid summons as a public emergency. This is local observed behavior, not a legal declaration that every creature may go anywhere with any weapon.
- **Competent staff:** The caller reports speech; the receptionist records it; the assessor is prepared to check it and adjusts when she hears it. There is no citywide shock, hostile bureaucracy, or instantaneous diagnosis. The office neither guarantees return nor secretly confiscates Nib.
- **No accelerated progression:** No assigned rank, numerical stats, mana total, activated skill, dismissal function, return button, telepathy, hunt, guild offer, second draw, or new system rule. The promised UI art style is not used simply to decorate an episode that does not require UI.
- **Pacing:** The largest change is not the trip itself; it is the shift from examining Taeo's power to hearing Nib's own account. Reception is one panel. No form-filling, queue dispute, route tutorial, feeding sequence, or administrative side quest. The 62 panels are acting units, not a quota. Combine small transitions rather than stretching them into extra production pages.

## Comparative structure note — evidence and limits

The parent supplied a firsthand visual inspection of the official *My S-Class Hunters*, Episode 8, “Hunters Association”: https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/my-s-class-hunters/ep-8-hunters-association/viewer?episode_no=8&title_no=3963 . This draft's writer did not independently inspect that episode. The supplied observation describes sibling negotiation; association expectations changed by a brief registration result; then shopping, pursuit, and rescue. It does not establish a detailed assessment procedure or a universal chapter formula.

The transferred craft principle is that an institution scene should change a relationship or expectation instead of spending its length on procedure. Here, a brief measurement establishes low output without completing the assessment. Nib's independent responses lead the assessor to a separate joint test; low output is not asserted to cause or contradict intelligence. Nib also becomes the witness to his own prior life, and Taeo realizes someone may be waiting for him. No borrowed rank reversal, celebrity escort, shopping scene, chase, rescue, character, dialogue, or layout is used. The public hunter scene also follows the existing architecture's instruction to introduce an established career through ordinary media rather than an exposition parade.
