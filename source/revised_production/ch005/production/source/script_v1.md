# My Summons Go First
## Chapter 5: The Way Around

**Status:** Independently reviewed script, ready for parent production review. No art or publication authorized by this document. Chapters 1–4 are unchanged.

**Scope:** One stretch of Taeo's grocery shift. Nib turns passive viewing into a search he can organize himself. A minor delivery mishap lets the two solve an ordinary physical problem together, without an activated skill or a conveniently dangerous new gate. Taeo finally asks about the family visit he has been putting off.

**Immediate wants:** Nib wants to stop losing his place among unfamiliar landscapes. Taeo wants to finish the delivery and get a moment to ask his coworker about Sunday.

**Emotional turn:** Each has seen the other frightened and dependent. Here each gets to be useful at something ordinary. Neither becomes the other's employee or best friend.

**Visual direction:** Grounded vertical-scroll manhwa; continuous spatial actions and readable, natural speech. Nib is a lean adult goblin, head approximately at Taeo's thigh, with angular features, ear nicks, callused hands, worn pouch and a sheathed scouting blade. The delivery sequence is mildly stressful and then satisfying, not a disaster rescue. No aura, stat screen, magical tracking, strength boost or comedy baby proportions. Panel numbers are acting units, not mandatory page divisions.

**Time and geography:** Same afternoon as Chapter 4, during the established four-to-ten shift. Start in the break area with the phone against the tissue box on the low crate and Nib's original sketch beside it. The break doorway stays clear. Use the Chapter 1 storefront, counter and delivery route. For the delivery sequence, establish a low wheeled platform trolley just inside the entrance, away from customers. It has a flat deck and two low under-deck crossbars; it is not a tall cage trolley. No new hidden room or wall opening is needed.

## Scene 1 — Seen this one

**Panel 1**

Close on the phone where Chapter 4 left it. A survey camera walks past a forked pale boulder. The picture bobs with the operator's steps.

Nib pauses the recording with the one action Taeo showed him. His sketch lies beside the phone.

No dialogue. No matching tree or wall from Nib's home.

**Panel 2**

Nib looks from the frozen boulder to two little marks he has made with a fingertip in the dust on the crate's edge. They are not enough to keep track of anything.

He wipes them away with his thumb, dissatisfied.

**Panel 3**

Through the doorway, Taeo is finishing a customer transaction. Nib waits inside the break area instead of calling across the customer's question.

**CUSTOMER:** A bag, please.

**TAEO:** Small one okay?

**Panel 4**

The customer leaves. Nib comes to the threshold with the paused phone held in both hands.

**NIB:** I need something to draw on.

**TAEO:** More paper?

Nib nods.

**Panel 5**

Taeo takes a discarded clean cardboard divider from an open stock carton and a short shop pencil from beside the counter. He offers them across the counter's low side, where Nib can reach them.

**TAEO:** Will this do?

**NIB:** Yes.

Nib keeps hold of the phone until Taeo sets the materials on a low empty crate beside him. No three-handed prop exchange.

**Panel 6**

Back in the break area, the phone is safely propped again. Nib draws the forked boulder and a line curving around it on the cardboard. He puts one short cross at the line's end.

His original watch-post sketch stays separate and clean.

**Panel 7**

A compact passage of time: Taeo stacks cans on a low shelf; Nib watches a different part of the recording. Light at the shopfront has shifted slightly. No implication that six hours have passed.

The same forked boulder enters the picture from the opposite side.

Nib leans closer.

**Panel 8**

Nib stops the video. On his cardboard, he draws the second approach joining the first line, then circles the boulder once.

One place, seen twice. He has learned this from visible landmarks, not from reading unfamiliar labels or sensing the recording.

**NIB:** You came back around.

He says it to the absent camera operator, quietly annoyed.

## Scene 2 — Put it down

**Panel 9**

The delivery worker from Chapter 1 has rolled a low platform trolley into the delivery space by the counter. He braces one wheel with his shoe and points. His silent stone porter lowers two plastic crates, with a taped cardboard carton on top, onto the deck.

Taeo puts down his stocking carton and approaches.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** There. Put down.

**TAEO:** Thanks. I'll take them back.

The doorway remains clear. Treat arrival and set-down as one compact composition, not a new entrance sequence.

**Panel 10**

Nib comes out with his cardboard map, wanting to show Taeo the repeated route. He stops beside the delivery space as Taeo lifts the top carton by its side handles.

From below, Nib sees its bottom seam beginning to open. Show the failing tape and the small bagged rice portions inside.

**NIB:** The bottom's coming out.

The worker glances sharply at Nib's voice, then follows his gaze to the carton. One contained reaction, overtaken by the immediate practical problem.

**Panel 11**

Taeo lowers the carton back onto the stack; the worker steadies its side. Two rice bags slip through the seam before it settles. One lands on the trolley deck. The other slides over the side and onto the floor beneath it.

SFX: **THUP. THUP.**

Most of the load remains in the now-supported carton. Nothing strikes Nib.

**TAEO:** I've got a tray.

**Panel 12**

The worker holds the sagging cardboard inward while Taeo brings an empty stock tray from beside the counter. Together they slide the contents into it without lifting the broken bottom free. The porter stands still.

Nib puts his map and pencil on the nearby low crate, clear of their feet.

No dialogue is needed. The failed carton is handled competently; Nib supplied an earlier viewing angle.

**Panel 13**

Low side view under the trolley. The fallen bag rests between its two low crossbars. Nib has crouched at one short end; he reaches, but the nearer bar stops his forearm. He withdraws without crawling underneath.

Taeo kneels at the opposite end. Both can see the bag. The worker keeps the trolley braced.

**TAEO:** I'll move it once we've unloaded.

Keep the bars, bag and clear side edge visible together. The bag entered from that side, not through a solid brace.

**Panel 14**

Nib takes a long, flat packing strip from the discarded carton and folds it lengthwise to stiffen it. He slides its thin end beneath the near crossbar; his hand stays outside the deck.

Rather than aiming at Taeo across the second bar, he angles the strip toward the open side edge.

**NIB:** That side.

Taeo shifts his empty hand to the indicated side, clear of the wheel.

**Panel 15**

Floor-level view along the trolley's open side. Nib pushes the bag diagonally into Taeo's reach, between the ends of the two crossbars. Taeo retrieves it.

**TAEO:** Got it.

No overhead cutaway or transparent deck. The reader sees the actual opening and the bag emerging through it. This is one short retrieval, with no extra failed attempt.

**Panel 16**

The seals are intact, but the wrappers picked up dust. Taeo and the worker place both fallen bags in a small returns tray beside the counter, separate from the clean stock.

Nib sets the packing strip with the discarded carton and straightens.

No dialogue. Keep this a small transition rather than a food-handling procedure.

**Panel 17**

The delivery worker looks directly at Nib.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** Thanks for spotting that.

**NIB:** I could see the bottom.

Plain and matter-of-fact. His lower viewpoint helped; he is neither fishing for praise nor performing a joke about his height.

**Panel 18**

The worker gives the torn seam a rueful look, then turns to Taeo.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** Check the others before you lift.

**TAEO:** I will.

The worker gestures to the porter.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** Follow.

The porter leaves silently after its owner. No reward, recruitment pitch, or new explanation of summons.

## Scene 3 — A different angle

**Panel 19**

Nib picks up his cardboard map and pencil. Taeo is still kneeling beside the counter, now at a comfortable conversational height.

**TAEO:** Was that what you came out for?

Nib looks at the drawing in his hands.

**NIB:** No.

He turns it toward Taeo.

**Panel 20**

Close on the crude route loop, forked boulder and crosses. It is a record of footage, not a complete survey map.

**NIB:** I kept thinking it was somewhere new. They went around the same rock.

**TAEO:** You remembered it?

**NIB:** The split. And the white bit here.

He points to two ordinary details, also visible in the earlier phone images.

**Panel 21**

Taeo looks at the map, then toward the break room.

**TAEO:** I can show you how to go back to a bit you've seen.

Nib starts toward the room at once.

**NIB:** Good.

**Panel 22**

Before Taeo follows, his coworker arrives with the shelf-label roll, glances at the sorted delivery, and takes the counter position.

**COWORKER:** Take your break. I'll finish these.

Taeo starts to lift the next crate anyway, then stops himself.

**TAEO:** Thanks.

The delivery problem is finished. Do not turn Nib into an unpaid stock assistant.

**Panel 23**

In the break area. Taeo sits on the floor beside the low crate. Nib stands on its other side. The phone and two separate drawings remain within their reach.

Taeo taps once to expose the playback bar, then drags its small marker back. The same forked boulder returns to the screen.

**TAEO:** This moves through the recording. Left goes back.

**Panel 24**

Nib tries. He drags much too far, returning to a dark close view of the camera operator's glove at the beginning of the recording.

He takes his finger away.

**NIB:** Too far.

**TAEO:** A little at a time.

Taeo keeps his hands in his own lap rather than taking over.

**Panel 25**

Nib moves the marker a shorter distance. Now the camera approaches the boulder for the first time.

He pauses and places the phone next to his loop drawing. His mouth lifts briefly at one corner.

**NIB:** Here.

A small, privately satisfying result: he can revisit a view on purpose. Taeo does not turn it into a lesson reward.

**Panel 26**

The boulder fills the phone image. Nib puts a fresh cross on the route beside it, then looks at his separate watch-post sketch.

**TAEO:** Anything familiar?

**NIB:** No.

No false match or secret symbol waits in the corner of the frame.

**Panel 27**

Nib turns the cardboard over. A few usable blank spaces remain.

**NIB:** How many more are there?

Taeo opens the supplied list briefly, checks it, and returns to the current recording.

**TAEO:** Six in this link. This is the first.

**NIB:** Put that one on next.

He points to the next thumbnail he saw. He does not suddenly read its title or navigate the whole site.

**Panel 28**

Taeo queues the next item but leaves the current recording paused. Nib picks up the pencil to finish his route marks first.

**TAEO:** You don't have to get through all of them tonight.

**NIB:** I want to.

Taeo looks at the watch-post drawing and leaves the phone where it is.

No argument, vow, or suggestion that this finite sample covers all gates.

## Scene 4 — Ask now

**Panel 29**

A later beat in the same break. Taeo eats an ordinary packed meal from his bag. Nib has a small piece of bread on its wrapper beside him, already eating while reviewing his marks. Food has been offered during the short transition, with no new negotiation or biological rule.

Taeo looks up at the staff schedule. The Sunday column catches his attention.

**Panel 30**

The coworker steps into the open doorway to collect the shop pencil when it is free. Nib passes it back, having finished his marks on this side.

**COWORKER:** I'll put another one in here.

**NIB:** A longer one.

The pencil is worn almost to the eraser. The coworker looks at it and snorts softly.

**COWORKER:** Fair.

**Panel 31**

Taeo speaks before the coworker turns away.

**TAEO:** Are you working Saturday?

**COWORKER:** Morning. Why?

**TAEO:** Could we swap? Your Saturday for my Sunday?

The schedule stays in the background; do not turn it into a full-screen roster.

**Panel 32**

The coworker thinks, checking their own phone briefly.

**COWORKER:** This Sunday? I can. You'll have to ask the manager.

**TAEO:** I'll ask him today.

**COWORKER:** Before he goes. He's leaving at six.

**Panel 33**

Taeo checks the wall clock. It is twenty to six.

He closes his meal lid and gets up.

**TAEO:** Right. Now.

The coworker steps aside to let him pass. The joke is that asking took more effort than the practical answer, not that Taeo's family need is trivial.

**Panel 34**

One compact exchange at the stockroom's open doorway, with the existing manager present but not given a grand character introduction. Taeo stays on the shop side.

**TAEO:** Can we swap my Sunday for Saturday morning? We both agreed.

**MANAGER:** Fine. Change it on the schedule before you forget.

No employment contract, application, second approval scene or offscreen promotion.

**Panel 35**

Back in the break area. Taeo corrects the two shifts on the ordinary paper schedule using the replacement full-length pencil the coworker has just brought. The coworker checks the change, nods and heads back to the shop.

Nib glances up from his route drawing.

**NIB:** What changed?

**TAEO:** I can see my family on Sunday.

**Panel 36**

Taeo picks up his phone, first checking Nib.

**TAEO:** Just a message.

Nib takes his hand off the paused screen. Taeo opens the sibling conversation.

**TAEO, TEXT:** Got Sunday off. I'll come over.

No instant reply needed. This is a concrete arrangement Taeo can make, not a promise that every future conflict is solved.

**Panel 37**

Taeo restores the paused survey and sets the phone back against the tissue box. He leaves the longer pencil beside Nib's cardboard.

**TAEO:** Sorry. Where were you?

Nib points to the turn just before the forked boulder on his drawing.

**NIB:** Here.

He moves the playback marker himself. The corresponding view appears.

**Panel 38**

Final tall composition, framed closer to the drawings than Chapter 4's doorway ending. Taeo steps back out to finish his shift. In the foreground, Nib adds the next turn to his own rough route map, with the watch-post sketch still beside it. He can now keep track of where the recording has already taken him.

At the upper edge of the composition, the corrected Sunday square remains visible on the wall. Do not enlarge it into a sentimental insert or require the reader to read the whole roster.

Two modest things have actually changed. Neither person has found a way home for Nib.

No dialogue. No interface, phantom hand pressure, new notification from Chae, threatening silhouette or cliffhanger override.

## Editorial intent and craft basis

The chapter intentionally spends its small progression on practical competence rather than another controlled power test. The broken carton changes physical activity quickly; Nib notices something from his actual viewpoint, then uses a cheap object and a different angle. Taeo and the delivery worker act sensibly. Nib's independent search remains his own reason to be in the shop, and the playback lesson answers an obstacle he identified himself.

Taeo's family action closes an opening-chapter promise instead of adding another abstract plan. It also supplies a future obligation that coexists with the summoning problem. The warmth is behavioral: Taeo leaves his hands out of Nib's phone lesson, Nib gets his own usable pencil, and Taeo sends his family message during his break. There is no loyalty speech or reward for unpaid labor.
