Font software is intentionally NOT included in this archive. The reader PDF contains flattened page images and must not embed editable font software. Supply your own properly licensed local font when rebuilding with ANIME_ACE_FONT. Additional permissions may be required for font embedding, redistribution, or webfont use.
The tablet heading, assessment finding and phone message are part of the native image-generated artwork; no separate vector UI or font program is supplied. The exact U+2014 em dash in panel54 uses the prior renderer’s DejaVu Sans per-glyph raster fallback because Anime Ace2 BB lacks that glyph.
