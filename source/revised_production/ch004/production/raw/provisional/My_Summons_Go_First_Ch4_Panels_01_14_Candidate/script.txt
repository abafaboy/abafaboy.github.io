# My Summons Go First
## Chapter 4: Something There

**Status:** Working script. No illustration or publication authorized by this document. Chapters 1–3 are unchanged.

**Scope:** The promised joint examination produces one small, repeatable result. It does not send Nib home. The appointment ends, and Taeo returns to the shop for his afternoon shift with someone he cannot simply put away.

**Immediate want:** Find out whether the connection that brought Nib here can help him get back.

**Production direction:** Grounded vertical-scroll manhwa. Panel numbers are acting units, not a quota or one-panel-per-page instruction. The controlled exercise should feel curious and increasingly personal, not like a laboratory report. Its payoff is modest but unmistakable. Preserve Nib as a wiry adult whose head reaches approximately Taeo's thigh, with angular features, worn gear, callused hands and old ear nicks. His blade remains sheathed. No childish proportions, enormous eyes, obedience pose, visible magical leash, shared eyesight, or spectacular aura.

**Starting geography:** Continue the final panel of Chapter 3. Taeo and Nib stand on separate marks a few paces apart, facing each other. Chae stands to one side. The desk and visitor chairs remain outside the marked space. The entrance is behind Chae. Two handheld contact sensors come from the equipment already established against the wall. These are separate from the palm-sized desk instrument used for the initial output reading.

## Scene 1 — Hold still

**Panel 1**

Match the previous chapter's final wide composition, then move slightly closer. Chae approaches the equipment shelf, remaining in view of both participants.

**CHAE:** Hold out a hand. Both of you.

Nib watches Taeo do it before opening his own hand.

**Panel 2**

Chae lays a small contact sensor in Nib's palm. Its casing is smooth, with a short wrist strap. An identical one already rests in Taeo's hand.

**CHAE:** These only measure. They won't move either of you.

Nib turns his wrist to inspect it.

**Panel 3**

A broad side view keeps the distance between them visible. Chae checks a small physical display on the shelf. Both instrument traces are steady and low; no connecting line has appeared.

**CHAE:** Taeo, look at Nib. Don't try to give him an order.

**Panel 4**

Close on Taeo trying much too hard. Jaw tight, eyes fixed, free hand half clenched.

Nib looks back at him.

**NIB:** Do you have to do that?

**TAEO:** Do what?

**Panel 5**

Nib copies the stare just enough to make his meaning plain. Dry irritation, not a distorted comedy face.

Taeo blinks and looks away for an instant.

**TAEO:** Sorry.

**Panel 6**

Chae glances up from the unchanged traces.

**CHAE:** Breathe. You're looking for a feeling, not trying to make something happen.

She has a normal first exercise for an inexperienced summoner. She does not know what Taeo's unusual connection will do.

**Panel 7**

Taeo exhales. His shoulders drop. He looks back at Nib without fixing his expression in place.

Nib has adjusted the oversized wrist strap. The loose end keeps slipping from his fingers.

**Panel 8**

Nib traps the loose strap against the sensor with his thumb and presses it flat.

Close enough to see the ordinary thumb pressure, with no light or magical effect.

**Panel 9**

Taeo opens his free hand abruptly and looks down at its palm.

**TAEO:** Wait.

His sensor is in the other hand. Nothing touches this palm.

**Panel 10**

Nib releases the strap and looks up.

**TAEO:** There was something. Here.

He touches the center of his empty palm with one finger.

**CHAE:** What did it feel like?

**Panel 11**

**TAEO:** Like someone pressing it.

He looks from his hand to Nib's.

Chae checks the instrument. A brief disturbance is visible in each trace at the same point on the display. Keep the graphic simple, without numbers or a diagnostic label.

**Panel 12**

Chae turns the display so they can see it.

**CHAE:** Both readings changed there.

Nib looks at the two short marks.

**NIB:** Is that the way back?

**Panel 13**

Chae's answer comes without a dramatic pause.

**CHAE:** Not by itself. We need to find out what passed between you.

Taeo lowers his hand. Nib looks at the sensor again.

## Scene 2 — Again

**Panel 14**

Chae puts a thick, soft test pad on the low stool beside Nib. She presses it once with two fingers; it indents easily and springs back.

**CHAE:** Nib, press this with your free hand when I point. Gently.

Nib feels the surface before nodding once.

**Panel 15**

Chae indicates the blank wall behind Taeo. No blindfold or restraint.

**CHAE:** Face that way. Tell us if you feel it again.

Taeo turns. He can no longer see Nib, the pad, or Chae's hand.

**Panel 16**

A wide overhead establishes the whole test: Taeo facing the wall; Nib at the stool behind him; Chae visible to Nib but outside Taeo's view. The sensors remain in their opposite hands.

Chae points silently. Nib presses the soft pad.

**Panel 17**

Taeo's empty fingers flex. Use a close-up of the hand, then his face within the same vertical composition. No floating indicator tells him the answer.

**TAEO:** Now.

Behind him, the pad is still compressed beneath Nib's palm.

**Panel 18**

Nib lifts his hand. Taeo's fingers relax.

**TAEO:** Gone.

Chae marks one short line in her notebook. It occupies the edge of the frame, not the center.

**Panel 19**

Chae holds a flat palm toward Nib: wait. He keeps his hand clear of the pad.

Taeo remains facing the wall, waiting.

A quiet beat. No one speaks. The pad is visibly untouched.

**Panel 20**

Chae points again. Nib presses.

Taeo looks down at his empty palm.

**TAEO:** There.

His surprise is beginning to become concentration.

**Panel 21**

Nib releases the pad. He looks at Chae, then at Taeo's back.

**NIB:** Which hand?

**Panel 22**

Taeo starts to turn, catches himself, and keeps facing the wall.

**TAEO:** My left.

**NIB:** I mean mine.

**TAEO:** I don't know.

The reader can see that Nib used his right. The sensation is not a faithful duplicate of Nib's body.

**Panel 23**

Nib curls his empty right hand and uncurls it. Nothing touches the pad.

**NIB:** What am I doing now?

Taeo waits for the feeling. It does not come.

**TAEO:** I can't tell.

**Panel 24**

Chae gestures for Taeo to turn back.

**CHAE:** All right. Turn around.

Taeo turns, looking at Nib with a new, cautious interest.

**CHAE:** You felt pressure twice without watching. You didn't see what he was doing.

**Panel 25**

Nib considers the pad. Then he places his hand over it again, without pressing yet.

**NIB:** Once more.

Chae checks Taeo, who nods and turns back toward the wall.

This repetition is Nib's initiative, testing something that concerns him. Do not play it as eagerness to entertain Taeo.

**Panel 26**

Nib presses. The pad gives beneath his palm.

Taeo waits, brows drawing together.

**TAEO:** Nothing.

**Panel 27**

Nib presses a little farther into the soft pad. Chae immediately raises her hand.

**CHAE:** Don't press harder.

Nib stops and lifts his hand. There is no injury, recoil or transfer of force.

**Panel 28**

Taeo faces them again. He rubs his empty palm once, as if the faint feeling might return.

**TAEO:** I had it.

**CHAE:** You did. And this time you didn't.

She takes the pad off the stool, ending the test rather than making him chase the result.

## Scene 3 — What it doesn't do

**Panel 29**

Chae brings Taeo back to the other question from the interview.

**CHAE:** Now try bringing up those words again. Just look. Don't select anything.

Taeo focuses on the empty space before him and raises his free hand as he did at home. Nothing appears. Leave a short beat within the composition before his reply.

**TAEO:** Still nothing.

**CHAE:** All right. Leave it for now.

This is a visible retry with Chae present, not a new activation method or proof the interface is permanently inaccessible.

**Panel 30**

Chae collects the sensors. Nib removes his own and places it in her waiting hand.

Taeo remains on his mark.

**TAEO:** Could that work when he's somewhere else?

**CHAE:** Maybe. We haven't tested distance.

No promises about range, gates or remote combat.

**Panel 31**

Nib steps off his mark toward Chae.

**NIB:** But you can't send me back.

Chae meets his gaze directly.

**CHAE:** I can't do that with what we've found.

**Panel 32**

Nib stands still. The test pad is now on the shelf again. A useful thing has happened, and it is far smaller than the thing he came for.

Allow a little vertical space. No thought caption.

**Panel 33**

Taeo looks toward the place where he first felt the pressure, then back at Nib.

**TAEO:** There must be something we can look for.

**NIB:** My watch post.

The answer comes promptly. It is obvious to him.

**Panel 34**

Chae brings a blank sheet from the desk and lays it with a pencil on the low stool. Nib can reach it comfortably while standing.

**CHAE:** Show me.

No additional origin interview. Move directly into a physical attempt to communicate the place.

**Panel 35**

Over Nib's shoulder. He draws a rough ridge, a crooked tree and a low stone wall. Functional marks, not a professional map or a secret emblem.

**NIB:** Wall here. Tree behind it.

He puts a short mark along the wall.

**NIB:** I sat here. You could see the lower path.

**Panel 36**

Taeo leans forward from the other side of the stool, careful not to cover the paper with his shadow.

**TAEO:** How tall was the wall?

Nib holds a hand near his own chest.

Taeo looks at that height, then at the tiny mark representing Nib. The drawing begins to become a place to him.

**Panel 37**

Chae takes a tablet from her desk and brings it down to their level. On it are a few landscape thumbnails beneath an ordinary heading, **PUBLIC GATE SURVEYS**. No readable mission rewards, ranks or recruitment offer.

**CHAE:** We have recordings from surveyed gates. We can start by looking for that landscape.

**Panel 38**

Nib looks sharply at Taeo.

**NIB:** You said dungeon.

**TAEO:** I guessed.

**CHAE:** This is somewhere we can check. It doesn't mean that's where you're from.

The distinction takes one exchange, not an argument about terminology.

**Panel 39**

Chae opens a landscape view: an ordinary rocky hollow under pale daylight. Nib compares it with his sketch, then shakes his head.

**NIB:** Not that.

**CHAE:** All right.

She moves on without pushing a match. No mysterious shared symbol or near-identical landmark appears.

**Panel 40**

A later moment, marked by Chae closing the tablet and Taeo putting his jacket back on. Nib holds his folded drawing.

Chae hands Taeo a single assessment sheet; the only reader-legible finding is **Low output. Intermittent sensory response.**

**CHAE:** I'll send you the recordings and keep checking the arrival report.

**CHAE:** Don't try a dismissal to see what happens. We haven't established where he'd go.

This is a safety recommendation under uncertainty, not proof a dismissal command exists.

**Panel 41**

Taeo folds the assessment sheet and puts it in his pocket.

**TAEO:** I won't.

Nib holds his original sketch. A photocopy lies beside Chae's notes on the desk. Chae opens the exit for them.

**CHAE:** If you find a place that looks familiar, send it to me.

**Staging clarification:** Chae has made one ordinary photocopy during the short time cut. Nib takes and keeps his original; the copy stays with Chae. No additional panel is needed for copying.

## Scene 4 — The aisle again

**Panel 42**

Clear time transition. Late afternoon, just before four. Exterior of the grocery store from Chapter 1. The cordon is gone, the doors are open, and ordinary shopping has resumed.

Taeo and Nib stand outside. Taeo has his work vest folded over one arm; Nib carries the folded sketch. They have visibly returned home between scenes to collect the vest, without implying another assessment, power test or agreement happened there.

**TAEO:** This is where I work.

**Panel 43**

Nib looks through the window, searching the ordinary aisles.

**NIB:** Where's the gate?

**TAEO:** Gone. The hunters cleared it.

Nib looks up at him, then back through the glass. The storefront offers no route for either of them to investigate.

**Panel 44**

Inside. Taeo's coworker looks up from putting a price label on a shelf. Their face brightens briefly at Taeo, then their eyes move down to Nib.

**COWORKER:** You brought someone.

**TAEO:** This is Nib. I awakened yesterday.

The admission lands in ordinary conversation. No stunned customer chorus.

**Panel 45**

The coworker sets the label roll down.

**COWORKER:** Yesterday?

**TAEO:** After we got out.

A brief look passes between them: the shaking water bottle now has more context.

**Panel 46**

Nib looks beyond them into the store. A hanging sign marks the aisle where the gate stood. Nothing magical remains.

The coworker looks back to Nib.

**COWORKER:** Does he need to stay near you?

**TAEO:** We don't know how far yet.

**Panel 47**

Taeo speaks to the coworker but keeps Nib included in the composition.

**TAEO:** Can he wait in the break room while I'm here? I'll ask the manager too.

**NIB:** I can hear you.

**Panel 48**

The coworker's gaze snaps to Nib. One believable startled beat: their hand stops halfway back to the label roll on the shelf.

**COWORKER:** Oh.

Then, directly to him:

**COWORKER:** It's small back there. I'll show you.

**Panel 49**

The coworker opens the break-area door. Nib walks in to inspect it himself; Taeo follows far enough to see the space.

The same cramped room from Chapter 1: staff schedule, tissue box, upturned crate, narrow surface for a meal. Nib's height makes it a usable space without rendering the adult furnishings toy-sized.

**COWORKER:** I'll check with the manager. He's in the stockroom.

**Panel 50**

Nib places his folded sketch on the low crate and unfolds it. Taeo stands beside the open door, looking at the familiar staff schedule.

**NIB:** How long?

**TAEO:** My shift? Six hours.

Nib looks up. That is a long time to sit with no answer.

**Panel 51**

Taeo's phone vibrates. A message from the assessment office contains the promised survey-recording link; use one ordinary message bubble, **The public survey recordings we looked at. — Chae**.

He looks at the link, then at Nib's drawing.

**Panel 52**

Taeo sits on the floor beside the crate so the phone is within Nib's reach. He starts a landscape recording and shows a single action on the touchscreen.

**TAEO:** Tap here to stop it.

Nib touches the screen. The landscape freezes.

**Panel 53**

Taeo leaves his phone propped against the tissue box on the crate, beside the sketch. He is putting on his vest as the coworker returns to the doorway.

**COWORKER:** Manager says it's fine. Just keep this doorway clear.

**NIB:** It is clear.

Taeo catches the familiar wording and almost smiles. Do not give Nib a matching sentimental reaction.

**Panel 54**

From inside the break room, looking out. Taeo pauses at the threshold.

**TAEO:** Call me if you need—

Nib already has one finger poised over the paused picture.

**NIB:** I know where you are.

Taeo takes that in, then goes back to the counter.

**Panel 55**

Final tall composition. In the foreground, Nib sits on the floor beside the crate, sketch open, watching moving country he does not recognize. He restarts the recording himself.

Through the clear break-room doorway, beyond a strip of ordinary shelving, Taeo is visible helping a customer. Neither is staring toward the other.

No dialogue, glowing thread, matching pressure pulse, miraculous landscape recognition or new-system reward. Taeo has something he must do. Nib has something he can do.

**END CHAPTER 4**

## Production and continuity ledger

### New observations proposed by this draft
- During a supervised same-room exercise, Taeo experiences a faint pressure sensation in his empty left palm while Nib applies pressure with his own right hand. The physical contact sensors record a coincident disturbance. An unseen repeat succeeds twice, with one untouched-pad interval producing no report. A third attempt fails. This supports an intermittent sensory connection; it does not establish range, exact transmission fidelity, cause, control over another body, reciprocity, pain sharing, force transfer or a magical contract.
- Nib elects to participate and initiates the last attempt. Nothing establishes a universal opt-in mechanic, automatic enrollment, permanent monitoring or inability to end a future link. Ordinary summoner instruction helps Taeo attend to a possible sensation; Chae does not understand his whole power.
- Nib describes a watch post by a low stone wall, with a crooked tree behind it and a view of a lower path. It is a mundane remembered place. Chae retains a copy; Nib keeps the original. None of the recordings shown matches it. Do not turn the tree or drawing into an unexplained magic sigil.
- The office supplies public survey recordings as a finite, accessible place to begin looking, not evidence that Nib must come from a dungeon. Chae remains willing to investigate. Today's appointment ends with an actual written finding and an action they can take; no new appointment is required before story movement.
- The coworker and manager now know Nib is present. Taeo tells the coworker he awakened during the previous incident, and the coworker hears Nib speak. The manager's permission covers waiting in this break room for this shift, not employment, accommodation or universal workplace access.

### Deliberately unresolved
Final rank and field authorization; source world and return mechanism; interface access; dismissal; living-versus-construct ontology; exact sensory activation conditions and range; combat membership; gate crossing; death/recreation; second draw. The script does not disguise any of these as already tested offscreen. The office's low-output finding is retained without a hidden high-rank reversal. The story can move while a specialist investigation remains incomplete; do not begin Chapter 5 by repeating this assessment.

### Props, timing and models
- Starting positions match Chapter 3. Handheld sensors are added from its established equipment shelf; the prior palm-sized desk device is not silently transformed into a pair.
- Taeo holds the sensor in his right hand; his left stays empty. Nib holds his sensor in his left, operates its loose strap with his right, and presses the pad with his right. Preserve this throughout. The pad sits on a low stool at comfortable adult-goblin hand height. There is no forceful strain.
- Neither hand contact nor instrument causes a return attempt. Never draw a portal around the test floor. No Gate appears in this chapter.
- Chapter 3's eleven-o'clock appointment is followed by an explicit afternoon transition and the established four-o'clock shift. Going home for the work vest is a mundane transit compression; no milestone is completed in the gap. The six-hour shift is a proposed new ordinary-work detail.
- Nib keeps the pencil sketch through departure and arrival. Taeo keeps the assessment sheet in his jacket. Phone goes from Taeo's pocket to the crate in the break area. Nib learns only pause/play here, not reading, web searching or full device fluency.
- Do not dress the coworker as an assessor or invent a celebrity arrival. Retain the established shop and coworker model. No new named character is necessary.
- The last panel's doorway and aisle must permit the shown sight line. If the existing shop layout cannot support it, use a broad threshold view with Taeo passing into the shop instead of inventing a wall opening.

## Causal and pacing audit

The promised test happens immediately, yields an observable success, distinguishes the sensation from eyesight, then fails on repetition. The small success cannot answer Nib's larger request, so he identifies the concrete place he wants to find. That produces a sketch, a resource and an actual search rather than another promise to ask someone. Work obliges Taeo to leave the office and prevents his new role from replacing the life already established. Nib's unfamiliarity with a phone becomes a small, useful lesson with an immediate independent action.

The relationship gain is access and room to act, not affection or a combat pact. Nib tests the result for himself, corrects an assumption at the shop and begins looking while Taeo works. Taeo asks a useful question about the wall instead of proposing an origin for Nib; then leaves his phone with him. Their brief comedy comes from their present wants and the coworker's revised expectations, not humiliation.

The test occupies roughly half the chapter, its limits and consequences a fifth, and the return to ordinary work the remainder. Silent beats have separate functions: accidental discovery, a no-pressure control, the failed repeat, disappointment, and independent activity. They should not be padded into repeated gazes. The 55 panels can be compressed into fewer scroll units without deleting causal evidence.
