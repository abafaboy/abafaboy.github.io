## Scene 1 — You came

Taeo carries a plain small grocery bag in his LEFT hand; phone pocketed. Nib looks back once at the bus as its doors close, interested in the unfamiliar vehicle without a transport lesson.

### Panel 2

NIB: You're late?

TAEO: No.

Nib looks at the hand that hesitated. He does not diagnose the family relationship.
