## Scene 1 — The hour

### Panel 1
Friday morning. The yard is already open. Taeo and Nib arrive together at the equipment bench. Taeo takes the folded assessment sheet from his jacket and gives it to Minho. Nib watches a worker finish placing a screen in the distance.

MINHO: Morning. You're on time.

TAEO: You asked me to bring this.

No journey recap, greeting montage or new introduction.

### Panel 2
Minho reads the sheet once, folds it along its existing crease, and returns it. Taeo puts it in his inside jacket pocket before anything else is handed out. The writing is not framed for reading.

MINHO: Keep it with you. We're only working in the yard.

Minho turns to Nib with the ochre radio in his hand.

MINHO: Same button as yesterday.

### Panel 3
Nib takes the radio in his LEFT hand. Minho points toward the newly arranged screens; Taeo stays by the bench, clear of working traffic.

MINHO: Walk the new route. Tell me anything the crew will need moved before they start.

NIB: All right.

This is the agreed paid ground check, not a test for an announced rank. No promise of expertise with modern machines.

### Panel 4
Low, honest view from Nib's position in the north–south passage. At its eastward turn, a loose loop of cord lies across the exit. One end leads to an unused coil beside the outer face of the screen. Nib stops before it. His size does not confer supernatural detection; it is simply visible when he reaches the turn.

Nib presses the radio button.

NIB, RADIO: There's cord across the way out. By the last screen.

### Panel 5
At the exit apron, Minho crouches and gathers the loose cord toward its coil, clearing the entire walking line. Nib stands in the passage, radio lowered in his left hand, free right hand indicating the affected stretch. Nobody steps into a tightening loop.

MINHO: This one?

NIB: Yes. It goes round the foot there, too.

The cord's short wrap around the screen support foot is plainly visible. Minho lifts the loose loop clear by hand; no screen moves or collapses.

### Panel 6
Compact time-passage composition: later in the same hour, Nib returns along the exterior of a different screen toward the bench. The new route is now clear. Minho and an ordinary crew member walk it at adult height before opening it for use. Taeo watches from the same bench apron. Morning light has moved slightly.

No dialogue. This closes ordinary checks economically. Do not add a montage of hidden hazards, ten discoveries or a score screen. Nib has performed a modest supervised job, and the crew checks its own equipment.

### Panel 7
At the bench, Minho checks his watch: nine. Keep this an ordinary glance, not a giant clock insert. Nib places the radio into Minho's waiting hand. Minho puts it in the charging rack.

MINHO: That's the hour. Thanks.

Nib flexes his now-empty left hand once. He has been walking, not battling exhaustion.

### Panel 8
Minho takes two ten-thousand-won notes from a small payment envelope and holds them down where Nib can take them comfortably. Nib takes both with his right hand. Taeo stays out of the exchange.

MINHO: Twenty thousand. As agreed.

NIB: Both of these?

MINHO: Both.

Payment is completed here. No bank account, ownership law or payroll scene is introduced.

### Panel 9
Nib folds the notes once and places them in his right-hip pouch beside, not inside, his separately folded original sketch. He reties the pouch.

Taeo's expression is pleased, with a trace of envy he does not announce. The job was Nib's. He has watched rather than quietly been promoted to operator.

NIB, TO MINHO: Where is your crew going next?

## Scene 3 — His money

### Panel 21
Inside the shop entrance at the order counter. Two plain sample food photographs are mounted low enough for Nib to see. Nib indicates the noodle bowl. Taeo stands beside him, not between him and the server.

NIB: How much is that one?

The server starts to answer Taeo, catches that Nib asked, and redirects their gaze down. One contained beat.

### Panel 22
Nib takes out his two folded notes, separates ONE ten-thousand-won note, and returns the other to the pouch before holding out payment in his right hand. Taeo has not reached for his wallet yet.

NIB: One.

TAEO: You don't have to—

NIB: I'm buying mine.

Matter-of-fact, not a rejection of shared meals or repayment of a moral debt. Taeo lets the unfinished offer go.

### Panel 23
The server puts three one-thousand-won notes on a low change tray within Nib's reach. Nib gathers them; Taeo points to the three notes without taking them.

NIB: Right.

Nib puts the change into his pouch and reties it with both hands now empty. This is one small purchase, not a literacy or arithmetic tutorial. His balance is now thirteen thousand won.
