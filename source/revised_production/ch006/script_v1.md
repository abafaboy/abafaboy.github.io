## Scene 1 — Something we can do

### Panel 1
Morning at the apartment entrance. Taeo, shoes on, crouches to tie the second lace. Nib stands beside the open door holding his original folded watch-post drawing. The two rough route-map faces on the cardboard rest on the folding table farther inside. No recording plays.

A small phone-message inset supplies the cause of the outing, not another conversation scene. Three short messages, in this order:

TAEO, TEXT, SENT LAST NIGHT: Is there any work I can do without going into a gate?

CHAE, TEXT, THIS MORNING: A crew supervisor can talk you through it. Minho runs a survey crew. West practice yard, ten today, if you can make it.

TAEO, TEXT: We'll come.

No new diagnosis or assertion that Chae completed her investigation.

### Panel 2
Taeo puts the phone into his jacket. Nib checks the folded sketch's edge, then tucks it flat into his belt pouch without forcing an impossibly small pocket. Preserve the existing pouch size; one folded sheet fits.

NIB: Does he go through gates?

TAEO: His crew does. They make surveys.

NIB: Then I'll ask him.

They leave. No renewed agreement to remain together.

### Panel 3
Exterior arrival at the practice yard. Through the open pedestrian gate, an ordinary crew is working. A trainee carries coiled rope; another checks a helmet strap. Minho steadies a wheeled equipment rack while a colleague fastens a loose clamp.

Taeo checks the address on his phone. Nib is already looking at the work.

TAEO: Minho?

### Panel 4
Minho looks over without letting go of the rack.

MINHO: Chae sent you?

TAEO: I'm Taeo. This is Nib.

MINHO, TO COLLEAGUE: Hold that. I'll get the other wrench.

The colleague takes over the rack before Minho releases it. Their work has not waited for Taeo to arrive.

### Panel 5
Minho selects a wrench from an open tool tray. Nib addresses him directly.

NIB: Have you seen a place like this?

Nib unfolds his watch-post sketch and holds it up. No new elaborate origin account.

### Panel 6
Minho crouches to view the drawing, wrench held low and away from Nib. A brief eye flick from the speaking goblin to Taeo, then back to the drawing.

MINHO: Not one I recognize.

He studies it long enough to be credible, then shakes his head.

NIB: All right.

This negative answer is immediate. Do not tease a hidden match.

### Panel 7
Minho returns the wrench to his colleague, then turns fully to the visitors. Nib folds the sketch and replaces it in his pouch.

MINHO: Chae said you wanted work outside.

TAEO: If there's any I can do.

MINHO: Carrying, equipment checks. Dispatch, once you know the job.

### Panel 8
Beyond them, a trainee directs a plain wooden porter to set down two heavy padded cases. It obeys. No dialogue from the porter.

TAEO: Could I stay outside while he scouts?

Minho looks toward Nib instead of answering for him.

NIB: I'd want to see where you're going first.

MINHO: So would I.

A small dry acknowledgement. No promise of gate entry.

### Panel 9
Minho starts toward the course entrance. The visitors follow.

MINHO: We've got ten minutes before the next group. Show me how you talk to each other.

TAEO: We haven't tried that yet.

MINHO: Good time to find out.

The course is a practical opportunity in an existing crew session, not a second ability assessment.

## Scene 2 — The short way

### Panel 10
High oblique establishing view of the empty course. Make both routes and the shared end apron legible to the reader. Minho, Taeo and Nib stand at START. The cart is parked beside them, case securely strapped. The narrow left exit and broad right exit must be visible together here; later conceal them with honest screen angles.

No dialogue. Keep this wide panel large enough to orient the route once.

### Panel 11
Minho rolls the cart two paces forward and back with one hand, showing it is easy to move. He points into the course.

MINHO: Get this to the other end. Scout the route, then talk him through it.

Nib looks along the wheel span, then through the entrance.

MINHO: Walk. Nothing here is going to jump out.

### Panel 12
At the equipment bench beside START, Minho hands them the two radios. A small close view shows his thumb pressing his own handset's talk button, then releasing.

MINHO: Hold this to speak. Let go to listen.

Nib tries his button with the handset below his mouth.

NIB, RADIO: Taeo?

### Panel 13
Taeo's radio gives back Nib's voice a fraction of a beat later. He looks down, faintly pleased by this ordinary tool working exactly as promised.

TAEO, RADIO: I hear you.

MINHO: Keep it that simple.

No echo multiplies into magical telepathy.

### Panel 14
Nib walks into the course, radio in his left hand. Taeo stays at START with the cart, radio in his right. Minho watches from the apron beside him.

Nib reaches the fork. The left route looks shorter; daylight from END shows around its turn. He chooses left to inspect it.

No dialogue.

### Panel 15
Nib reaches the left route's narrow final opening. Front-on from END, we can see his shoulders pass easily between the two posts. He turns sideways to look back along their width, then down at the ground.

His gaze sharpens. He remembers the cart's spread of wheels without a flashback.

### Panel 16
Back at START, Taeo has taken the handles. He looks into the entrance, wanting to get moving.

TAEO, RADIO: Left?

### Panel 17
Nib turns back through the narrow opening, already heading to check the other branch.

NIB, RADIO: I went left. The gap at the end—

### Panel 18
Taeo presses his own talk button before Nib has finished. Show the premature button press: his handset stops receiving the rest of Nib's report. Nib is still holding his own talk button and cannot hear Taeo's reply either. These are simple half-duplex radios. No magical failure or unexplained silence: each mistakenly thinks the other heard.

TAEO, RADIO: Okay. Coming.

Minho's eyes go to Taeo's thumb. He does not let an unsafe action begin: the course and cart are harmless at walking speed.

### Panel 19
Taeo wheels the light cart through the entrance and takes the left branch. His right-hand radio is now hooked onto the top of the case strap; both hands are on the cart handles. Minho walks a few paces behind, visible at the entrance.

Nib, returning along the left branch, meets them before the narrow opening.

NIB: Why are you coming this way?

TAEO: You said left.

### Panel 20
Nib steps aside so Taeo can see the opening ahead. Keep the wheel span and post spacing visible in the same low three-quarter view.

NIB: I said I went left.

Taeo stops the cart short of the opening. Its wheels never wedge between the posts.

### Panel 21
A short close view of Taeo's face. He has already understood. His hand comes off the cart handle toward the radio, then stops.

TAEO: I cut you off.

NIB: Yes.

No second apology or scolding speech.

### Panel 22
Minho stands at the fork, leaving room to reverse. He points back toward START.

MINHO: Take it back. Let him finish looking.

Taeo carefully wheels the cart backward. Nib moves toward the right branch. The group corrects a minor error, not a dramatic failure siren.

### Panel 23
Compact parallel composition, connected by the actual plywood wall rather than a magical split screen. Taeo returns the cart to START and unhooks his radio. On the far side, Nib walks the longer right route.

No dialogue. Taeo has to wait while someone else does useful work. Let that impatience register in his fingers, not a thought caption.

## Scene 3 — Tell me when

### Panel 24
At the broad right opening, Nib looks back along the right route and then at its clear width. The passage is visibly wide enough for the cart. He traces its turns in the air once with his free right forefinger, mentally reversing the route to explain it from Taeo's starting point.

No supernatural spatial overlay, numbered arrows or perfect blueprint.

### Panel 25
Nib presses his radio button.

NIB, RADIO: Take the right route. There's room all the way through.

At START, Taeo listens with his thumb clear of his button. Minho can see the difference.

Taeo presses only after the voice ends.

TAEO, RADIO: The right route. Do I start?

### Panel 26
Nib looks back at the long stretch he has just walked. The cart is still out of sight. He makes his own small revision.

NIB, RADIO: Wait. I'll come to the corner.

He walks back from END to the course's northeast corner, where he can see the east leg down to Taeo's next turn. He stays outside the cart's future path.

From Nib's position at the northeast corner: the long east passage runs toward the southeast corner. The START apron and eastbound bottom passage are hidden around that corner.

NIB, RADIO: Now. Tell me when you're through the first turn.

### Panel 27
Taeo parks the radio on the case strap again, talk button facing outward and speaker unobstructed. He pushes the cart through the entrance and turns RIGHT, moving east along the bottom passage toward the southeast corner. Both hands manage the turn. Minho follows without coaching.

No dialogue. The turn should occupy one clean action panel.

### Panel 28
At the southeast corner, Taeo stops the cart before turning north. He takes the radio off the strap; his left hand stays on the stationary cart handle.

TAEO, RADIO: First turn done. There's another left here.

NIB, RADIO: Yes. I'm at the far end.

Two sequential balloons, not simultaneous transmissions.

### Panel 29
Use one tall flowing composition with a small upper approach beat and a larger exit beat below; do not duplicate people in one continuous physical space. Taeo hooks the radio back, rolls the cart around the southeast corner, then sees Nib at the northeast corner. Nib has moved one step out of the lane and points west toward the broad exit.

NIB: This way.

The moment of seeing the actual person at the correct corner is the small payoff. No magical sensation accompanies it.

A wide view across END. Taeo wheels the cart out through the broad right opening. Nib follows along its side at a safe distance from the wheel. The narrow opening is visible farther left, recalling the first mistake without a replay.

The cart reaches the painted finish bar intact.

SFX: KRRR.

### Panel 30
Taeo stops the cart beyond the finish bar, sets its support feet down, then breathes out. A brief genuine grin; completing even this simple thing felt good.

TAEO: We got it through.

Nib looks back at the route.

NIB: Took you long enough.

Dry, not sneering. Taeo's grin survives it.

### Panel 31
A distant whistle from the yard's next group. Minho checks his watch and gestures them clear of END. A regular trainee is already approaching with their own radio, ready for the next rotation. Beyond them a colleague brings replacement screen panels from the shed; the afternoon course reset is ordinary scheduled work.

MINHO: It did. But you stopped guessing.

He walks with them toward the equipment bench. The course continues to be used after they finish.

## Scene 4 — Who got the job?

### Panel 32
At the bench, Nib sets his radio into Minho's open hand. Taeo has retrieved his from the cart strap and does the same. Minho collects both before making the offer.

MINHO, TO NIB: We're moving the screens this afternoon. I need someone to check the new route before the crews arrive tomorrow.

### Panel 33
Nib studies him. Taeo starts to speak, then leaves the question to Nib.

NIB: This place?

MINHO: Here. No gate. One hour, twenty thousand won. I'll be here the whole time.

A modest supervised ground trial, not a salary or a bounty. The offer is concrete and low-risk. Minho still needs to check whether Nib can reliably report what matters to his crew.

### Panel 34
Nib looks at Taeo.

NIB: How much is that?

TAEO: About two of my lunches.

Nib thinks, glances at the course, then back at Minho.

NIB: All right.

He knows its rough buying power before accepting. No pay is received today.

### Panel 35
Taeo leans forward, encouraged.

TAEO: And I could do the radio?

MINHO: Tomorrow? I'll take his reports.

Taeo's expression stalls.

### Panel 36
Minho clips both exercise radios into the charging rack, addressing Taeo plainly.

MINHO: You need more practice before you guide a crew.

TAEO: Right.

MINHO: You can watch. Bring that assessment sheet.

No rank downgrade or exclusion from learning. The supervisor is not punishing him for cowardice; a useful role requires a learned skill.

### Panel 37
Taeo takes out his phone to check his work schedule before agreeing. The screen remains angled away: no full roster for the reader.

MINHO: Eight tomorrow. Done by nine.

TAEO: Yes. I can come.

He puts the phone away after checking. This confirms only Friday morning. His existing Saturday shift and Sunday family visit are unchanged; no new shift swap or scheduling conversation is needed.

### Panel 38
Leaving the yard on foot. The operating crew remains visible behind them. Nib rolls his right shoulder once after the unfamiliar handset grip. Taeo is quiet.

NIB: You're annoyed.

TAEO: A bit.

NIB: I didn't ask him to leave you out.

### Panel 39
Taeo looks down at him as they walk.

TAEO: I know. I wanted to be good at it.

A beat. Nib looks ahead.

NIB: Then let me finish a sentence.

Taeo gives a short embarrassed laugh. It lands because both know the exact mistake.

### Panel 40
They reach the ordinary pavement outside. Nib pauses, takes out his folded watch-post drawing and checks that it hasn't been creased through the tree. He smooths the edge with his thumb.

NIB: I'll ask what places they're surveying next.

TAEO: Tomorrow?

NIB: Tomorrow.

The paid task is useful to Nib, but it has not replaced his reason for being here.

### Panel 41
Final tall panel, viewed from inside the yard looking outward. Nib leads along the pavement by half a pace; Taeo follows, phone in hand, saving the meeting time. Behind the fence, the next crew takes the same cart through the practice course with practiced efficiency. The world has its own competent people.

Taeo's phone shows only the short note he has just entered:

8:00 — yard. Bring assessment sheet.

No final new notification, looming monster, magic window or hidden home match. Nib has accepted his first bounded job on his own terms. Taeo has found a role he actually wants and discovered that he cannot simply claim it.
