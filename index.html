An experiment, not an abandoned account

From March to June 2026 I ran a Twitch clipping account on TikTok as a deliberate three-month test: 7.3M views, 600K likes, 69K shares. The best clip had 663K views, and 87% of traffic came from the For You feed.

But only 1,129 of 53,700 profile visitors followed, about 2%. The algorithm handed out reach; almost nobody chose the account. That was the finding, so I stopped and wrote it up.
