## Rules for every variant
- **No text at all:** no letters, numbers, logos or UI. Claude adds the words afterwards.
- **16:9 safe area.** Claude crops the 1536×1024 image to 1536×864 through the middle, so the top and bottom 80 px are lost: keep every face, hand and weapon inside the middle 864 px.
- **Room for the words.** Keep the top-left third calm and simple (sky, glow, a plain wall): no face, hand or weapon there. Claude puts one big outlined word and an arrow in it.
- **Reads at phone size.** Big shapes, faces large, a strong rim light separating each figure from the background, saturated river blue against Jaeha's orange. Shrink it to 320 px wide: you must still read both faces and Lyra's bow.
- **Lyra** exactly as design 2.10, every lock on the right side: the braid over her LEFT shoulder, the bracer on her LEFT forearm, the glove on her RIGHT hand, the quiver at her RIGHT hip, the chest guard over the left side of her chest, very pale cool skin with silver freckles. Beautiful and confident: the smug half-smile, chin up, a hand on her hip or the bow drawn.
- **YouTube's limit, strictly:** the focal point is her face, her smile and her bow, never her chest, hips or thighs. No sexual pose, no cleavage close-up, no low camera looking up her skirt, no camera aimed at the crotch. YouTube age-restricts a video whose thumbnail does that, and that would bury it.
- **Jaeha** in his orange trap-tester vest with the block T patch (the only letter allowed), reacting, as large on screen as Lyra.
- **Proportions per 4b:** Lyra a little taller than Jaeha when they stand on the same ground; Nib's head at Jaeha's belt; full adult bodies, never compressed.
