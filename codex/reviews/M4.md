## What passes
- **The design** (`CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md` 4b.4):
  - a giant hooded serpent in jade-green scales with dull gold bands;
  - two bone-white horns sweeping back from the skull (the profile makes the sweep clear);
  - yellow eyes with slit pupils;
  - a small violet Hush crack on the brow;
  - heat shimmer and steam rising off the body.
- **The belly:** muted pale-ochre scutes. The design didn't say, and these are now canon.
- **Details:**
  - the horns from behind;
  - the eye;
  - a patch of scales with a gold band;
  - the brow crack;
  - frost spreading over the scales.
  - The frost is the chapter 12 tell: frost means it is failing.
- **The house style:** flat cel colour and clean line, with no bevelled per-scale rendering (the first front was rejected for that).

## Scale: `scale.png` is the reference
The serpent rears beside the two-storey market building and the footbridge at night, with one ordinary man on the road:
- **Its raised head is at about third-floor height** (9 m), which matches the chapter 11 rule.
- **The body on the road is about 1.5 m thick** next to the man.
- **Small misses, kept under the five-try rule:**
  - the footbridge deck reads a little lower than its 5 m;
  - the man and the building aren't on one exact depth plane.
- Take the numbers from the design: 20 m long, 1.5 m thick, the footbridge 5 m over the road.
