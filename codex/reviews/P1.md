## How to use it
- **Sizes:** each prop is drawn large, not to a common scale. Size them to the hands that hold them; the stake is 1 m long.
- **The stake's timer:** keep it the green screen; the letterer puts the time on it.
- **Sora's compass:** it hangs from her RIGHT wrist (S20) until it shatters in chapter 31.
- **Geum's ladle:** draw it as her own sheets do (S24), with the handle ending in a small eye, not this sheet's hook. It is always in her RIGHT hand.
- **Which prop and when:** the chapter rules in PANEL_BRIEF_v2 section 9 name the props.
