**Finding.** Changing the style block alone moves the look only a little, because the reference images (the V2 sheets, keys and panels) carry their own rendering into every new image. Nothing got worse, so chapter 12 may start with the S38 block now. To move the look further, the next step is the **style keys** (S38-K, `S38_MANHWA_LOOK.md` section 5): new images in the target look that ride along as a reference, plus a sentence that tells the image tool to take only the design from the other references.

**How to use them (every chapter panel from chapter 12 on):**
- Pass the closest key as one reference whenever a slot is free: K1 for close-ups, eyes and reactions; K2 for talk and medium shots, above all with crowds or busy places; K3 for action and impacts.
- **The keys set the line, the shading and how far the background steps back. They never set the light or the time of day.** K2 and K3 came out pale grey; a night scene stays a night scene: follow the panel's named key light and the location's colour key (`MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md` 5.3). Establishing shots stay detailed (clean line art, flat fills), as the density ladder says.
- Keep each character's colours from their sheets, not from a key.
