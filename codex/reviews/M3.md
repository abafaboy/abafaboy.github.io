## What passes
- **The hound** (`CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md` 4b.3), in the front, side and back views:
  - lean and long-legged, with a smooth black body;
  - no eyes and no nose;
  - a long split jaw of white bone teeth;
  - ribs pressing through;
  - cracks down the neck and back shedding violet ash flakes.
- **Details:**
  - the eyeless head with the jaw shut and wide open;
  - the cracked back;
  - a four-toed paw with black claws.
- **Pack:** six hounds running down a shuttered alley at night past a man pressed against a door.
  - The leaders' shoulders reach about half his height (90 cm), and every paw is on the ground.
  - The light is a little inconsistent: the shadows fall away from the bulb. That doesn't matter for a reference.
- **Alpha:**
  - the same animal, heavier in the neck;
  - a ridge of deep cracks down the neck and back that flare violet;
  - side view, and a howling three-quarter view with the head thrown back.

## Scale (Claude's card: `scale_card.png`)
The card stands the side views beside the standard 178 cm man on one baseline:
- **A hound** is 90 cm at the shoulder, at the man's hip.
- **The alpha** is 1.6 m at the shoulder: **level with the man's chin**.
- **Fixed (27 October):** the design, the asset brief, PANEL_BRIEF_v2 and the chapter 10 and 25 scripts used to say the alpha "reaches a man's chest". At 1.6 m that was wrong, so they now say chin. The 1.6 m figure stands.

## How to use it
- **A hound:** attach `front.png` and `turnaround.png`, plus `scale_card.png` whenever a person or summon is in the panel.
- **The alpha:** attach `alpha.png` and `scale_card.png`.
- **Several hounds:** `pack.png` shows the spacing.
- **The tell:** the pack only moves when the alpha howls (chapter 10).
