## What passes
- **The design** (`CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md` 2.18):
  - a blank, rounded granite head with no face;
  - a broad block chest, pillar arms and heavy stone legs with a gap between them;
  - brass bands binding the blocks, with short braces at the thighs and knees for footholds;
  - one small amber test core behind a cross-shaped brass grille at mid-chest, and no core on the back;
  - broad stone feet on rails of light (`details.png`).
- **The style:** matte hand-inked granite, clean cool cel shadows and restrained brass highlights. No text and no scale figure.
