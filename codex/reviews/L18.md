## What passes
- **The canyon** (`key.png`, `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md` 5c), from the ledge by the inner rim:
  - sheer dark rock walls down to a thread of river in sunless violet-grey twilight;
  - steel walkways and stairs bolted to the walls in zig-zags;
  - the giant pipes running down beside them;
  - work lights;
  - the engineers' cabin on the first landing;
  - a locked grille in a far cleft;
  - one tiny worker. Against him the pipes are about 4 m thick.
- **`walkway.png`:** a catwalk with its guardrail over the drop, a square trap stone carved with a spiral set flush in the rock landing, and a big flanged pipe along the wall.
- **`relief.png`:** the carved coral-crowned giant bracing against a great wave, as grey bas-relief on the canyon wall, lit from the maintenance catwalk below. Against the catwalk's rail it reads about 30 to 35 m; the design says 40 m. Not a blocker.
- **`pipe_gallery.png`:** a vaulted tunnel carved with waves, three separate pipes on cradles running into three far arches, and the service aisle along the wall.
- **`junction.png`:** the round wave-carved chamber where the main pipe splits through a Y into two passages, one inspection glass leaking violet light, and a railed route round it.
- **`glass_wall.png`:** the broad curved glass wall, fogged with condensation, a pipe feeding through its lower edge, and the brass plaque with the double hexagon and crescent and no letters.
- **`rim_ledge.png`:**
  - from the approach side across the gap to the exit ledge;
  - the gate's white ellipse inside Kanghan's open steel iris, with its blades and hydraulic arms. Against the ledge's 1.1 m rail it is about 8 m tall, as the design asks;
  - the old crossing broken off at both ends, the river far below.
- **The style holds across all seven:** clean ink, flat colour and hard shadows. The only saturated violet is the junction's leak.
- **Not a blocker:** the key also shows a second, smaller service hut. It changes nothing on the route.
