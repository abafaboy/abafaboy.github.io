## Why `dam_gate/key.png` is redone
- **The fault: the spillway is drawn far too small for the slit and the people beside it.**
  - **Measured on the key:** the slit at the foot of the chute is 110 px tall, and the chute's bottom edge is about 806 px wide, only 7.3 times the slit's height. With the slit at its canon 2.2 m, the chute is about 16 m wide.
  - **What canon asks:** 40 m wide (18 slit-heights), with its crest 30 m above the apron. On the key the crest is only about four slit-heights up the screen.
  - **The other measures agree:** the press crew, the 3 m tents and the 2 m vans are all large against the dam. Counting its steps, the service stair on the left wingwall climbs only about 8 to 10 m (an estimate).
- **Why it matters:**
  - Chapter 16 opens on a high (20 m) camera over "a huge sloping chute ... Scale: the chute is 30 m tall; the people are small".
  - Chapter 21 puts a crowd of hundreds at its foot.
  - A reference at half size would teach every panel a small dam.
- **The rest is good:** the midday light, the stained concrete, the five closed sluice bays, the press tents, cameras, rope line and vans, the stair on the left wingwall, and the green hills.
- **Why a redo, not a canon change:** this was the first try, so four of the five remain.
