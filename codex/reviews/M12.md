## What passes

  - a whale-like body with a broad blunt head and a broad tail;
  - grey-blue hide crusted with coral, barnacles and kelp;
  - the rusted plates of the old lid stuck in her back like scales;
  - two huge webbed hands at the front;
  - the long curved crest-spine from the top of her head, with the lantern hanging at its tip: a cage of pale bone-white coral round a burning core.
