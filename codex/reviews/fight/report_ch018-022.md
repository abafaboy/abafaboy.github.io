## Headline

- **Nine sequences, graded 4–6/10.** None reaches 8, so all nine get a full storyboard below. Ch22 has no fight.
- **In ch18–20 the hits are often drawn, then covered by the lettering.** The original art shows the contact (or the face) that an SFX or a window then covers in ch19 P01, P28, P33, P34, P35 and P36, and in ch20 P25, P41, P43, P46 and P53.
  - The scripts set `sfx.at` before the art exists, almost always in the upper middle ([0.3–0.5, 0.2–0.3]). That is exactly where Codex centres the contact.
  - This corrects the ch11 note that "hits never land": here they land and are then covered.
- **`full` is drawn at 1536×1024, the same size as `wide`.** That holds for every `full` art file in ch18–22.
  - Every scripted peak therefore reaches the video at talk-panel size: ch18 P01 and P33; ch19 P01, P10, P34 and P36; ch20 P41 and P53.
  - Ch20 has 42 wide panels and one tall (P51, Mok's charge), so the biggest image in Nib's fight belongs to the villain.
- **Lyra is drawn left-handed in five face-on draws:** ch18 P33 (the entrance splash), P38 (the name card) and P57; ch19 P31 and P35. Each time her target was on her screen-left. In P38 she even aims away from the bell, which is behind her at top-right.
- **Rule 7 ("violence happens between panels") should be replaced** (section 8).

## Conventions used in every storyboard row

- **Locks.**
  - Lyra is right-handed: bow in the LEFT hand with the silver bracer on that forearm, gloved RIGHT hand drawing to the right jaw, braid over the LEFT shoulder, quiver at the RIGHT hip. Facing the camera she aims screen-RIGHT. The camera is at her chest height or higher, never at her hips.
  - Borro and Mok carry their shields on the LEFT forearm. Mok's sword is in his RIGHT hand.
  - Nib is 105 cm and barefoot, knives in reverse grip, the husk mask over the upper LEFT of his face.
  - The Bell-Ringer's clapper is in its RIGHT hand; two-handed swings have the RIGHT hand nearer the head.
  - No text in any art.
- **Screen direction, ch18–19.** The team, the pillar and the shields are screen-LEFT; the Bell-Ringer is screen-RIGHT. The team's attacks travel left to right; the giant's blows fall from the upper right. Ch20 has its own rule (section 4).
- **SFX.** The SFX column names a zone, and the art keeps that zone as flat speed lines or flat colour. The letterer sets the SFX there and nowhere else: never on a face, a hand, the contact point or the bell's crack. One SFX per impact; XL only on a chapter's peaks.
- **Shape.** Peaks are `tall` until `full` is drawn in portrait (section 8).
- **Marks.** KEEP means no change. KEEP (re-letter) means the art stays and only the SFX or window moves. REDRAW and NEW go back to Codex.

## 7. Patterns across ch18–22

1. **`full` is drawn the size of `wide`.** Every `full` art file in ch18–22 is 1536×1024; only `tall` (1024×1536) is bigger. Ch18 has 34 wide panels to 6 tall, ch19 40 to 6, ch20 42 to 1. Every scripted splash comes out at the size of a talk panel.
2. **The SFX lands on the contact.** This is the main reason hits "never land" in ch18–20 (about 25 panels, listed in section 1). The cause is mechanical: `sfx.at` is written before the art and clusters at [0.3–0.5, 0.2–0.3], and Codex centres the contact in the same place.
3. **System windows sit on the key beat:** the boss's bell-head (ch18 P01, P08; ch19 P01), Nib at the moment of his seal break (ch20 P40), Jaeha's hands as he falls (ch20 P27), Jaeha's face in his reaction (ch19 P41).
4. **Lyra flips handedness whenever her target is on her screen-left.** The model keeps her facing the camera and mirrors her rather than turning her round.
5. **The Bell-Ringer never swings.** Every blow is a symmetrical overhead lift or a vertical press (ch18 P54, P61; ch19 P09, P10, P28, P32): no twist, no backswing, no arc.
6. **Borro is always the same frontal weightlifter at eye level** under the shield (ch18 P02, P04, P05, P47, P48, P52; ch19 P11, P33). The pose suits a man buried to the waist; the problem is that the camera never moves round him.
7. **Nib stands to attention when hit** (ch20 P22, P26, P34) and is the centre of a symmetrical tableau when he attacks (P21, P41, P42, P44). "Doesn't flinch" needs weight and a turn into the next man, not a straight vertical body.
8. **Lineups:** the team facing the camera in a row (ch18 P02); attackers abreast (ch20 P19, P33); triptychs (P42, P44).
9. **The camera rarely moves.** About four in five fight panels are eye level, side-on and wide. There is no high angle on a fighter in trouble outside the aftermaths, and tilt and foreshortening appear only in ch20 P51.
10. **Two actions in one panel:** ch18 P32; ch19 P28; ch20 P25, P44, P48.
11. **The camera crosses the line by accident:** ch20 P19 and P33 against P22–P34; ch18 P33, P38 and P46 against the rest of the cathedral fight.
12. **The target changes size.** The bell-head, the target of both fights, is as tall as Lyra in ch18 P33 and ch19 P06, but fist-sized on a long neck in ch18 P32, P46, P54 and ch19 P09, P17, P28.

## 8. Rule 7, and three more changes to MANHWA_ART_DIRECTION.md

**Drop rule 7 as written.** It mixes two things: hiding gore, which is right, and hiding contact, which kills a fight. In these chapters the scripts mostly ignore it and do write the contact, so it isn't the main cause here. Where it is applied, the reader loses the hit: ch19 P27 → P28 shows no buckle between the cut and the kneel, and in ch20 P48 the crash happens off-panel.

Proposed replacement:
> **7. Contact is drawn; wounds are not.** The impact panel shows the moment of contact at full size: blade on blade, a blade passing a body as a white slash-flash, a fist or pommel meeting a jaw with a radial burst, an arrow entering bronze. On people, a hit is a thin red slash line or a small spray, never an open wound. Summons don't bleed; the wound opens on Jaeha's CARRY panel. A person's death stays off-panel, in silhouette or as a splash on a wall.

Add three more rules:
- **SFX placement.** Every impact art prompt names where the contact sits in the frame and reserves an SFX zone on the far side, kept as flat speed lines or flat colour. The letterer sets the SFX only in that zone: one per impact, angled along the motion, never on a face, a hand, the contact or the target's weak point. Use L for a key blow and XL only for a chapter's one to three peaks.
- **Peaks.** `full` must be drawn in portrait, taller than `tall` (for example 1024×1792), or not used at all. Until then, write every peak as `tall`.
- **Windows.** Every window gets a reserved empty band named in the art prompt.
