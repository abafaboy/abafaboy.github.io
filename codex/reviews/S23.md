## What Claude fixed
- **The fault:** the tabards had no numbers.
  - The design gives each tabard a big black stencilled number front and back: 1 Pyo, 2 Dami, 3 Woo, 4 Jaeha, 5 Ho.
  - The brief asked for the masked lineup "so the numbers and heights read". With the respirators on, the numbers are the only way to tell the five apart (chapter 23 says so).
  - The chat left them out because the style guide's negative block bans numbers in art.
- **The fix:** Claude painted big black stencilled numbers on the front of each tabard, in the same place on both lineups. Nothing else changed.

## Now canon
