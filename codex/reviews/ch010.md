- **P01, Borro's size in the rift.** The script says that sitting, he is "as tall as a one-storey house". That can't be true of a 2.4 m giant, who sits about 1.5 m tall. The chat kept his canon size and let the low camera make him loom over Jaeha in the foreground, which is the right call.
- **P16, the window space.** The morning sky fills only the top 39% of the frame, not half, and Borro's head starts just below it. Five tries didn't clear more. The letterer must fit the five-line summon log above his head, narrower or smaller if needed.
- **P17 and P36, Jaeha beside Borro.** Jaeha is drawn about 10% tall.
  - **P17:**
    - **Measured:** from crown to soles, Jaeha is 0.93 of Borro on the page. His soles are at y 950 and Borro's at y 919, so he stands nearer the low camera.
    - **Assumed:** the horizon at the far river, about y 680. Correcting for the distance on that assumption, Jaeha is 0.82 of Borro, against the canon 0.74.
  - **P36:** all three stand at about Borro's depth, with soles at y 928–973. Against Borro:
    - Jaeha is 0.82 (canon 0.74);
    - Yerin is 0.70 (canon 0.65);
    - Nib is 0.44 (canon 0.44).
  - **Why they stay:** Jaeha's crown comes to Borro's collarbone instead of his chest, about half a Borro-head too high. Borro still reads as the giant, and both panels match their scripts. P36 already used its five tries, and the chat flagged it.
- **P23, Borro at the right edge.**
  - **Measured:** his eyes are only about 80 px above the far river, at y about 205. Jaeha's eyes sit just below that line, at y about 218.
  - **Assumed:** the horizon is at the far river, so the camera is at about Jaeha's eye height (1.66 m). On that assumption, Borro's eyes are about 1.8 m up instead of 2.25 m, so he is drawn about 20% short.
  - **Why it stays:** on the page his big head at the top edge, well above Seoa's at the door, reads as a giant standing close to the camera.
