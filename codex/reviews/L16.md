## What passes
- **The nave** (`drowned_cathedral/key.png`, `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md` 5c):
  - a vast Far Side Gothic nave half under water: paired pillar rows, side chapels and passages;
  - floating pews;
  - broken windows of pale coloured glass in abstract patterns;
  - bronze bells hanging silent from the vault on chains;
  - at the far end, the bronze double doors under the rose window, with the red lamp and one bell between them.
  - Two tiny figures wade with the water about knee-deep. Against them the pillars stand about 17 m to their capitals, under a 30 m vault.
- **The crypt** (`crypt.png`):
  - a low rib vault on squat piers;
  - plain stone tombs with their lids just above rising black water;
  - entrance steps at the front;
  - a half-drowned arch at the back right;
  - pale light from a breach in the vault.
- **The doors** (`doors.png`):
  - the closer view of the bronze double doors, each leaf with a ring;
  - the red lamp and the silent bell above them;
  - the rose window in pale abstract glass;
  - the sill under water.
- **The bell chamber** (`boss_chamber_bells/key.png`):
  - a round domed chamber flooded knee-deep, ringed with pillars, its oculus and clerestory slits lit;
  - bronze bells of every size;
  - a stone stair along the back-left wall to a small doorway in the back wall.
  - Against the two tiny figures at the stair's foot, the doorway's sill is about 6 to 7 m up, and the doorway is about 2.6 m high, as the design asks.
- **The ruined chamber** (`ruined.png`, from chapter 19):
  - the left half of the dome fallen, with rubble and fallen bells piled on the left;
  - shafts of daylight through the gap;
  - the stair gone;
  - exactly two ropes hanging from the same high doorway, still about 6 m up;
  - the bells on the right still hanging.
- **The two chamber views agree:** the same doorway, centred between the same pillars, and the same water.
