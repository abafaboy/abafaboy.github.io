## What passes
- **Front and turnaround:** they are what the redo asked for:
  - gaunt and bony, with the spine, shoulder blades and hip bones standing out under patchy, hairless grey hide blotched black;
  - small eyes leaking thin violet ash;
  - cracks along the spine leaking violet ash;
  - long yellow-brown chisel incisors (the first try's fanglike teeth were rejected);
  - thin crooked limbs with pink-grey claws;
  - a long hairless tail.
- **Details:**
  - the paired incisors;
  - the eye with ash wisping off it;
  - the spine cracks with ash coming out;
  - a clawed paw.
- **Swarm mother:** the same animal at 1.4 m, in side and front views:
  - a ridge of bony spurs along her back;
  - wide cracks down her spine pouring violet ash.

## Scale (Claude's card: `scale_card.png`)
The card stands the side views beside the standard 178 cm man on one baseline:
- **A rat** is 70 cm long plus its tail, and its hunched back reaches the man's knee (about 48 cm). This matches the chapter 6 script: "about 70 cm long, up to a person's knee".
- **The swarm mother** is 1.4 m long, and her back is just above the man's hip (about 1 m).
- **Fixed:** the asset brief said the swarm runs "at ankle height". That was wrong, and it is now "knee-high".

## Why swarm.png is redone
- **The rats are too small:** they reach about 20–25 cm on the hunter's boots, roughly ankle height, which is half the size of the approved rat. A swarm reference at the wrong size would teach every chapter 6 swarm panel to draw them half size.
- **The rest is good:** the tunnel, the density and the running poses.
