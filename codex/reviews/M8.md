## Size: canon now follows the sheet
- **The wings are drawn shorter than the design asked.** On every sheet the wingspan is about twice the body's length from beak tip to rump. The design asked for more than three times that: a 1.2 m body and a 4 m wingspan.
- **Scaled to a 4 m wingspan, the drawn wyvern would be about 2 m long and stand 2.2 m tall.** That is taller than a man, no longer "a big dog with wings", and it would not match the flock scene, where each body is about the size of the worker's torso.
- **The fix keeps the dog-sized body:** 1.2 m from beak tip to rump.
  - The wingspan is then about 2.5 m.
  - Standing upright with its neck raised, its head is about 1.4 m up, level with a man's chest.
- `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md`, the chapter 24 script and its PANEL_BRIEF_v2 rule now say "wings about 2.5 m".
