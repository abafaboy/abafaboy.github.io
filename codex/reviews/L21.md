## How to use it
- **Scale:**
  - the walkway is 6 m wide and the parapet about 0.9 m high;
  - a tetrapod is about 3 m, the size of a car;
  - the lighthouse is 12 m with a 2 m door;
  - the light tower is 25 m;
  - the market is about 200 m behind the neck.
- **Close shots at the lighthouse:** use `end.png`; never enlarge the distant lighthouse from a wide key.
- **Chapter 35's memory:** `busan_breakwater_flashback/key.png`. Amber belongs only to the fighters made of light (S28), which this key leaves out.
- **The crawlers:** match them to `characters_v2/brine_crawler/scale_card.png`.
