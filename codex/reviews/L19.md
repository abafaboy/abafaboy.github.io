## Why the pit key and `alarm.png` are redone
1. **The Warden is off-model.** L19 drew him from the written design, because M9 didn't exist yet. He now has his own sheet (`characters_v2/tide_warden/`, M9, awaiting review), and the two disagree:
   - **the beard:** grey and human in the pit key; on the sheet it is the long green kelp beard the design asks for;
   - **the coral armour:** less of it in the pit key than the sheet's red-and-white plates;
   - **the trident:** its break doesn't read in the pit key; on the sheet it lies broken in two.
   - The pit key is the reveal of chapter 26, so its giant must be the sheet's giant.
2. **The scale is off. Measured on the key:**
   - **The Warden:** kneeling, he is about 255 px tall.
   - **The engineers:** the two on the west catwalk are 41–44 px, at about the same depth, so he is only six engineers tall: about 10.5 m. Canon is 21 m kneeling, about 12 engineers. The script asks "the engineers are ants".
   - **The pit:** its floor is about 1,050 px across at his depth, only four times his height. With him at 21 m, the pit reads about 86 m across, against 150 m.
3. **The faults DONE.md lists:**
   - the tank terraces are flattened behind the glass, and their caps don't show the double hexagon and crescent;
   - the cranes don't read;
   - a second round port on the east wall could be mistaken for a second way out.
   - `alarm.png` is an edit of the key, so it inherits all of these.
