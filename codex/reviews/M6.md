## What passes

  - its hide plated with rough slabs of black basalt like broken paving, coarse dark bristles between them;

## Size: canon now follows the sheet
- **The turnaround's body is shorter than the design asked:** its side view is about 1.7 times as long as it is tall at the shoulder, against 2 times ("2.5 m at the shoulder, 5 m long"). Five tries all came out short, as with the drowned hound (M2).
- **The design also contradicted itself:** Borro is 2.4 m, so a 2.5 m shoulder would come only 10 cm above his head, not "a head above" it as the design, the chapter 15 script and its panel rule all say.
- **The fix keeps the drawing and the "head above Borro" rule:** the shoulder is now 2.8 m (the bristles reach about 2.95 m), which makes the drawn body about 4.7 m from nose to rump. It still reads as truck-sized. `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md`, the chapter 15 script and its PANEL_BRIEF_v2 rule changed with it.
