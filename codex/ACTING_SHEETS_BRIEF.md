# ACTING SHEETS BRIEF: body language and dialogue faces for the main cast (one Codex chat per entry)

**Why.** The owner's verdict on the last manhwa was that body positions, emotions and dialogue panels looked unnatural and AI-made. On 25 October he added: "remember about the faces and emotions and body language and dialog face movements", and "as much fan service and as beautiful characters as possible". The model sheets show each character standing neutral. These two extra sheets show how each one moves, talks and listens, so every chapter panel has an acting reference that is not a standing pose.

## Read first
- `README_START_HERE.md`.
- `MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md`: 6 and 6b (faces, bodies, acting; note the new Faces 8–10 on talking mouths), 10.1–10.4 (STYLE BLOCK, CLEAN-UP LINE, NEGATIVE BLOCK), 11 and 11b (including the new 11b.10 left/right rule).
- `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md`: your character's section and their snippet in section 8, word for word.

## References you may pass
- Your character's `characters_v2/<id>/front.png` and `expressions.png` (and `details.png` for props). Nothing else: no other character, no old `characters/` file, no real webtoon image.
- Add this sentence to every prompt: "The reference images show only the character's design (face, hair, clothes, colours, props). Do not copy their standing pose or front-facing angle: every pose and expression comes from the text."

## The two sheets (both 1536×1024, flat #E9E9EC background, no text, no labels)

1. **`acting.png`: six full-body acting poses** in one row or a 3×2 grid, at one scale on one baseline per row. Each pose is caught mid-action and uses a different camera angle (three-quarter front, profile, three-quarter back, slightly low or high). Follow 6b Bodies: weight on one leg, every hand has a job, the head turns and tilts, the body moves first. Draw exactly the poses listed in your entry, in that order.
2. **`talking.png`: eight heads and shoulders** in a 4×2 grid, each a different three-quarter or profile angle, facing left and right in turn (never all straight at the camera).
   - **Top row, talking:** the mouth is open in the middle of a word, shaped to the line (6b Faces 8); the brows, eyes and head angle change with the emotion; hands may come into the frame to gesture.
   - **Bottom row, listening:** the mouth is closed or reacting; the face shows the line landed (6b Faces 10).

Keep every lock on its correct side in every pose (11b.10).

## Checks
- Run 11 and 11b on both sheets; up to 3 tries per sheet. Check the side of every lock in every pose (11b.10), count fingers, and make sure no pose is a neutral standing pose.
- Rejects go to `characters_v2/_rejects/<id>_acting_<reason>.png`.

## Finish
- Save `acting.png` and `talking.png` in the character's folder, with their exact prompts and references in a new `acting_prompts.json` and a short `ACTING_DONE.md` in the same folder.
- Write only those four files and `_rejects/<id>_acting_*`. Another chat may be fixing the same character's other sheets at the same time, so never edit `prompts.json`, `DONE.md`, `card.json` or any other sheet.
- Then STOP.
