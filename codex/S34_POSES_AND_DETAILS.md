## Rules for every S34 sheet

1. **Adults only.** Lyra, Han Sora (27), Moon Ahin (22) and Yoo Bomi (21) are adults. Never Kang Seoa or Song Yerin.
2. **Attractive and flirty, never explicit.**
   - Beautiful faces, teasing expressions, confident poses (the S33 posing section: weight on one leg, three-quarter turns, hands that frame the figure, a line of action).
   - No nudity, nothing see-through, no wardrobe slips.
   - No sexual poses: no all-fours, no spread-legged sitting, no arched-back lying poses.
   - In every seated or lying pose her knees stay together, crossed or turned to one side.
3. **The camera.**
   - At eye level or at her shoulder height, the way 2.20 says.
   - Never below her waist aimed up, and never aimed at her chest, crotch or buttocks.
4. **Details are shown as costume pieces, not as body crops.**
   - Garments and jewellery are shown laid flat, like product photos, or on the whole figure.
   - No callout zooms in on her chest, crotch or buttocks.
   - Hands and feet are anatomy callouts (five digits each, nails, joints).
5. **The sheet.**
   - Each sheet is one 1536×1024 PNG on a plain light backdrop, labelled in English.
   - Outfits, sides and locks exactly as in the design sections:
     - Lyra's braid over her LEFT shoulder and her band on her LEFT thigh;
     - Sora's bandage on her RIGHT wrist and the new compass on her LEFT;
     - Ahin's slit up her LEFT leg and her baton at her RIGHT hip.
6. **Hands first.**
   - Crop every hand at 2x before you keep a sheet. Check the finger count, the thumb side, the joints, the grip and that the arm is attached.
   - Pass the closest file from `characters_v2/hands/` as a reference for each pose's hands.
7. **References.**
   - Use the character's approved sheets and her S33 sheets as references.
   - Never use a picture of a real person.
