### T1 · Jaeha at Gate 0912 (wide, 1536×1024)

**Recipe line.**
> Medium close-up, waist up, his body turned three-quarters toward the image's right, the camera slightly below his eye line. Key light: a cold white LED floodlight from the upper right of the image. Rim light: a thin cold white-cyan line along the left edge of his silhouette, from the gate behind him on the left. The upper-left third of the frame is calm and empty (dark night sky only) for a name card.

**Scene.**
> Night, at the bottom of a subway construction pit in a Seoul street: steel pile walls, scaffold stairs, a blue tarp, puddles on wet concrete. Far behind him on the left, below the calm area, simplified and lower in contrast, a door-height black-violet slit hangs between steel struts, with a cold white-cyan rim. In the foreground at the right edge, cropped by the frame, the black-jacketed shoulder of a big hunter and the chipped edge of a black tower shield. The pit is shown at about half detail: big shapes and a few telling props, with detail fading toward the edges. It is simplified and lower in contrast than Jaeha.
>
> Kang Jaeha, 24, a lean young Korean man: a narrow oval face, defined cheekbones, a straight nose, heavy upper eyelids with faint tired lines under dark brown eyes. Messy near-black hair with navy-grey shine bands, a long fringe falling over his LEFT eye. A yellow pencil behind his RIGHT ear. A heather-grey hoodie under a safety-orange trap-tester vest with two silver reflective stripes and a small white square patch with a black block letter T on the right chest; that T is the only lettering anywhere in the image. Black fingerless gloves. His left chest pocket is empty: he holds his battered black notebook, closed, low against his left chest with his right hand.
>
> Acting: his public face, a polite, slightly bowed, tired smile; but his eyes are sharp and slide sideways, back toward the gate on the left, calculating.

## How to build each prompt

There are two images: T1 and T2, both in V2. Each prompt is, in this order:
1. the **V2 STYLE BLOCK** (`MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md` 10.1), exactly;
2. the panel's **recipe line** (above);
3. the panel's **scene** (above);
4. the **NEGATIVE BLOCK** (`MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md` 10.3), exactly.

**Tries.**
- Up to 3 tries per image. Keep the best.
- Put the others in `style_test_v2/_rejects/` with a one-line reason each.
- Check each kept image against `MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md` section 11.

## Deliverables (folder `style_test_v2/`)

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `T1_V2.png`, `T2_V2.png` | The two panels, at native size |
| `PROMPTS.json` | The exact prompts used for every kept image and every reject |
| `REPORT.md` | See below |

**`REPORT.md` says, for each panel:**
- what worked and what did not, honestly;
- its results on the 11 checks in style guide section 11;
- which locks failed (ear notch, fang side, fringe side, and so on);
- anything in the V2 STYLE BLOCK you think should change, and why. Do not change the block yourself.

## Then

- **If both panels pass,** go straight on to `MODEL_SHEETS_BRIEF.md` step 1: Jaeha's and Nib's sheets. Stop after `characters_v2/READY_JAEHA_NIB.md`.
- **If either panel still fails check 2, 3 or 11 after 3 tries** (it looks glossy, rendered or flat-lit), do not start the sheets. Write what failed in `REPORT.md` and stop.
- Do not start other characters, location keys or chapter panels.
