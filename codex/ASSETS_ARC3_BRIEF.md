# ASSETS BRIEF, ARCS 3-4: characters, monsters, places and props for chapters 13-33 (one Codex chat per entry)

**Why now.** Arc 2's sheets (`ASSETS_ARC2_BRIEF.md`) cover chapters 6-12. Arcs 3 and 4 (chapters 13-33) bring Han Sora's full sheet, Nib's seal 3/5 armour, Oh Minseok, Auntie Geum, the Arc 4 crew and Kanghan's staff, five monsters and about twenty places. These must exist before those chapters are drawn. Do the entries in the order of the table below: it follows the chapters.

The designs are in `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md`: sections 2.2 (Nib's seal 3/5), 2.16 (Han Sora), 2.17 (Auntie Geum), 2.18 (the Arcs 3-4 cast), 4c (monsters), 5c (places), 6 (props) and the new prompt snippets at the end of section 8. The scripts' `continuity` blocks repeat the details per chapter.

**Read first:**
- `README_START_HERE.md`;
- `MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md`: the V2 STYLE BLOCK, the CLEAN-UP LINE, the NEGATIVE BLOCK, sections 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 11b and 13;
- `PANEL_BRIEF_v2.md` section 4b: the owner's strict rules on proportions, scale and perspective. They apply to every sheet;
- your entry's design in `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md`.

## Character and monster sheets
Follow `MODEL_SHEETS_BRIEF.md` sections 1-3 exactly: 1536×1024 PNGs on flat light grey #E9E9EC, no text or labels; the front view first, checked, then passed as the reference for every other sheet of that character; `card.json` and `prompts.json`; rejects in `characters_v2/_rejects/<id>_<reason>.png`; `DONE.md` when the entry is finished.

**Scale on every sheet.** Draw a faint grey silhouette of a 178 cm man (Jaeha's height, no details) beside each turnaround and each monster view, on the same baseline. For the Tide Warden, the silhouette stands at his knee.

**Fan service** (owner's direction): Han Sora is drawn beautiful and confident in every view, nothing explicit, no view aimed at her chest. Dami's daughter is a child: simple and cute, nothing else.

## Location keys
Follow `LOCATION_KEYS_BRIEF.md` "Rules" exactly: 1536×1024, V2, the key light named in the design, no people except tiny figures for scale where asked, no text (blank signs, #00FF00 screens), `key.png` plus the views listed, `notes.md` (layout and light for later panels), `prompts.json`, `DONE.md`. Every key must read at the right scale: put one or two tiny people in each `key.png`, correctly proportioned and on the floor plane.

## Never
- Nothing from the old `characters/` or `chapters/` folders, and no real webtoon images.
- No text anywhere in the art (the letterer adds every label, sign and screen).
- No compressed bodies or wrong scale (`PANEL_BRIEF_v2.md` 4b): Claude rejects them.
