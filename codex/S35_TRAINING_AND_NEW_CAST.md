## Rules for every image
- **Adults only.** Every woman here is an adult, drawn with adult faces and adult proportions. Never Kang Seoa (15); Song Yerin keeps her covered look and is never in these sheets.
- **Fan service as the owner wants it:** beautiful faces, attractive athletic and curvy figures, the revealing training outfits as designed, confident and flirty poses, a light healthy sheen of sweat where the pose is mid-workout. Make every woman as attractive and flirty as these limits allow.
- **Camera angles: use the whole range** (the owner, 29 October: "why are different camera angles not allowed? what about camera from the bottom or from above?"). Low-angle hero shots from knee or hip height looking up at the whole figure; high angles from above looking down at her on a mat, a bench or the floor; three-quarter back views looking over her shoulder; tilted (Dutch) angles in action. The pose list says which angle each image uses; vary them so no two images in a set share one.
- **Training poses as real training** (the owner: "what about training poses like squats or stretching poses?"): squats, lunges, deadlift stance, push-ups, planks, splits, hamstring, quad and side stretches, yoga poses, all drawn with correct form and a strong line of action.
- **The only limits:** no nudity and no see-through fabric; shorts and leggings stay opaque and sit as designed (never riding up); nothing sexual (no sexual acts, no sexual poses or expressions); her head and most of her figure always in the frame (in a back view her face may turn in profile or over her shoulder), so no shot is framed on the chest, the crotch or the buttocks alone (no crotch or chest close-up, no up-skirt angle, no shot of only her hips). Squats, bends, ducks and dodges may be seen from any side, directly behind included (the owner: "dont leave them out they could be important ... especially for dodging scenes and etc where the character has to bend down"), drawn as the real movement: the whole figure from head to feet, her head visible, and what she dodges or the floor and space around her in the frame.
- **Format.** Sheets: 1536×1024 PNG on flat light grey #E9E9EC, no text, labels or rulers. Single poses (`poses_training_a.png` to `_f.png`): 1024×1536 portrait (or 1536×1024 landscape where the pose says), ONE figure filling the frame (full figure or knees up, as the pose says; never small in the frame), a simple soft background that suits the pose (a training hall, a rooftop, a range) kept light and uncluttered. The STYLE BLOCK and CLEAN-UP LINE in every prompt.
- **References,** at most four per image:
  - existing women: the approved `front.png` is always reference 1 (face, hair, body, proportions); then the sheets the entry names. Change only the clothes and the pose;
  - new women: one image from `style_test_v2/acting/` as the style anchor and, for the first image only, `characters_v2/han_sora/front.png` as a STYLE reference for line and colour (never copy her face, hair or outfit). After her `front.png` is kept, it is reference 1 for every other image of her.
  - Never pass anything from a `_rejects/` folder or the retired `characters/` folder, and no outside pictures.
- **Hands and arms first** (README 28 October; `PANEL_BRIEF_v2.md` 4c): before you keep an image, crop every hand at 2x and check the finger count, the thumb side, the joints, the grip, and that every arm is attached and no longer than it should be.
- **Faces:** never blank. Use the teasing faces of S33 (half-lidded crescent eyes, a glance from under the lashes, a lopsided smirk, a light blush, a wink, a finger at the lips) or the character's own acting line below.
- **Tries:** up to 3 per image. Rejects go to `characters_v2/_rejects/s35_<name>_<try>_<reason>.png`. Save every kept image as an ordinary file the moment it passes, so the owner can look at it while you draw the next.

- **Low angles** are dynamic and far enough away that the whole figure fits against the sky or the enemy; never under a skirt.
- **Bends and dodges from behind are wanted** (poses i to l): seen from directly behind or three-quarters behind, as the real movement, with the whole figure, her head and what she dodges in the frame.
- **Format:** 1024×1536 portrait unless the pose says landscape; a simple soft background (training hall, rooftop, arena) kept light.
