## 1. Legs that work like real legs (every image in S36, and every pose sheet after it)

### 1a. What went wrong in S35 (Claude's review)
The same five poses failed for all four women, because one shared pose line produced the same mistake each time:
- **Guard stance and the hero shot facing the giant (`fight_ref_a`, `fight_ref_c`):** both legs locked straight in a wide upside-down V, feet two and a half shoulder widths apart, toes splayed out or twisted onto the tips. That is a pin-up pose. Nobody fights, shoots or stands ready like that.
- **Overhead side stretch (`fight_ref_b`):** no side bend at all; she stands upright with her arms up.
- **Spinning kick (`fight_ref_d`):** the standing leg straight and up on its toes like a ballet relevé, the body upright, the kicking leg coming out of the back of the hip. No real kick looks like that.
- **Pull-up (`fight_ref_g`):** a dead hang with the head below the bar, not a pull-up.
- Also: Lyra's bend-back dodge (`fight_ref_l`: one shin folds back under the thigh), Lyra's side stretch (`poses_training_b`: barely bends), Moon Ahin's plié (`poses_training_d`: knees far outside the toes), Han Sora's quad stretch (`poses_training_f`: the bent knee swings forward of the standing knee) and Yoo Bomi's overhead stretch (`poses_training_f`: knees knocking inward).
- These passed and stay: the leaps (e), landing crouches (f), lunges (h), the duck under the claw (i), the forward folds (j), the sweeps (k), the other bend-back dodges (l), the squats, the kneeling aim, the bench, the cat stretch, the straddle and the side split.

### 1b. The pose sketch comes first
Korean webtoon artists pose a figure for the panel, set the camera, and draw over it. We do the same with a drawn mannequin (no 3D program):
1. **Draw the pose sketch first,** saved as `characters_v2/<name>/_pose/<image stem>_pose.png`: a plain mid-grey artist's mannequin, smooth and faceless, no hair and no clothes, with a ball at every joint (shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles), adult female proportions about 8 heads tall, in the exact pose and from the exact camera angle of the final image, on flat light grey #E9E9EC with a floor line and a soft cast shadow so every contact with the ground shows. Same size and framing as the final image. A weapon, if the pose has one, as a plain grey shape.
2. **Check the pose sketch** against the leg checklist (1c) and the form notes for its pose (Part B or the Part A entry). Redraw it until every line passes (up to 3 tries).
3. **Then draw the character.** Pass the kept pose sketch as reference 1 and write: "Image 1 is the pose guide. Copy its pose exactly: the angle of every joint, the stance width, where each foot touches the ground, the lean of the body and the camera angle. Do not copy its look: draw <name> from the other images." Her `front.png` is reference 2.
4. **Check the final image** against the checklist again; its legs must match the kept pose sketch. If they drift, try again.

Keep every kept pose sketch as an ordinary file (Claude reviews them beside the finals). Pose sketches do not count toward the 3 tries of the final image.

### 1c. The leg checklist (every line must be yes before you keep an image)
1. **Knee over toes:** each knee points the same way as the toes of that foot. No knee bends sideways or backwards, and no foot is twisted away from its knee.
2. **Feet on the ground:** every foot that carries weight stands on the floor plane in perspective, flat or on the ball of the foot exactly as the pose says: never floating, sinking or tipped onto its side.
3. **Balance:** the body's weight (about the navel) sits over the feet that carry it or between them; she would not tip over. A raised leg is always balanced by the upper body leaning the other way.
4. **Stance width as the pose says,** measured in her own shoulder widths. A wide, straight-legged upside-down V is never a fighting, ready or shooting stance.
5. **Soft knees:** in every fighting, ready, shooting or landing pose both knees are bent. A leg is straight only where the pose says (the kicking leg at impact, the standing leg of an arabesque, a forward fold, a split, a hang).
6. **Leg length:** thigh (hip joint to knee) a little longer than the shin (knee to ankle); both legs the same length; the legs about half her height. A low camera may lengthen the nearer leg a little in perspective, never by half again.
7. **Hips follow the legs:** in a lunge the hips face forward; in a kick the kicking hip rolls over on top of the standing hip; in a split the legs leave the body at the hip joints, not at the waist.
8. **Feet and ankles:** an ankle at every foot, five toes on a bare foot, each foot about one head long, the heel and arch where they belong for the side we see.

### 1d. Real form, in words (sources)
- **Fighting stance:** feet about shoulder width apart and staggered (the front heel in line with the rear toes), front toes pointing at the opponent, rear foot angled in, weight on the balls of the feet, knees bent, chin down, hands up. ([FightCamp](https://blog.joinfightcamp.com/training/how-to-do-the-perfect-kickboxing-stance/))
- **Roundhouse kick:** the knee chambers straight up toward the target, never out to the side; the standing foot turns until its toes point away from the target (about 135 degrees from the kick's line); the trunk drops and leans away as the leg rises, and she sees the target over the kicking-side shoulder; the kicking-side arm swings down behind her and the other arm guards her face; the shin or instep strikes. ([Stadion](https://stadionbooks.com/errors-in-basic-kicks-roundhouse-kick/))
- **Archery stance:** feet about shoulder width, side-on to the target (perpendicular to the shooting line, or opened up to 45 degrees), hips facing the way the feet face, weight toward the balls of the feet, upright, no lean, no hollow back. ([Archery 360](https://archery360.com/2021/03/03/form-breakdown-stance/))
- **Plié:** each kneecap points exactly where its toes point; all the turn-out comes from the hips, never from the knee or ankle. ([Dance Advantage](https://www.danceadvantage.net/leg-alignment-refresher/))
- **Standing quad stretch:** stand tall; hold the ankle of the bent leg with the same-side hand; both knees close together; tailbone tucked, hips square; no arched lower back. ([Julie Lohre](https://julielohre.com/quad-stretch/))
- **Pull-up (top position):** chin over the bar, elbows bent and pulled down beside the ribs, shoulder blades drawn down and together, legs straight and together slightly in front of the body, feet off the floor.
- **Standing side bend:** feet hip width and flat, legs straight but not locked, the hips push a little one way while the upper body curves the other way in one long C from the hip to the fingertips.
