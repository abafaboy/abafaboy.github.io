# CHARACTER DESIGNS v2: the rebuilt cast, monsters, locations and props

**For Codex.** These designs come from the approved story (`STORY_BIBLE_v4.md`). Spellings are in `NAME_SHEET.md`; the look of the whole series is in `MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

**Retired.** Every old sheet in `characters/` is retired: Taeo, the old green Nib in the pink wrap, and the rest. Never pass an old sheet as a reference.

**Approved.** The owner approved all 15 of Claude's design proposals (section 7) on 25 October. Everything in this file is now the design to draw.

**Clarified the same day,** after Claude's strict review of the first model sheets (`REVIEW_FIXES.md`):
- Nib's goblin face and his belted, sleeveless cloak;
- Kett's goblin face;
- plain shoes for Yerin and Bomi, and Bomi's 90 cm bow;
- Seoa's knee-length skirt and unlit keychain;
- the husk's simplified mannequin body;
- the lizards' heads, hides and teeth;
- the knight's plain helm and sigil badge;
- Maud is a woman;
- the gate opens in the pit's far wall.

**Fan service (the owner's direction, 25 October).** In his words: "adding overall slightly more fan service is good for retention and hype", and later that night: "as much fan service and as beautiful characters as possible. revealing clothes when possible."
- **Who:** the clearly adult women:
  - Lyra (2.10);
  - Yoo Bomi (2.7);
  - Moon Ahin (2.11, drawn now so she is in the owner's sheets);
  - the Black Hound crossbow hunter (2.5, hunter 5).
- **Never:** Kang Seoa, who is 15. Song Yerin also keeps her approved, covered look: her design reads young (small, round-faced, freckled), and fan service never goes to anyone who could read as under 18. She stays the scared rookie, drawn cute, never sexy.
- **What it means:**
  - beautiful faces and attractive, curvy, athletic figures;
  - the revealing outfits as designed, and confident poses;
  - glamour intro panels;
  - comedic reactions from the men around them: Jaeha flustered, Nib unimpressed.
- **What it never means:**
  - nudity, or sexual acts or poses;
  - camera angles aimed up a skirt or at the crotch;
  - anything explicit.

  YouTube may age-restrict a video whose images make the body the focal point (`ledger/PUBLISHING.md`).

**Colours** are given as base / shadow hex codes for the flat colour and the one hard cel shadow.

### 4b.5 The Hush in the Call (ch 9)

## 4c. Monsters, Arcs 3-4 (chapters 13-33)

Every boss here is a hushed hero of the Far Side (a Hush crack leaking violet ash #6B4C9A); small monsters are Hush-spawn.

### 4c.1 The basalt boar (ch 15, D-grade boss)
A hushed boar the size of a truck: 2.5 m at the shoulder, 5 m long. Its hide is plated with slabs of black basalt with rough edges like broken paving, coarse dark bristles between the slabs; two curved tusks of glassy black obsidian; small red-brown eyes; a small violet Hush crack on its brow. It charges in straight lines and lowers its head three strides before impact (its tell). Its shoulder is a head higher than Borro's head.

### 4c.2 The Drowned Bell-Ringer (ch 17-19, B-grade boss)
A hushed giant 6 m tall: a body like drowned grey stone wrapped in rotting dark-blue robes and rusted chains; long arms, huge hands; in place of a head, a cracked bronze cathedral bell green with age, a violet Hush crack down its side leaking ash, darkness inside its mouth. Its hammer is a bell clapper the size of a tree trunk. When it tolls, a ring of force bursts out a step beyond its own reach; inside its arm's length the force passes over you. Borro reaches its waist; Jaeha its knee.

### 4c.3 Canyon wyverns (ch 24, Hush-spawn)
Small cliff-dwelling wyverns: a body the size of a large dog (1.2 m long without the tail), a 4 m wingspan; slate-grey leathery skin streaked with rust; a long whip tail ending in a hooked barb; a narrow skull with a beaky snout full of needle teeth; pale clouded eyes; Hush cracks leaking violet ash along the wing bones. They nest in crevices and dive in flocks of ten to twenty, shrieking like tearing metal.

### 4c.4 The Hushed Tide Warden (ch 25-28, the kept boss of the Han River gate)
A sea-giant, 30 m tall if he stood, kept kneeling and sagging in chains. Pale blue-grey skin like a drowned man's, with Hush cracks leaking violet ash; armour of red and white coral grown like plate, crusted with barnacles and shells; a crown of coral branches; a long beard of kelp; a broad, noble, sorrowful face. His eyes are deep sea-green, enormous and ancient (closed until ch 27). Chains as thick as tree trunks run from steel cuffs on his wrists, neck and waist to anchors in the pit walls; the chain links are matte black factory steel with one small stamped mark (the same mark as the knight's chains in ch 1 and 4: a shape, never letters). Dozens of steel pipes are driven into his body through collars bolted into the coral; smoky violet light flows out through their glass sections. A broken trident lies at his knees. Kneeling, he is as tall as a seven-storey building. His voice reaches only Far Side people, as words; humans hear a deep hum.

### 4c.5 The thing under the Busan sea gate (ch 33, never shown whole)
Only ever a curve of dark hide too big to see the end of, and the edge of something like a fin or a hand, sliding past a crack in the sealed lid on the seabed. It is the Deep Lantern (4d.3).

## 4d. Monsters, Arc 5 (chapters 34-40)

All Hush-spawn are eyeless or dull-eyed and leak violet ash from their cracks; bosses are fallen Far Side heroes.

### 4d.1 Brine crawlers (ch 34, 39-40, Hush-spawn)
Dog-sized things out of the sea: a crab's front half (a flat slick black-grey carapace 80 cm long, two serrated claws, six spiny legs) and an eel's rear half (a 1 m tail that thrashes and slaps); a round mouth ringed with needle teeth under the carapace; no eyes; violet cracks along the carapace leaking ash; weed and barnacles. In a break they come up the breakwater in hundreds, clicking.

### 4d.2 The Reef Hermit (ch 35, a small boss, new kind)
A hermit crab 4 m tall whose shell is a sunken Far Side stone shrine: a small domed temple of grey stone with carved pillars, crusted with coral and barnacles, a bronze bell still hanging inside that clangs when it moves. The shrine is old and too tight for it: cracks run through the dome, and bits crumble off when it moves, so it is always after a sturdier shell (it covets Borro's). Out of the shrine's doorway: two huge armoured claws (the RIGHT one bigger), spindly armoured legs, two eye-stalks with dull violet eyes, a violet Hush crack across its carapace. Its weak point is the soft, pale pink-grey curled abdomen hidden inside the shrine; it only shows when the Hermit crawls out to take a bigger shell.

### 4d.3 The Deep Lantern (ch 39-40, the Busan sea gate's boss; Oriel, a hushed hero)
A leviathan about 150 m long, rising out of the sea gate: a whale-like body with a broad blunt head, grey-blue hide #3E4C5E / #25303D crusted with coral, barnacles and kelp; rusted steel plates of the old lid stuck in the hide of her back like scales, from ten years of pushing against it. At the front, two huge webbed hands, each 15 m across, four long fingers and a thumb. From the top of her head a long curved crest-spine rises 30 m, and at its tip hangs the great lantern: a cage of pale bone-white coral, 12 m, round a burning core of light.
- **Hushed:** the lantern burns violet and pulses; its light lures (people who look at it go glassy-eyed and walk toward the water); a Hush crack runs down her brow between the eyes, leaking violet ash at its base under the lantern; her small ancient eyes open dull violet.
- **Freed (ch 40):** the lantern flares pure white and sweeps the sea like a lighthouse beam; for a moment her face is gentle and her eyes are sea-grey; then she sinks and dissolves into light.
- Her real name, heard once: Oriel. She guided her people's boats home through storms.
