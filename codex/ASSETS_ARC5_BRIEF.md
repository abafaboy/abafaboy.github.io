# ASSETS BRIEF, ARC 5: characters, monsters, places and props for chapters 34-40 (one job per entry)

**Why now.** Arc 5, "The Wall of Busan" (chapters 34-40), brings the fourth summon (Pim), Jaeha's training look, the ch 35 flashback cast, three monsters (the brine crawlers, the Reef Hermit and the Deep Lantern), three glamour looks for the beach and the pool, the Busan places and a props sheet. These must exist, and be approved by Claude, before those chapters are drawn. Do the entries in the order of the table: it follows the chapters. This is **Wave C**; it comes after Waves A and B (`ASSETS_ARC2_BRIEF.md`, `ASSETS_ARC3_BRIEF.md`).

The designs are in `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md`: the scale table (section 1), 2.19 (Pim), 2.20 (the Arc 5 cast and looks, including the flashback cast), 4d (monsters), 5d (places), 6 (props). The scripts' `continuity` blocks (`scripts_v2/ch034.yaml` to `ch040.yaml`) repeat the details per chapter, and `ARC5_BEATS.md` tells the story.

**Read first:**
- `README_START_HERE.md`;
- `MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md`: the V2 STYLE BLOCK, the CLEAN-UP LINE, the NEGATIVE BLOCK, sections 5, 6, 8, 9, 11, 11b and 13;
- `PANEL_BRIEF_v2.md` section 4b: the owner's strict rules on proportions, scale and perspective. They apply to every sheet;
- your entry's design in `CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md`.

**References.** Attach the approved sheets named in each entry, and one or two images from `style_test_v2/acting/` as the style anchor, to every generation. The approved art is on the `msgf-art` branch (see its `README.md`). Never use anything from a `_rejects/` folder.

## Character and monster sheets
Follow `MODEL_SHEETS_BRIEF.md` sections 1-3 exactly: 1536×1024 PNGs on flat light grey #E9E9EC, no text or labels; the front view first, checked, then passed as the reference for every other sheet of that character; `card.json` and `prompts.json`; rejects in `characters_v2/_rejects/<id>_<reason>.png`; `DONE.md` when the entry is finished.

**Scale (changed 27 October).** Leave the 178 cm silhouette out of every sheet and line-up: draw the character or creature alone, filling the height. Claude adds the silhouette at the exact ratio after review (`README_START_HERE.md`, 27 October). Scene images (a swarm, a pack, a creature beside a building) keep a person drawn into the scene for scale.

**Fan service** (owner's direction): Lyra, Moon Ahin and Han Sora are drawn beautiful and confident in every view; nothing explicit, no view aimed at the chest or up a skirt; every view at eye level. Children (the evacuee family's kids) are drawn simply, nothing else.
