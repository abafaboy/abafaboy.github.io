PLACE AND SEASON: Aldcrown, the royal capital in the warm south, in early winter (this replaces the summer-in-Greyhollow note above): pale winter sun, morning frost, breath steaming. The Lowmarket is the city's slum: crowded, leaning timber houses along the river below the white city walls, washing lines, mud, chimney smoke and the black smoke of brick kilns. The aqueduct is a long line of tall, old stone arches marching across the valley from wooded hills into the Lowmarket; one arch in the middle had fallen, and it is being rebuilt with fresh red brick inside a timber frame and scaffolding. Under the city run very old vaulted brick tunnels with a stone water channel down the middle.
This is a BUILD chapter: busy, hopeful and warm, with comedy, ending on an uneasy note underground.
