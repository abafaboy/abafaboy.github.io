# My Summons Go First, Part One: chapter beats 1–100

> **RETIRED on 25 October 2026.** These beats belong to the old Yoon Taeo story. Canon is now `STORY_BIBLE_v4.md`. Kept for history only.

Canon was defined in `STORY_BIBLE.md` (also retired).

Each chapter lists:
- its beats, in order;
- **HOOK**: the final panel;
- the threads it carries: **Issue**, **Plant** (P-numbers from the bible's ledger), **Laugh**, **Power**, and **₩** (the price tag).

A script may add small beats. It may not skip a listed beat or move a plant without updating the ledger.

**Timeline.** Chapter 1 is a Tuesday in early spring. Part One covers about 14 months.
