# Judge: chapter 2, Who's in Charge
**Overall: 7/10**

| Art | Storytelling | Lettering | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7/10 | 7.5/10 | 7/10 | 7/10 |

This is a properly built chapter. A ten-page cold open lands on the title drop ("Um... summons go first?" / "They always do."), then the logo card. The fight is won by observation, not power, and each of the two new skills costs Jaeha blood. There is a real bonding beat between operator and summon, a five-minute clock that keeps ticking down on screen, and a last-panel reveal that turns the boss into backstory. Nib walks off with the chapter: dry, competent and, at last, shaken. The art backs him with confident camera work and a colour script that does real storytelling. What keeps it below Originals level is that the avoidable slips sit at the worst places. The chapter's first balloon points at the wrong speaker. The final reveal draws the "giant" barely taller than a man beside Nib. A chibi gag sits between the cliffhanger and "To be continued". And in the middle, the system twice hands Jaeha the exact skill he needs at the exact moment he needs it, which quietly undercuts a lead whose whole premise is that he survives by thinking. Fix the bookends, make him earn one of those skills, and this is an 8.

## Strengths
- **The fight is a puzzle the reader solves alongside Jaeha.** It runs in order:
  - the notebook line ends in "LEFT SHOULDER?" (page_18);
  - Link confirms the crack (P29);
  - he counts the tell (P32, whose cyan sweep arcs and ring on the pauldron are the best single device in the chapter);
  - the plate dips (P33), Nib ducks (P34) and the first wound lands (P35).

  The on-screen field-form clock (05:00, 03:31, 02:14, 01:20, 00:40) gives the whole chapter a pulse with no exposition.
- **Nib is a fully formed character within a page.** Four lines give him a voice:
  - "Then we do the same job. Mine paid better." (P09)
  - "They always do." (P13)
  - "Stop shouting. I can hear you thinking now." (P31)
  - "Strange man. Healer, stop shaking and fix him." (P48)

  His turn from deadpan to shock (P44, then P47's eye close-up) earns the cliffhanger. Jaeha's "Because you're the one who can actually hurt it" (P46) is the right answer for this lead: practical, not sentimental. P07 is a textbook character-intro splash, name card included.
- **Polished, consistent production.**
  - Jaeha's pencil, fringe and both T patches are right in every panel I checked.
  - The knight's nine-tower sigil and cracked left pauldron hold throughout, P11 to P60.
  - The system windows share one clean design.
  - The colour script carries meaning: red for the knight, amber for Link and Carry, mint for healing, and an orange flash for the two near-misses (P05, P34).

## Problems, most damaging first
1. **[page_01, P01]** **The first balloon points at the knight.** The chapter's first line, "You're in charge? Then why are you the one bleeding?", has its tail aimed at the knight's cropped, red-glowing helmet at top left. Nib is centre-right, so a reader's first impression is that the boss is talking. Jaeha is not in the panel either, so P04 (Nib dragging *both* of them out from under the blade) is the first we learn he was there. Fix: re-aim the tail at Nib's head, and widen or recompose P01 so Jaeha is on the floor beside Yerin. That also earns the line's "you".
2. **[page_44, no-art]** **A chibi gag deflates the cliffhanger.** The chibi tissue gag sits between "THAT KNIGHT WAS MY COMMANDER" (P60) and "TO BE CONTINUED" (page_45), so the chapter's best reveal is instantly played for laughs. (The index also labels this page "no art", though it holds a full illustration.) Fix: run P60 straight into "TO BE CONTINUED", and move the chibi below the end card as a post-credits bonus or cut it.
3. **[page_20, P26] and [page_29, P39]** **Both new skills arrive on demand.** LINK pops up the instant Jaeha thinks "I need to see what Nib sees", and the narration even says "the window answers". CARRY pops up the instant Nib is cornered. Twice in one chapter the system solves the problem, which is a story-level leak for a hero who "survives by thinking". Fix: let at least Link be triggered by something he *does*. P26 already shows him reaching for an amber thread: have him notice it and grab it, then let the window appear *afterwards*, naming what he did. Cut "And the window answers."
4. **[page_42, P58]** **The knight shrinks at the climax.** He stands a few metres behind Nib but is drawn shorter on the page than Nib himself. By the floor perspective he comes out under two metres tall, against the "four and a half metres" the narration set up at P16. This is the moment Nib names the Ninth Wall, and the giant looks man-sized. Fix: either redraw the knight so he fills the background with Nib's head no higher than his knee, or bring Nib right up to camera and crop the knight at the waist so he overflows the frame.
5. **[page_21, P27]** **The Link point of view is narrated, not shown.** The right half of the split reads as an ordinary low-angle shot of the knight, with nothing that says "through Nib's eyes". Without the narrator, P27 to P29 read as Jaeha simply looking. Fix: give the POV half a frame cue, such as:
   - an amber vignette or lens edge matching the link thread;
   - Nib's knife hand or scarf at the bottom edge;
   - an eyeline at rubble level;
   - or a one-line window: [LINK: NIB].
6. **[page_23, P32] and [page_25, P34]** **Link speech looks the same as Jaeha's private thoughts.**
   - "NOT YET. WAIT FOR THE SHOULDER." uses the same black caption box as his inner monologue (page_01 P02, page_18, page_21 P28).
   - "NOW!" then switches to a fuzzy whisper balloon, the same one he uses to answer the system in P40.

   The reader can't tell what Nib hears from what Jaeha only thinks. Fix: give the Link voice its own balloon (for example an amber-edged caption echoing the link light) and use it for every link order.
7. **[page_26, P35]** **The SFX covers the key action.** "SHRAKKK" runs across the exact spot where Nib's knives open the seam under the arm, which is the chapter's first real wound. Fix: shrink and re-angle the SFX across the knight's lower armour (bottom left), so the hands, the blades and the violet spray are clear.
8. **[page_28, P38]** **Nib's asymmetric marks slip in three panels.**
   - P38: the mask is flipped. Seen from behind, it peeks out on his right while the V-notched ear is correctly on his left. Fix: show the mask edge on the left, beside the notched ear, as P60 does correctly.
   - **[page_09, P12]**: the V-notch is on his right ear (seen from behind, face turned right).
   - **[page_32, P44]**: he shows a fang on each side instead of one on the left.
9. **[page_06, P08]** **Text breaks out of the first balloon.** The "I" of "I'M" and the full stop after "TRAP-TESTER" cross the outline, and the balloon has no tail. Fix: widen the balloon or reflow it to four lines, and add a tail to Jaeha (or merge it with the "I walk in first" balloon below).
10. **[page_04, P06] and [page_29, P39]** **System windows hide Jaeha's face.** Both big windows sit over his head, so when his summon is named and when he is offered Carry, we see a vest and a hand, not a face. P40's eye close-up rescues the second moment; nothing rescues the first. Fix: slide the windows up or sideways so his face reads, or add a small reaction inset.
11. **[page_21, P28], [page_07, P10], [page_40, P55], [page_36, P51]** **The narration repeats the page, and drifts from the art twice.**
    - P28 restates the caption almost word for word.
    - P10 and P55 restate the balloons.
    - P51 repeats P49's narration (hands trembling, the light keeps coming anyway).
    - **[page_26, P36]** says the knight "staggers", but he stands planted, clutching his side.
    - **[page_28, P38]** says "Nib is still landing", but he is in a set wide crouch.

    Fix: spend these slots on subtext, the way the best lines already do ("the only rule anyone ever gave him", "never been allowed to do on a raid", "the way he counted the lizard's bites"). For P36, draw a real stagger or change the word.
12. **[page_27, P37], [page_32, P43], [page_35, P50]** **Yerin drifts off-model.** She is never freckled in the main panels, though freckles are part of her design. Her bag also jumps to her right hip in P50, after sitting on her left in P37, P43 and P55. Fix: add the freckles, which are cheap and keep her from reading as a generic heroine, and keep the bag on one side.

## To gain one point
1. **Fix the bookends.**
   - Re-aim the P01 tail and put Jaeha in that frame.
   - Redraw P58 so the knight is a giant.
   - End on P60, then "TO BE CONTINUED", with the chibi after it or gone.
   - P60 would also gain from Nib lowering or sheathing his knives before his old commander, which shows "commander" without a word.
2. **Make Jaeha earn Link, and give it an identity.** Trigger it with his action (grabbing the amber thread) rather than his wish. Mark the P27 point of view with an amber frame, and give link speech its own amber balloon (P32, P34).
3. **A clean-up pass:**
   - the P38 mask;
   - the P35 SFX;
   - the P08 balloon;
   - the windows over Jaeha's face (P06, P39);
   - the narration lines that restate balloons.
