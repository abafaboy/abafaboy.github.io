# Judge: chapter 9, Not Yet, Operator
**Overall: 7/10**

| Art | Hands and arms | Storytelling | Lettering | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.5/10 | 7/10 | 7.5/10 | 6.5/10 | 7/10 |

The script carries this chapter, and it is good. A trap-tester writes the Far Side a job advert, gets dismissed as a merchant, reads the fine print and wins on the truth. The callbacks land: "You were standing in front of someone", "Promise you'll come back", and "Not yet, Operator", which turns a refusal into a promise. The countdown cuts cleanly between the black water and the room. The art is polished, Jaeha is on-model in almost every panel, the hands are mostly clean, and the bell spreads are the best images in the chapter. What holds it at a 7 is assembly, not ideas. Timer windows sit on Jaeha's face and on the very waterline they are counting down. The cold open doesn't match its own rewind. Seoa's trousers and Jaeha's footwear change inside a ten-minute scene, and Nib has lost his mask. The hill at the climax never visibly moves, so the last beat leans on the sound effect and the narrator. One lettering pass and one continuity pass would make this an 8.

## Strengths
- **A script with a real turn.** The job advert is pure Jaeha (P07). Its failure is funny, then painful (P23–P31), and the fix comes from his own trade: a trap-tester reads the fine print (P34–P36). Three callbacks pay off on cue: "You were standing in front of someone" (P12 → P37), "Promise you'll come back" (P13 → P48), and the title line (P28).
- **Countdown and cross-cutting.** The timers hold the Far Side half together, and each cut back to the room adds something: Nib hears the bell (P32), frost bursts from the foot that was grabbed (P41), Gomi hisses (P42), Nib's ear twitches at the rule (P49), Seoa holds the frozen hand (P53). The chapter ends on a quiet, eerie reveal (P57) that makes you want chapter 10.
- **Polish where it counts.** The strongest images are the fortress and bell spreads (P25, P28), the black-hand grab (P39) and the warm room shots (P06, P12). The acting is clear: Seoa's panic (P02) and deadpan (P08), Nib's almost-smile (P49). Jaeha's pencil, fringe, left-forearm bandage and gloves are right in nearly every panel.

## Problems, most damaging first
1. **[page_35, P51]** The [00:10] window covers Jaeha's face from the nose down, in the quietest close-up of the countdown. The same fault hides the very beats the timers count. In **[page_36, P52]** the window covers his waist and the waterline, and the beat is "dragged to his waist". In **[page_37, P54]** it covers his chest where it meets the water. In **[page_11, P15]** the CALL label sits across his wrist and glove, so it isn't clear whether the button is the label or the ring he presses. Fix: move the timers into the gutters or the empty sky or water, and put the CALL label inside the ring.
2. **[page_01, P01]** The cold open doesn't match **[page_29, P41]**, which shows the same moment. In P01 and P02 (page_02), Seoa wears solid navy trousers, is barefoot and stands at Jaeha's right, with Nib beside a desk on the left. In P41 she wears plaid trousers and grey socks and stands at his left, and Nib lunges in from the right. The room is laid out differently too: a desk on the left in P01, the kitchen on the left in P41. Fix: rebuild P01–P02 to match P41's costumes, blocking and room, or the reverse. Readers check the rewind against the opening.
3. **[page_08, P12]** Costumes change inside the ten-minute scene. Seoa's trousers switch from solid navy (P06, P08) to plaid from P12 on, and even the plaid changes colour between P13, P14 and P53. Jaeha kneels indoors in black boots in P12, after grey socks in P06 and before black socks in P13. Fix: one outfit per character for the whole scene, with Jaeha in grey socks in every room panel.
4. **[page_02, P02]** Nib has no white mask over his left eye here or in any later panel (P06, P09, P12, P13, P32, P37, P41, P49); the left eye is bare amber throughout. He also wears a sleeveless hooded vest, not the hooded cloak. The notch in his left ear, the fang on the left, the two knives and the bare feet are all correct. Fix: restore the mask in every Nib panel, or show in the story why it is gone.
5. **[page_12, P17]** In the establishing shot, the [CALL OPEN · 01:00] window hides the far row of rifts. Only their reflections show, and by colour they are the burning fortress (orange) and the green field (yellow-green), the two rifts the chapter turns on. Fix: lift the window into the black sky above the ring, or into the gutter.
6. **[page_38, P56]** The climax doesn't move. The narration says the hill "rises as if breathing" and "something like a shoulder lifts", but the art is a still, mossy rock, so the reveal only arrives with the eyes in **[page_39, P57]**. There the eyes are already fully open while the narration says they "begin to open". Fix: show the motion (moss and soil sliding off, petals shaking loose, a shoulder ridge lifting out of the hill), or split it into two panels, and draw the eyes half-open in P57.
7. **[page_17, P24]** The refusals aren't acted. The archer has her back to us in P20 (page_14) and still does in P24, so "the archer turns her back" shows no change. The forest creature faces the camera with its head down instead of walking away. Fix: in P20 have the archer glance toward the rift, and in P24 have her turn and walk off. Show the creature's back retreating into the trees.
8. **[page_03, P04]** The hook depends on "[CALL · 00:22 · ANSWERS: 0]", but that text is about 15 px tall at 1080 px wide. That is half the size of the 28 px dialogue and too small to read on a phone. After it come about 1,100 px of empty gradient before "TEN MINUTES EARLIER", and the title card has about 1,400 px of dark around it (page_11 to page_12). Fix: set system-window text at dialogue size and cut the empty scroll by half.
9. **[page_16, P23]** He reads his advert from blank pages, here and again in **[page_22, P31]**. The page drawn in P07 (muscle doodle, bullet lines, ramen) isn't there. The page he tears out in **[page_31, P44–P45]** has a different layout and spiral-binding holes, but the notebook is a bound hardback. The rifts drift as well: their order changes between P19 and P23, and by P31 the fortress, river, ward and snow worlds have become generic green mountains and grey towers. Fix: reuse the P07 page art, drop the spiral holes, and keep one fixed map of the rifts.
10. **[page_09, P13]** Jaeha's pencil is behind his LEFT ear. He faces left toward Seoa, and the pencil sits on the near ear we can see. Everywhere else it is correctly on the right. Fix: move it to the hidden right ear, or leave it out of this panel.
11. **[page_15, P22]** Jaeha is a small cut-out pasted into an otherwise empty black panel, with no water, no reflection and nothing under his feet, and the balloon below him has no tail. **[page_26, P35]** has the same pasted look: he floats over a blue hex-grid floor that doesn't match the black water. On **[page_40, end card]** the chibi holds a blank sign that reads as forgotten lettering. Fix: stand him on the water with his reflection, add the tail, and letter the sign.
12. **[page_04, P06]** Several narration lines describe what the art doesn't show. Gomi is "squashed against the window" (P06) and has a "squashed face" (P10, page_06), but she sits behind the glass, then peeks out startled. "Frost racing up his leg" (P39, page_28): there's no frost on the leg. "With nothing to read from" (P46, page_32): the notebook is still in his hand. "The last few lines flicker brighter" (P50, page_35): nothing flickers. Fix: rewrite the lines to match the art (for example "Gomi presses against the glass", "the notebook hangs useless at his side"), or add the frost and the flicker to the art.

## Hands and arms
- **[page_01, P01]** Nib, right arm: it reaches dead straight from the floor to Seoa's wrist at Jaeha's shoulder and reads slightly long. Borderline.
- **[page_02, P02]** Nib, right hand gripping Seoa's wrist: a mitten-like lump with no visible thumb and no separate fingers, so the grip doesn't read. Seoa's gripped hand splays into a stiff claw with over-long fingers.
- **[page_09, P13]** Seoa, both hands spread wide: the fingers are thin, over-long and spidery. Minor.
- **[page_10, P14]** Seoa, right hand: the phone isn't held. One raised index finger props an edge and no thumb holds the other side, so the phone floats.
- **[page_13, P18]** Jaeha, both hands on the notebook: the left arm crosses his chest, both gloved hands stack on one edge, and the lower (right) hand blurs into the notebook's bottom edge. Muddy.
- **[page_20, P28]** The bell-ringer: besides the hammer in his swinging hand, his other fist holds a second rod with a ball on the end that isn't there in P25. It reads as an extra weapon.
- **[page_22, P30]** Jaeha, right hand reaching: the fingers run index to little from top to bottom, but the thumb tip pokes out beside the ring and little fingers. On a right hand seen from the back the thumb belongs beside the index finger, so it is on the wrong side.

## To gain one point
1. **A placement pass on every system window.** Keep them off faces and the waterline (P51, P52, P54), off the key rifts (P17) and off the hand (P15), and set the cold-open timer (P04) at dialogue size. It is cheap, and it removes the most visible amateur tell in the chapter.
2. **A continuity pass.** Rebuild the cold open (P01–P02) to match P41, give Seoa and Jaeha one outfit each for the room scene, restore Nib's mask, fix the pencil in P13, reuse the P07 advert page, and keep one fixed map of the rifts.
3. **Make the ending and the rejections move on the page.** Show the hill stirring in P56 and the eyes half-open in P57, and let P24 show the archer actually turning away. Then the narrator describes what we see instead of standing in for it.
