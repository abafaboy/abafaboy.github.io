# Judge: chapter 8, Eyes
**Overall: 7.5/10**

| Art | Hands and arms | Storytelling | Lettering | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7.5/10 | 7.5/10 | 8/10 | 7.5/10 | 8.5/10 |

Chapter 8 is well built, and it reads that way. The cold open is a joke that also does a job: the cat chase is Link training, and the new range is what finds the unregistered gate. The bridge plants a real mystery. The car park is a puzzle the reader watches Jaeha solve on the page (drip, pebble, notebook, plan). Then the plan fails for a reason you can see, and Bomi's eyes-shut miss turns into the opening. After that come the rent, the pork belly, and a cliffhanger built from the chapter's own meter. The art carries it with lovely light and colour, and the hands are much improved: one clear finger-count miss among dozens of visible hands. The narration is the strongest element, witty and additive. It slips only where it reads out windows the reader can already see (P07, P09, P53), and once where the art doesn't match it (P59: the landlady never counts the money). What holds the chapter below 8 is a run of slips a fan will screenshot:
- Nib's mask vanishes in the biggest close-up of the dinner.
- The bite that turns the fight is hidden under CHOMP.
- Bomi's stream shows exactly the face Jaeha told her never to show, and nobody reacts.
- Jaeha is bandaged before anyone has treated him.
- A third knife turns up on Nib's belt.

None of it needs new pages. Fix those and this is an 8.

## Strengths
- **Structure and payoffs.** Each thread is set up and paid off:
  - The cat chase is comedy and Link training at once, and the 600 m leash it earns is what reaches the gate under the bridge.
  - The hound is decoded on the page before it is fought (P29 drip, P30 pebble, P31 notebook).
  - The plan fails for a visible reason (the wet ramp, the bite), and the finish comes from Bomi's miss plus the car alarm.
  - "THE RENT IS STILL DUE" (P24) is paid off in P59 and P63.
  - The Route Alignment meter left at 60% in P57 becomes the P69 call.
- **Light, colour and camera.**
  - The light runs from sunset to blue hour to night across the chapter, and the sodium orange under the bridge plays against the gate's violet.
  - The flooded car park's reflections are lovely.
  - The P10 aerial, with the leash threading the rooftops, is a standout.
  - So are the P25 establishing shot from behind the team, the P36 eruption and the P43 drop.
  - The quiet end beats are just as well chosen: P65's faint glow, P68's falling pencil, P70's button in both eyes.
- **Voice and comedy.**
  - Gomi is a running gag that also saves Nib (P19).
  - Nib keeps his dignity ("SHE WANTS TO EAT ME.", "YOU FIRST.").
  - Seoa has her ledger and her proud rent payment.
  - Jaeha gets "BAIT IS WHAT I'M BEST AT." and Bomi gets "GET AWAY FROM MY CO-STAR!"
  - The narration adds wit rather than repeating the balloons ("Someone guards that nothing very carefully", "ringing the dinner bell").

## Problems, most damaging first
1. **[page_44, P62]** **Nib has no mask.**
   - Both eyes are bare in the largest close-up of the dinner, the one panel where the reader looks straight at his face.
   - The mask is his signature mark, and it is present in every other panel.
   - Fix: paint the white mask back over his left eye (the far eye, on the image right, as he faces Seoa). The end-card omake (**[page_50, omake]**) drops it too; give the chibi its mask.
2. **[page_29, P38]** **CHOMP hides the bite.**
   - The SFX is lettered directly over the hound's head and Jaeha's raised forearm, so the bite cannot be seen, and the bite is the turn of the whole fight.
   - The reader only works it out from P40.
   - Fix: move CHOMP into the empty red field at the top left, and let the jaws closing on the forearm read.
3. **[page_37, P51]** **The stream breaks Jaeha's one rule.**
   - Bomi's stream shows Nib's face full-on, mid-leap on the hound. Jaeha's one rule in P26 was "NEVER HIS FACE", and nobody reacts.
   - If it is a planted consequence, give it one beat: Jaeha's face as he sees the screen, or a chat line about the goblin's face. If not, crop, blur or back-view Nib on the stream.
   - The chat and the viewer count are also too small to read on a phone. The jokes ("she shot a CAR lmaooo", "PROTECT THE HUSK") reach the audience only through the narrator. Enlarge the chat, or pull one comment out as a caption.
4. **[page_24, P30]** **Missing finger.** Jaeha's throwing hand is in the foreground and is the focus of the panel, and it has a thumb and only three fingers. Fix: redraw with four fingers, slightly fanned from the release.
5. **[page_34, P47]** **Bandaged too early.**
   - Jaeha's left forearm is already wrapped in a bloodied bandage mid-fight, three panels before Yerin treats it in P50. P43 showed a torn, bleeding sleeve.
   - Fix: keep the torn, bloody sleeve here, and let the bandage first appear in P50.
6. **[page_34, P47]** **WEE-OO covers the opening.**
   - "WEE-OO WEE-OO" sits on top of Nib and the hound's brow, exactly where the reader needs to see that its brow is open.
   - Fix: move the SFX into the dark ceiling on the left, or along the car-light glow on the right, and keep Nib and the brow clear.
7. **[page_33, P44]** **A third knife.**
   - Nib holds a knife in each hand, but a third hilt (brown grip, metal pommel) sits on his belt at the hip. That is three knives, against the "two, never three" rule.
   - Fix: paint out the belt hilt, or turn it into a pouch.
8. **[page_04, P06] and [page_46, P67]** **Ruler-straight brow.**
   - Jaeha's right eyebrow is a ruler-straight black bar of even weight, laid over his hair strands. It reads as a strap or a rendering glitch, and P67 is the climax reaction shot.
   - Fix: redraw a tapered, slightly arched brow sitting under the fringe, as in P41.
9. **[page_13, P16]** **Cloned guards.**
   - The two guards are the same man twice: face, haircut, suit, stance and torch hand. The guard in **[page_14, P18]** and **[page_15, P19]** is a third copy.
   - It reads as copy-paste, the most machine-made moment in the chapter.
   - Fix: vary at least one (age, build, haircut, earpiece, cap).
10. **[page_41, P57]** **Unreadable system window.**
    - The second window ("ROUTE ALIGNMENT · SYNCING A NEW BOSS KIND · 60% ...") is set much smaller than the first and cannot be read on a phone. Its "OPERATOR INTERFACE" header also runs into the frame.
    - This 60% is exactly what P67 pays off (60% → 100%).
    - Fix: match the first window's type size, or give it its own beat.
11. **[page_03, P05]** **Tail points the wrong way.**
    - Seoa's line ("IF YOU'RE GOING TO KEEP DOING THIS, I'M KEEPING THE NUMBERS.") has a tail that points left, off the page, towards where Jaeha sat in P04.
    - The panel is Seoa's own point of view, so a reader hesitates over who is speaking.
    - Fix: point the tail down and right, into the panel, towards her hands at the laptop.
12. **Smaller design slips.** Fix each in place:
    - **[page_43, P61]** Seen from behind, the pencil is behind Jaeha's left ear. Everywhere else it is behind the right (e.g. P25, P64).
    - **[page_25, P32]** Seen from behind, Yerin's mint cross is on her right sleeve.
    - **[page_04, P07]** Nib's open mouth shows two upper fangs.
    - **[page_10, P13]** Nib's single fang sits at the right corner of his mouth. The design is one fang, on the left.
    - **[page_12, P15]** The app's punchline "0" is under Seoa's thumb, and her phone case has switched from green (P14) to black.

## Hands and arms
- **[page_24, P30]** Jaeha, the right hand throwing the pebble (foreground, the panel's focus): a thumb plus three fingers, so one finger is missing. This is the one clear error.
- **[page_30, P40]** Jaeha, the right hand splayed on the ramp while he is dragged: at phone size it reads as a thumb and three fingers. Borderline; give the fourth finger a clear gap.
- **[page_16, P20]** Jaeha, the right hand holding the pencil: only the thumb and the tip of the index finger come out of the glove. The other fingertips are lost in a mitten-shaped black mass, though fingerless gloves should show them. The grip itself holds.
- **[page_48, P69]** Jaeha, the raised left hand: the thumb is an indistinct pale-yellow blob, like a stray pencil tip. It is tiny at phone size.
- **[page_29, P38]** Jaeha, the left forearm in the jaws: hidden under CHOMP, so the bite cannot be judged at all (see Problem 2).
- Every other visible hand, checked at full size, holds up. That includes:
  - Nib's knife grips (P42–P48), his pallet and pipe grips (P13, P34) and his feeding hand (P62);
  - Yerin's sleeve grip and Jaeha lifting it away (P33), her reach (P39) and her healing hands (P50);
  - Seoa's typing (P05), tissue (P08), envelope (P59) and thumb-point (P60);
  - the phone holds (P15, P24, P51, P58), Bomi's peace sign (P26) and draw hand (P45), the pointing hand (P54) and the core hold (P56);
  - the bitten left arm, which reads consistently from P40 to P57.

## To gain one point
1. **Clear the fight's two key beats.** Take CHOMP off the bite in P38 and WEE-OO off Nib and the brow in P47. Then a reader without the narrator sees both the bite and the opening that wins the fight.
2. **Fix the continuity a fan will screenshot:**
   - Nib's mask in P62 (and the omake);
   - the third hilt in P44;
   - the early bandage in P47;
   - the three-fingered hand in P30.
3. **Resolve Bomi's stream in P51.** Either hide Nib's face, or give the reveal a reaction beat so it reads as deliberate. Also make the chat legible, so its jokes land on their own.
