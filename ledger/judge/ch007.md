# Judge: chapter 7, Ten Thousand Won a Trap
**Overall: 7.5/10**

| Art | Hands and arms | Storytelling | Lettering | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7/10 | 8/10 | 8/10 | 7/10 | 8/10 |

This is a clever, funny and well-built chapter. It opens with a rooftop comedy of manners. Inside the gate it plays fair with its mystery: a menthol butt, a boot print, clean grain and a zip tie. The trap-tester then traps the trappers, staged so clearly that you always know where the chutes, plate, ledge and lever are. After that it seals a deal with Bomi, plants a watcher in a nice suit, and ends on Seoa turning "just one more gate" into their father's words on the night of the opening. Jaeha's polite deadpan and Nib's dry inversions give it real personality, and the narration adds jokes rather than repeating the balloons. What keeps it below a strong Originals chapter is execution, not writing:
- one glaring drawing error: Nib's head in P27;
- the decoy pencil comes back while it is still on the mound;
- the trapper changes haircut and loses a glove mid-scene;
- one of Nib's design markers is doubled (an extra ear notch);
- three speaker tails hand lines to the wrong person, one of them the setup for "HE TALKS!";
- the payoff phone screen can't be read.

Hands, which the owner flagged in earlier chapters, are mostly clean here. Fix the first eight problems below and this reads as an 8.

## Strengths
- **A fair-play caper.** Every clue is planted before it pays off:
  - the menthols: the notebook's "THEY SMOKE MENTHOLS" (P15), the banded butt at the gate (P21), Jaeha's "THEY SMOKE MENTHOLS." (P31), and "same brand" on the mound (P41);
  - the rigging: clean grain plus a zip tie (P29–P30);
  - the arithmetic: the job board's ₩30,000 base plus ₩10,000 a trap adds up exactly to the ₩150,000 for twelve traps (P16, P58).

  "TWELVE TRAPS. ONE OF THEM HAD TWO HUNTERS IN IT." pays off the title.
- **Staging and a sense of place.** The rooftop skyline, the shuttered arcade (P18) and the granary's light shafts (P23) are lovely. P28's wide shot, P32's cyan plan overlay and P44's view straight down the second chute mean the reader always knows where everything is. Small continuity is often excellent: Jaeha works with one bare hand from P37 to P49 because his other glove is lying on the mound.
- **Voice and heart.** Jaeha's polite deadpan ("IT'S MY ONLY SKILL", "GOOD AFTERNOON, GENTLEMEN", "IT'S GRAIN, MISS.") plays against Nib's inversions ("YOU FIRST.", "HUSKS DON'T HAVE OPINIONS. I DO."). The narration's wit ("like a citizen reporting a lost umbrella", "His answer is honest, at least") adds to both. The closing sting (P69–P72) lands hard.

## Problems, most damaging first
1. **[page_20, P27]** Nib hangs upside down by his knees, but his head is drawn the right way up, with chin toward the floor and hair toward his chest. That is a head turned about 150° on an inverted body, and because the face reads as upright, his mask sits on what reads as his RIGHT eye. Fix: invert the head, with the chin toward his chest and the hair hanging to the floor. The mask on the image-left of an inverted face then reads as his left eye.
2. **[page_47, P64]** The tail of "NOBODY ASKED ME." stops in the sky between Bomi's and Jaeha's heads, about 260 px above Nib, so the line reads as Jaeha's. The next panel's gag, "HE TALKS! HE'S SO CUTE!" (page_48, P65), only works if the reader knows Nib said it. Fix: run the tail down to Nib's mouth, or give him a small balloon right beside him.
3. **[page_07, P10]** Jaeha's wavy "HE'S A VERY HUNGRY HUSK, SIR." points its tail into the panel at Choi's hand, so both balloons belong to Choi. Fix: point it off-panel to the left edge, the way "VERY UNUSUAL HUSK." points off-panel for Choi in page_10, P14.
4. **[page_37, P51]** The yellow pencil is back behind Jaeha's right ear. Seconds earlier he left it on the mound as part of the decoy (page_27, P36; page_30, P40; page_31, P42), and two men are now buried in that mound. It is correctly missing in P43 and P49. Fix: remove it from P51; it can come back in P55, after he has been down at the mound. Also in page_04, P06 (left profile) the pencil reads as tucked into his fringe at the LEFT temple: hide it behind the far ear.
5. **[page_36, P50]** The trapper changes between shots:
   - His hair: a short spiky crew cut in page_30, P40, page_33, P47 and page_34, P48, then shaggy, shoulder-length hair in P50, page_37, P52, page_39, P54 and page_42, P57.
   - His glove: his crossbow hand is gloved in P50 and P57 but bare in P52 and page_38, P53.

   Fix: keep the crew cut and the glove throughout.
6. **[page_06, P09]** Two design markers slip:
   - Both of Nib's ears carry the V-notch. His right ear (image-left) should be clean, as it is in P14 and P33. Fix: paint out that notch.
   - Yerin's freckles are barely there in her biggest close-up (page_23, P31). Fix: add them.
7. **[page_06, P08]** Two Jaeha lines point their tails toward the wrong side:
   - "THEN SOMEBODY MUST HAVE LEFT IT BEHIND." (Jaeha's "technically true" answer) points off the left edge, toward Choi's side, above a panel of Choi alone. Without the narrator it reads as Choi thinking aloud.
   - In page_23, P31, "THEY SMOKE MENTHOLS." sits on top of Yerin's close-up, right where her tail from page_22 ends, with its tail pointing left toward where she crouched, so for a beat it reads as hers.

   Fix: point both tails to the right, toward Jaeha's side, and lift the MENTHOLS balloon up beside Jaeha in page_22.
8. **[page_42, P58]** The payoff screen ("TRAPS MARKED: 12 / ₩150,000 RECEIVED") is set at about 10 px, which is unreadable on a phone. Without the narrator the reader never sees the sum that pays off the title. Fix: set it large, like the job board in page_11, P16.
9. **[page_29, P39]** The bald man is called "the shield-bearer" (narration for P39, P42 and P54), and Jaeha's notebook sketch shows him behind a big shield (page_11, P15). But he carries only a sword and has no shield anywhere in the dungeon. In the same sketch the crossbow belongs to a woman, not the goatee man. Fix: sling the shield on his back for P39–P44, or have the narration call him "the bald swordsman".
10. **[page_01, P01]** Several sound effects sit away from their sources or cover the key action:
    - CRUNCH CRUNCH sits by Jaeha's boots, not by Nib's cereal;
    - DRIP (page_04, P05) is by Jaeha's legs, not Nib's chin;
    - GRAB (page_21, P28) lands on Jaeha's back, not on Yerin's shoulder;
    - TAK (page_10, P14) covers Nib's catching fingers;
    - SHUNK (page_31, P42) covers most of the blade going in.

    Also, on the bonus card (page_53, no panel id) Jaeha holds a blank sign, which looks like missing lettering. Fix: move each effect to its source, slide TAK and SHUNK off the action, and letter the sign.
11. **[page_34, P48]** The grain changes size and material:
    - In page_27, P36 and page_30, P41 the kernels are fingertip-sized.
    - In the foregrounds of P48, page_26, P35, page_33, P47 and page_21, P29 they become hand-sized, glossy gold ovals that read as coins or gold beans.

    Fix: keep foreground kernels near the P41 scale, with matte grain colour.
12. **[page_48, P66]** A few narration lines don't match the panel:
    - P66: "lowers a long-lens camera", but he is mid-shot (KSHK).
    - page_08, P11: "Then the smirk goes", but Choi was deadpan, not smirking, in P10.
    - page_21, P29: "Through Nib's eyes", but nothing marks it as his view (compare the Link frame in P25).

    Fix: change a word in each ("raises", "the joke goes"), or add the Link border to P29.

## To gain one point
1. Repaint the on-model slips a fan will catch: Nib's head in P27 (page_20), the pencil in P51 (page_37), the trapper's haircut and glove from P50 to P57, and the extra ear notch in P09 (page_06). Each is a small fix, and together they remove most of the machine-made tells in an otherwise polished chapter.
2. Re-tail the lines so every joke lands without the narrator: "VERY HUNGRY HUSK" (P10), "LEFT IT BEHIND" (P08), "THEY SMOKE MENTHOLS" (P31), and above all "NOBODY ASKED ME." (P64), which P65's "HE TALKS!" is built on.
3. Make the payoff readable: set P58's payment screen in large type, as P16's job board is, so the title's arithmetic lands on the page and not only in the narration.
