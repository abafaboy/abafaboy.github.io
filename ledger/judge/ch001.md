# Judge: chapter 1, Bait
**Overall: 7/10**

| Art | Storytelling | Lettering | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7/10 | 7.5/10 | 6.5/10 | 6.5/10 |

This is a confident, well-built first chapter. The cold open sells the premise in seven panels: a red sky over Gwanghwamun, a livestream chat that states the rule ("Summoners are bait"), then "You first?" / "Not yet." / "My summons go first." The flashback is lean, nearly every setup pays off on the page, and the ending works as both a cliffhanger and a character joke. The art is polished and well lit, and the leads stay on-model across dozens of angles. What holds it at 7 rather than an Originals-grade 8 is a run of small breaks that a careful reader will catch: the climax skips the beat that puts Yerin under the sword, Nib's reveal panel shows four knives, the knight swaps sword hands, and one of the seven summons changes shape in each cold-open panel. Several sound effects also sit on faces or on the action they describe. The rendering has an airbrushed sameness; the same cube-shaped rubble appears in P01, P07 and P45. About half the narration only re-reads the balloons. As a reader, I would open chapter 2.

## Strengths
- **The hook and the ending.** P01–P07 deliver the premise, the world's rule (the chat in P02) and the title line in seven panels. The "SIX MONTHS EARLIER, I WAS THE BAIT." card (P08) turns all of that into a promise. "Who's in charge here?" / "I think... it's me?" (P62–P63) works as both a cliffhanger and a joke about Jaeha.
- **Setup and payoff.** The notebook note in P19 pays off in the lizard fight (P20–P22). The red lamp (P26, P30) pays off in "isn't a C-grade boss" (P40). "One husk left" (P51) becomes the husk-as-door (P57), and the husk's face shard becomes Nib's mask (P60). "LEFT SOMEONE BEHIND: 0 TIMES" (P56) gives the system window real heart, and the factory chains (P34) leave a good open question.
- **On-model leads and strong colour.** Jaeha's asymmetric details are right in nearly every panel I checked, both chibi panels included: the fringe over his left eye, the pencil behind his right ear, the T patch on his right chest and the fingerless gloves. The knight's nine-tower sigil and cracked left pauldron hold, and Yerin's mint cross stays on her left sleeve in every clear view. The colour script (blood-red Seoul, sick-green dungeon sky, red lamp, cyan system windows) does real storytelling work.

## Problems, most damaging first
1. **[page_39_P52-P52.jpg, P52]** The climax skips a beat. In P50 (page_37) Yerin sits against the pillar under Jaeha's arm. In P52 she is suddenly sprawled in the open under the raised sword, and Jaeha is out of frame. Nothing shows how she got there, and the "keep your back to the pillar" plan from P46 is quietly broken. Only the narration ("Yerin is down in the open") bridges the gap. Fix: add one panel between P51 and P52 in which the knight's free gauntlet smashes the edge of the pillar (or drags her out by the bag strap) and knocks Jaeha back. The last husk then answers something the reader saw.
2. **[page_47_P60-P60.jpg, P60]** Nib's reveal shows four knives: two crossed in his hands, plus two more hilts in the sheaths behind his hips. He carries two knives, never three, and this is the panel readers will screenshot. Fix: paint the back sheaths empty, or remove them.
3. **[page_30_P40-P41.jpg, P41]** "KLANNNG" sits right on Mok's face and hides it completely, in the only panel that shows him taking the blow. Fix: move the effect into the white space on the right, or arc it along the point where the sword meets the shield, and show his strained face.
4. **[page_02_P02-P03.jpg, P03]**, also **[page_04_P06-P06.jpg, P06]** and **[page_05_P07-P07.jpg, P07]**: one of the seven summons changes in each cold-open panel. In P03 the bulky figure second from the right carries a spiked club on his shoulder. In P06 he is a helmeted knight with an upright halberd, and in P07 he swings a hammer. These silhouettes are the series' promise, and readers will freeze-frame them once the cast arrives. Fix: lock one line-up sheet and repaint the outliers to match it.
5. **[page_37_P50-P50.jpg, P50]** The knight faces us here (the sigil and the helmet opening are visible), so the sword in his screen-right hand is in his left hand. In P37, P43, P44 and P45 it is in his right. Fix: put the sword in his screen-left hand, and make the screen-right gauntlet the one reaching for them.
6. **[page_16_P22-P22.jpg, P22]**, also **[page_14_P20-P20.jpg, P20]**: sound effects cover the very action being counted. In P22, "WHAAM" lies over the tail and its point of impact, with the M on Yerin's head, so the near-miss the scene builds to is never seen. In P20, "CRUNCH" covers the lizard's head and its jaws on the shield. Fix: shrink both effects and move them into the sky, and show the tail club hitting the stone a hand's width from her face.
7. **[page_32_P44-P44.jpg, P44]** The staging works against the danger it is meant to show. The knight's breastplate faces the camera while Yerin stands behind it, off to the right, so neither "don't stand in front of it" nor the narration's "frozen right in front of it" reads. "SHAKE SHAKE" sits on the knight's thigh instead of beside her. The spiky balloon's thin tail points off-panel, so the speaker is unclear. Fix: shoot from behind the knight with Yerin small inside its sword arc, put "SHAKE SHAKE" next to her, and aim the tail at Jaeha behind his pillar.
8. **[page_03_P04-P05.jpg, P04–P05]** (narration) About half the narration only repeats the balloon or caption on screen: P04–P06, P13, P17, P24, P27–P30, P46, P51, P53 and P55–P57. Two lines are also out of step with the art. P16 says the spikes "shred it to powder", but the husk shatters into porcelain shards. P18 reads the "They laugh every time" caption, which sits under P17. Fix: cut the echoes, or turn them into subtext the way "answers like a waiter" (P11) and "Jaeha can." (P49) already do. Match the wording to the panels.
9. **[page_07_P09-P10.jpg, P09]** Yerin is missing from the raid party's establishing shot, and from the march in P14 (page_10). Yet the narration counts "a rookie healer" in the party, and she stands in the P26 line-up. As a result, her first appearance in P21 feels like she pops in from nowhere. Fix: put her (white jacket, pink bag) at the back of the group in P09 and P14.
10. **[page_03_P04-P05.jpg, P04]** The "YOU FIRST?" tail lands on the man's coat at the right edge of the panel, not on the goblin, so on first read the man seems to be asking. Fix: run the tail into the panel to the goblin's mouth. A related problem in **[page_34_P46-P46.jpg, P46]**: Yerin's line is a thought cloud with a speech tail. Pick one; if she says it aloud, use a shaky speech balloon.
11. **[page_49_P63-P63.jpg, P63]** "BLINK BLINK" sits on Jaeha's mouth and touches the tip of his raised finger, the two things the closing gag depends on. Fix: move it up beside his eye, in the dark space to the left of his face.
12. **[page_48_P61-P62.jpg, P62]** Nib faces screen-right here, so the ear we see is his right ear, and it carries the V-notch. The notch belongs on his left ear only. Separately, in **[page_08_P11-P11.jpg, P11]** the back view of Jaeha has no pencil behind his right ear. Fix: remove the notch from the near ear, and put the pencil back.

## To gain one point
1. **Fix the boss fight's continuity.** Add the missing climax beat between P51 and P52. Also give the boss room one wide shot that shows the door, the pillar and the knight together, so the final fight reads without the narrator.
2. **Do one pass on the design rules** in the panels readers will screenshot:
   - only two knives in P60;
   - in P62, the notch on Nib's left ear, not his right;
   - in P50, the sword in the knight's right hand;
   - one locked line-up of the seven summons across P03, P06 and P07.
3. **Do one pass on lettering and narration.** Take every sound effect off faces and focal action (P41, P22, P20, P63), and fix the P04 tail. Cut the narration lines that only re-read the balloons.
