# Judge: chapter 3, The Ninth Wall
**Overall: 7/10**

| Art | Storytelling | Lettering | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.5/10 | 7/10 | 7/10 | 6.5/10 |

This is the chapter the series needed. A cold open plants a wound: a promise, nine scratches in the stone, and "NOBODY CAME BACK FOR ME." A present-day crisis presses on it, and the power-up is earned by a choice rather than a stat screen: "YOU CALLED ME BACK." The script is tight and often moving, and Jaeha's competence and dry humour land. The last six panels turn the boss into a tragedy and open a mystery, the Hush, that makes me want chapter 4. Where it falls short is the trap, the very thing the series sells itself on. The notebook, the trigger panel and the narration disagree about which plate does what. The knight, the portcullis and Jaeha change sides of the bars from panel to panel, so the clever plan reads as "it worked somehow". There are also a few machine-art tells: a sword nobody is holding, a pauldron crack and a sleeve cross that switch sides, and a third knife. Add a narrator who reads balloons aloud and in four places contradicts the art, and a "rest form" with no visual marker. The result is a confident, emotionally effective indie chapter, not yet an Originals-grade one.

## Strengths
- **The script's spine.** The flashback sets up the exact wound Jaeha answers. It runs from "HOLD THE GATE… THEN WE COME BACK FOR YOU" and "GO FIRST, KETT. I'LL BE RIGHT BEHIND YOU." through nine tally marks to the black page. "EVERYONE COMES BACK. THAT'S THE RULE." answers it. "YOU CALLED ME BACK." and a seal that breaks "by choice" pay it off. "I STILL WON'T KILL HIM. BUT I'LL HOLD HIM FOR YOU." and "END IT, NIB… BEFORE THE HUSH TAKES YOU TOO." leave Nib with a real dilemma to end on.
- **The designs carry the story.**
  - The reveal that the commander is the boss rests on design alone: the bucket helm, the nine-tower sigil, the blue cape and the castle-shaped crossguard.
  - Jaeha's pencil, fringe, T patches and notebook pocket hold from almost every angle, down to the upside-down chest T when he is knocked flat in P47.
  - The colour script has a purpose. The flashback is grey, with Kett's yellow scarf its only colour. Green shafts of light play against the red helm, the system windows are cyan, and the Ninth Wall's blue and gold return to Nib's cloak in P53.
- **Jaeha reads as clever on the page.** He spots the plates and spike holes (P20), sketches in the notebook (P21), the cyan overlay turns the hall into a map (P22), and he says "SO I'LL BE IN FRONT." (P23). He jokes under pressure: "OVER HERE, SIR!" and "USUALLY THERE'S LESS SCREAMING." The pacing beats are confident too: the black page, the title after the cold open, the trap splash, and the quiet after the seal breaks.

## Problems, most damaging first
1. **[page_22, P31; page_24, P34; page_28, P40; page_31, P45]** The trap is the chapter's centrepiece, and it doesn't hold together.
   - The notebook (page_15, P21) says the plates in front of the arch fire the spikes and the big centre plate drops the portcullis. The only trigger shown is the knight's foot on the big plate (P31), followed by the spikes (P32), and the narration says the same.
   - The portcullis lands on the knight's back in P33. In P34 the knight kneels upright several metres behind a grate that hangs at head height, nowhere near "a metre from his face".
   - In P40 it has turned its back on the grate to face the hall, which it couldn't do with spiked legs and a portcullis on it. By P42 it faces Jaeha through the bars again.
   - In P45 Jaeha stands on the knight's side of the grate.

   Fix: draw one top-down plan showing the arch, the grate line, the pillars, the plates and the bait circle, and stage every panel from P30 to P47 against it. Keep the knight pinned under the arch facing the corridor and Jaeha always beyond the bars. Make P40 a twist of the torso only, and put the bars between Jaeha and the knight in P45. Either show a small plate firing the spikes before the centre plate drops the grate, or change the notebook line to match P31.
2. **[page_24, P34; page_17, P24; page_22, P31; page_10, P11]** The narration contradicts the art four times:
   - P34 says "Jaeha hasn't even taken his hands out of his pockets", but he waves in P29 and throws an arm over his face in P33.
   - P24 says "She has worked out what the plan needs", but her balloon, "YOU CAN'T FIGHT THAT THING!", shows she thinks he is going to fight it.
   - P31 has the centre plate firing the spikes, against the notebook.
   - P11 says "Nib lowers his knives" over a close-up of his face with no knives in it.

   Fix the lines: for example, P34 "…and Jaeha never laid a hand on it"; P24 "Yerin grabs his sleeve. She thinks he means to fight it."; P11 drop the knives clause; P31 follows the trap fix.
3. **[page_11, P13; page_34, P49]** "Rest form" has no visual marker. The narrator says the amber glow drains out of Nib's eye (P13) and floods back (P49). But his eye is the same bright amber in every rest-form panel (P15, P17, P24, P28, P35, P39, P41). The art never shows what the narrator describes, and without the window a reader can't see that he is powerless. Fix: give Nib a dull, unlit eye (desaturated, no catch-light) from P13 to P45, so the glowing amber returning in P49 becomes a visible payoff.
4. **[page_40, P58]** Nobody is holding the knight's sword. Its hilt sits against the helm's void while both gauntlets hang at its sides. This is the panel just before the knight speaks, so the eye lingers on it. Fix: put the gauntlet on the grip as in P54 and P62, or let the sword drop, which would also show that "the knight stops pushing".
5. **[page_19, P27; page_17, P24; page_27, P39; page_09, P10; page_37, P53]** Several design details switch sides:
   - In P27 the knight has its back to us and faces Jaeha, so the cracked shoulder nearest the camera is its right. At the same angle in P33 that shoulder is clean, and in every other panel the crack is on its left.
   - Yerin's mint cross is on her right sleeve in P24 and P39. It is on her left in P12, P35, P41, P43 and P49.
   - In P10 Nib appears to carry three knives: one in each hand plus a sheathed grip on his belt. The hip sheath in P58 may show the same.
   - In P53 both of Nib's ears are notched; only the left should be.

   Fix: move the crack to the far shoulder in P27, move the cross in P24 and P39, remove the third grip, and clean the right ear in P53.
6. **[page_01, P01]** The narration opens on a fragment, "Nib's commander.", over a landscape with no commander in it. It then repeats the caption ("the Far Side… the Ninth Wall before it fell"). The first words a listener hears sound like a mistake. Fix: cut the fragment or make it a full sentence that frames the flashback, and give the narrator something the caption doesn't already say.
7. **[page_07, P08; page_09, P10; page_34, P49; page_35, P50; page_40, P59]** The narrator reads out lines the reader can already see.
   - "Nobody comes back for him" in P08 uses up the line the black page (page_08, P09) exists to deliver.
   - "Nib, take its legs!" (P10), "Nib, come back!" (P49), "You called me back." (P50) and "Nib…" (P59) are read over their own balloons.

   Fix: cut them and narrate the subtext instead, for example in P50: "Nobody has ever called him back before."
8. **[page_02, P02]** "COMMANDER." breaks out of its balloon: the C and the full stop sit outside the outline. The reply's tail ends in the sky above the castle, nowhere near Nib. This is the first dialogue a reader sees. Fix: widen the balloon and run the tail down to Nib. On page_38, P55, the tail of "BUT I'LL HOLD HIM FOR YOU." also stops between the knight's helm and Nib. Run it all the way to Nib, because the knight speaking is the twist two pages later.
9. **[page_25, P37; page_37, P53; page_27, P39; page_22, P32]** The sound effects cover the action and all look alike.
   - KLANNNG crosses the helm just as it rams the bars.
   - VWOOOOSH covers Nib's chest in the seal-break hero shot, hiding the binoculars and much of the restored cloak.
   - FWASHHH cuts through Yerin's body, and SHHNNNK covers the knight's hips.
   - Nearly every impact uses the same rounded white font, so a sword on stone, a falling portcullis and a summoning glow all "sound" the same.
   - "SHAKE", "BLINK" and "NOD" are words, not sounds.
   - The red "BZZT" (page_11, P14) hangs off the panel edge.

   Fix: move the effects into empty space and letter each one to its sound: jagged and metallic for the clangs, thin and glowing for the light. Replace SHAKE, BLINK and NOD with drawn effects.
10. **[page_11, P14; page_25, P36]** The cooldown clock doesn't add up. It reads 01:00 at P14 and 00:41 at P36. That leaves 19 seconds for the talk, the searchlight sweep, reading the floor, the sketch, Yerin's argument, the stroll across the hall, the charge and the trap. Fix: set P36 to about 00:12 and change the narrator's "forty-one seconds" to match. The decoy sequence down to 00:03 still fits.
11. **[page_26, P38; page_28, P40]** The page never says what the decoy is for. Jaeha's plan rests on "IT ALWAYS HITS WHATEVER IS IN FRONT OF IT", yet the knight turns away from him toward the brightest thing in the room, and no one says what that gains. Fix: put the purpose into Jaeha's P38 shout (keep it busy until Nib can come back). Also make the habit in P23 "the brightest living thing in front of it", so P38 follows from it.
12. **[page_44, P63]** The chibi end card sits between the cliffhanger and TO BE CONTINUED, so the last image after "…BEFORE THE HUSH TAKES YOU TOO." is a joke. Fix: put TO BE CONTINUED directly under P63 and move the chibi below it.

## To gain one point
- **Re-block the trap from a single floor plan (P30–P47).** The plates should match the notebook, the knight should stay pinned under the grate facing the corridor, and Jaeha should always be behind the bars. That turns the centrepiece from "it worked somehow" into the payoff the series promises.
- **Do one narration pass.** Cut the opening fragment and the lines that repeat balloons and captions, fix the four contradictions, and give rest form a visibly dull eye so that "the amber floods back" lands.
- **Clean up the machine-art tells a careful reader will catch:**
  - the sword nobody holds (P58);
  - the flipped pauldron (P27);
  - Yerin's cross (P24, P39);
  - the third knife (P10);
  - "COMMANDER." breaking out of its balloon (P02).
