# Judge: chapter 2, Who's in Charge
**Overall: 7/10**

| Art | Hands and arms | Storytelling | Lettering | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7/10 | 6.5/10 | 7.5/10 | 7/10 | 6.5/10 |

I'd want to read the next chapter. The script does the hard part well. It turns last chapter's cliffhanger into a clean set of rules: summons go first; Link lets Jaeha see and feel what Nib does; Carry lets him take Nib's hit. Each rule arrives exactly when the fight needs it, each one costs something, and all three pay off inside the chapter. Nib is the star. He's a deadpan veteran whose few lines all land, and "That knight was my commander" is a real hook to end on. The rendering is polished, and the character designs hold across almost every panel. The weak point is the chapter's central image, a one-metre goblin against a four-and-a-half-metre knight, which isn't drawn consistently. In the opening panel, the overhead wide shot and the final splash, the knight reads as barely twice Nib's height. Elsewhere it reads as five times his height. Other problems hold it back too: a sword that isn't attached to its hilt in the key dodge panel, a starfish hand in the Link reveal, rules text too small to read on a phone, and narration that too often repeats the balloons and twice gives away a line before it lands (P29, P60). Together they put it a clear step below a strong Originals chapter. None of them is structural, and all of them can be fixed panel by panel.

## Strengths
- **The rules are taught through action.** "UM... SUMMONS GO FIRST?" / "THEY ALWAYS DO." works as a title drop and as character. Link brings sight, silent orders and pain; Carry brings blood and collapse. Each is introduced at the moment of need, and the FIELD FORM clock (05:00 → 03:31 → 02:14 → 01:20 → 00:40) gives the whole fight a ticking structure. Nib's voice is distinct and dry: "THEN WE DO THE SAME JOB. MINE PAID BETTER.", "STOP SHOUTING. I CAN HEAR YOU THINKING NOW.", "STRANGE MAN. HEALER, STOP SHAKING AND FIX HIM."
- **The designs hold.** Nib's left-eye mask, left-ear notch, left fang, two knives, scarf, binoculars and bare feet are right in nearly every panel. So are Jaeha's right-ear pencil, left fringe, vest and both T patches, and Yerin's left-sleeve cross and pink bag. The knight's nine towers (counted in P11, P57 and P60), cracked left pauldron and tower crossguard are consistent, the system windows share one clean design, and the violet wound glow carries from P35 into P38 and P54.
- **The set pieces work.** The P03 kick splash, the P14 dash, the P19 run up the blade, the P21 swat, the P27 split-POV Link panel, the P33 → P34 → P35 dip/drop/strike sequence, the P54 roar with its red alien-language balloon, the P57 sigil close-up and the P60 reveal all read instantly on a phone.

## Problems, most damaging first
1. **[page_01, P01]** The knight's size isn't fixed, and it is the premise image.
   - In P01, Nib's head reaches the bent knight's hip.
   - In **[page_12, P16]**, the knight stands barely taller than Jaeha, who is nearer the camera, and only about twice Nib's height. That is the panel where the narration says "four and a half metres".
   - In the **[page_44, P60]** splash, it is about twice Nib's height once you allow for perspective.
   - In **[page_14, P19]** and **[page_23, P32]**, it is four to six times his height.

   Fix: set one scale rule (Nib's head at the knight's knee; the knight's knee at Jaeha's chest) and re-render P01, P16 and P60 to it.
2. **[page_25, P34]** The sword is broken. The gauntlet at top right grips the tower crossguard, but the blade passes under the fist and carries on past it to the panel edge, so it isn't attached to its hilt. This is the panel where Jaeha's plan pays off. Fix: redraw it so the blade leaves the crossguard and sweeps left from the fist, or crop the fist out.
3. **[page_44, P60]** The narration gives away the last line. "The way a soldier stands before his officer" comes just before the balloon "THAT KNIGHT WAS MY COMMANDER." "Stands at attention" also contradicts the art, where Nib stands relaxed with his knives out and his arms down. Fix: change it to something like "Nib lowers his knives and looks up at it." Then the balloon delivers the reveal.
4. **[page_29, P39]** The CARRY window's body text is 13–15 px tall on the 1080 px strip, about 5 pt on a phone. It can't be read without zooming, and it is the rule the climax turns on. Other small text:
   - **[page_04, P06]** "FAR SIDE SCOUT · SEAL 1/5 · FIELD FORM 05:00": 16 px.
   - **[page_05, P07]** the name-card subtitle: 13 px.
   - **[page_20, P26]** the LINK body text: 19 px, borderline.

   Fix: set window body text at the size used in [page_31, P42] (about 30 px).
5. **[page_20, P26]** The hero hand of the Link reveal is a starfish. Its four fingers fan through about 180 degrees, index straight up and little finger straight down, and the thumb juts back along the forearm. The [NEW SKILL: LINK] window also sits above the reach, so readers meet the window first, while the narration says it comes "only then". Fix: redraw it as a natural reach (fingers together and slightly curled, thumb under the index). Put the window below the hand, or split the panel in two.
6. **[page_18, P24]** The captions are out of order. The notebook-paper caption ("KNIGHT. SLOW TO TURN. HITS WHATEVER IS IN FRONT. LEFT SHOULDER?") and the caption "I CAN'T SEE HIS SHOULDER FROM HERE…" sit under P24. That is before **[page_19, P25]**, the panel where Jaeha opens the notebook, and the notebook drawn there shows only sketches and tally marks. The P25 narration then describes a line the reader saw a page earlier. Fix: move both captions below P25, or letter the line onto the notebook page itself.
7. **[page_22, P29]** The narration makes Jaeha's deduction ("it dips when the swing begins") one panel before he shouts it in P30. It also runs over a still close-up that shows no dip. Fix: narrate only what he now sees, for example "Through Nib's eyes, there it is: a crack in the left shoulder plate." Then the P30 balloon is the discovery.
8. **[page_01, P01–P02]** The narration often just says what is already on the page.
   - Restating the balloon or caption beside it: P01 and P02, **[page_05, P07]**, **[page_14, P19]**, **[page_32, P43]**, **[page_33, P45]** and **[page_35, P50]**.
   - "For the first time" appears four times in sixteen panels: **[page_26, P36]**, **[page_27, P37]**, **[page_32, P44]**, **[page_36, P51]**.

   Fix: give each line something the art can't show (motive, cost, history), as P10, P12, P32 and P39 already do. Keep "for the first time" only for P44.
9. **[page_05, P07]** Balloon tails end on faces.
   - P07: the tail runs into Nib's forehead and mask.
   - **[page_23, P31]**: the tail runs into his mask.
   - **[page_36, P51]**: the tail's tip sits on Jaeha's eye.
   - **[page_07, P09]**, **[page_09, P13]** and **[page_33, P46]**: the tails end on cheeks or ears.

   Fix: stop tails short of the head, aimed at the mouth.
10. **[page_16, P21]** The knight's swatting arm grows out of the collar between the helm and the cracked pauldron, with no shoulder joint. The gauntlet is a stack of five or six tube fingers with no back of the hand, so the fingers can't be counted. Fix: re-render it with the arm coming from under a pauldron and a gauntlet that clearly has four fingers and a thumb.
11. **[page_37, P52]** Three design flips:
    - P52: the pencil is behind Jaeha's LEFT ear. His head is turned over his left shoulder, and the ear we see holds the pencil.
    - **[page_02, P03]**: Nib's RIGHT ear also has a nick, as well as the notch on the left.
    - **[page_32, P43]**: Yerin has no freckles in her close-up.

    Fix: move the pencil to the hidden right ear, paint out the right-ear nick, and add the freckles.
12. **[page_23, P31]** The TWITCH sound effect sits on Nib's chest and scarf, and no ear movement is drawn, so the narrated "Nib's ear twitches" isn't on the page. Fix: move TWITCH next to his left ear and add two motion ticks to the ear.

## Hands and arms
- **[page_20, P26]** Jaeha, right hand reaching for the amber thread: the four fingers fan through about 180 degrees and the thumb points back along the forearm. This is the most visible hand error in the chapter.
- **[page_25, P34]** Knight, gauntlet at top right: the grip doesn't hold the weapon, because the blade isn't connected to the crossguard in the fist.
- **[page_16, P21]** Knight, swatting arm: there is no shoulder, and the arm emerges from the collar. The gauntlet's fingers can't be counted (five or six tubes, no palm, no back of the hand).
- **[page_03, P04]** Nib, both arms: stretched straight back to Yerin's collar and Jaeha's vest, about 1.6 times his torso length, with no readable elbows. They look like rubber.
- **[page_21, P28]** Jaeha, right hand on the pillar: a black mitten blob with one fingertip showing; TWINGE covers the wrist.
- **[page_12, P17]** Nib, lower (cross-body) arm: the forearm is too long, with no clear elbow, and the hand is half hidden behind SKRANNG.
- **[page_01, P01]** Nib, raised left arm: the sparks, which mark the point of contact, are at his wrist. The sword seems to press on his forearm rather than on the knife he is blocking with.
- **[page_02, P03]** Nib, raised knife hand: a lumpy, mitten-like fist with no separation between the fingers.
- **[page_06, P08]** Jaeha, left palm-up hand: the index finger rises out of the top of the glove and the ring and little fingers curl under. It is countable, but it takes a second look.
- **[page_31, P42]** Jaeha, right hand flat on the floor: four identical parallel fingers, with the middle finger no longer than the others (a rake hand).
- **[page_01, P02]** Jaeha, right hand: the blood is on the nail side of the fingertips, though he would have touched his lip with the pads. The thumb is stubby.
- **[page_40, P55]** Jaeha, right hand over his ear: the pencil reads as held between his fingers rather than tucked behind his ear.

## To gain one point
1. **Lock the knight's scale.** Re-render P01, P16 and P60 so the giant looks giant in the hook, the establishing shot and the final splash. Repair the P34 sword and the P26 hand in the same pass.
2. **Edit the narration.** Cut it down to what the art can't say: drop the repeats, stop it giving away P30 and P60, and cut three of the four "for the first time"s. Reorder P24–P26 so captions, windows and narration follow the scroll.
3. **Make the text readable on a phone.** Set every system window at the P42 size (P39, P06, P07 and P26), and take balloon tails off faces.
