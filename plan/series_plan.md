# Series plan: 222 chapters

The story runs 222 chapters in four seasons and 17 arcs, Chapters 1–14 included as canon. Each arc gives Lucan a new kind of problem, not just a bigger enemy. Every side quest leaves behind a person, an asset or a clue that pays off later. At about 3 minutes per chapter, that is roughly 11 hours of video.

## How the plan is built

These rules come from how long-running manhwa pace themselves: Greatest Estate Developer (222 chapters), Slime, Solo Leveling, Trash of the Count's Family, TBATE and Mount Hua.

- **Arcs of 10–22 chapters, seasons of 46–60.** Each season ends on a resolved arc plus one new threat.
- **Build projects take 1–4 chapters inside a threat arc.** Each pays off later, the way Slime's festival dungeon becomes its war fortress.
- **Each arc widens who knows what Lucan is:** his village, then the Order, the north, the court, the kingdom, and finally the world.
- **Defeated rivals become allies.** Corvin, Hask, Sabine and Maric each turn, at a price.
- **Greyhollow never leaves the page for long.** Bram-led cutaways keep the estate growing while Lucan is away.
- **Lucan's power is rationed.** The Root sets a visible ceiling on how deep bonds can grow. Overdraw keeps costing him. He loses his bonds entirely in Arc 13. He doesn't fight until Chapter 156.
- **Problems a sword can't solve:** law, debt, trade, plague, court opinion and two fronts at once.
- **No tournament longer than 12 chapters, no reveal repeated, and a comedy breather at least every 15 chapters.**

## Rules added for the long run

- **The Root lamp.** The first rimeglass lamp Tobin made hangs in the keep's hall, and it has been brightening since Lucan arrived. Ilse reads it as Lucan's Root, so readers get a stat that visibly climbs after each build.
- **Root ceiling.** No bond can grow past what the Root allows. It stays at V until Chapter 120, when Lucan becomes Lord of the North and VI unlocks. Each step before that makes bonds grow faster, not deeper.
  - Ch 45: Saltmouth claimed
  - Ch 57: the Compact signed
  - Ch 120: Lord of the North
- **Distance.** Bonded people grow while they are near Lucan or on land he roots. Away from both, they stall but don't lose rings. Only a broken bond, or a cage like Arc 13's, makes rings ebb.
- **The Tide.** A surge lifts bonded people up to one ring above the ceiling for one night and leaves a small permanent gain. It never heals the unbonded.
- **Roots and grief.** A Wellspring who dies naturally leaves a gentle root: Aldric's south stayed warm, though after 300 years it is fading and frost creeps even there. A Wellspring murdered or drained to death leaves a grieving root that freezes the land. That is Hesper's north.
- **Rimeglass with human shapes inside is never mined.** This is Skeld law, adopted by Lucan in Ch 40.
- **Why the Order waits.**
  - Before Ch 58: a royal warrant has to be issued and carried north, and the column carrying it is caught in the Kesting surge.
  - After Kesting: the Compact makes open arrest a civil war, which King Edric refuses.
  - The summons is the answer. It pulls Lucan south, away from his bonds, while an army marches north behind him.

## Season 1: The March (Chapters 15–60)

### Arc 3: The Twelve Riders (15–28), detailed

The Order arrives with legal authority. Lucan can't fight it, because that's treason, and he can't run, because leaving breaks his bonds and Greyhollow's thaw. He hides, and uses the law. The arc is Lucan learning his power on purpose, and the Order learning it's real.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | Two Days | War council in the keep. Ilse: Measurer lenses read rings at a glance, so peasants at III and IV are proof. Run and the bonds break; fight and it's treason. Lucan: "We hide. In plain sight." She points at Tobin's first lamp, which has brightened every month since he came: "That's your Root. Bram is at five because it lets him be. No higher." Alone with her, Lucan asks why she never reported him. "I was going to. Then you fixed the mill." On the wall at night he tries to feel the cold on purpose and finds 112 thin threads. |
