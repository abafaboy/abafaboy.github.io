4. Agna, the Saltmouth elder: an old fisherwoman of about 70, the head of a dying fishing village. Small, wiry and tough, with weathered brown skin deeply creased by wind and salt, sharp black eyes, and a thick white braid wound with faded blue fishing twine. A short clay pipe in the corner of her mouth. A salt-stained dark oilskin coat over layered grey wool shawls, rolled sea boots, a gutting knife at her belt, and a necklace of small shells with an old silver ring hanging on a cord. Hands on her hips, thoroughly unimpressed.
