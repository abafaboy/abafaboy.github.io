STYLE: Korean webtoon / manhwa for a medieval northern fantasy, following the series house style in plan/STYLE_GUIDE.md. Draw forms through confident digital line art: darker tapered outer and overlap contours, fine sparse facial marks, clear silhouettes. Use a base colour plus one or two hard-edged shadow tones and small crisp highlights. Gradients only for skies and aether/rimeglass glow. Group hair, cloth, wood, stone and metal into graphic shapes; avoid soft material modelling and glossy skin or hair.

Use a limited palette and strong value hierarchy. Detail establishing shots selectively. Dialogue and reaction panels use simple, faded or plain backgrounds, a few relevant setting shapes and generous quiet space; roughly one third low-detail space is a flexible house target. Do not give every surface equal sharpness or fill it with granular stone, frost, fur, wood grain, paper fibres or clutter. Frame acting through brows, eyelids, mouth, head angle, shoulders, close-ups and hands. Vary shot distance and angle; crop figures where useful. Comedy may simplify expressions or briefly use chibi proportions while keeping identity and costume recognizable. Avoid making every panel a fully furnished cinematic illustration.

No decorative bloom, god rays, dust motes, haze, depth-of-field blur or photoreal surface rendering. Use focus lines sparingly for shock; glow only for actual aether or rimeglass. Keep necessary weather/smoke simple and clear of faces and hands. Identity sheets supply identity, clothing, anatomy and scale ONLY; do not copy their existing finish, lighting or texture. Style references supply line/shadow/shape principles ONLY; never copy their characters, lettering, costumes, staging or compositions. Existing glossy/painterly references do not override these rules. ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, speech bubbles, captions, sound-effect lettering, signatures or watermarks in art. Books and documents show only illegible squiggles. Leave the panel's requested calm space for later lettering.

For still panels, match the dominant spoken/emotional beat with natural, varied speaking-mouth shapes and credible jaws, lips and teeth. Preserve silent/listening moments; voice-over narration does not automatically open a character's mouth. Use coherent facial and head/shoulder acting, not the same neutral mouth everywhere. Maintain each identity's head/body ratio, shoulder width and limb/hand proportions across panels; check joints, fingers, grips and foreshortening. Chibi proportions belong only to deliberate comedy beats. This is still-panel expression design, not animated lip-sync.
