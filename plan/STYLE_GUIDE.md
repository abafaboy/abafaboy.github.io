## Purpose and evidence

The target is a readable, line-led Korean webtoon treatment for a medieval northern fantasy. This is Abafa's house style. Real manhwa varies in gradients, texture and detail; the restrictions below are deliberate choices for this series, not universal claims about the medium.

## Why the current rendering needs to change

The issue is the hierarchy of the image, not the absence of a manhwa label. Granular stone, frost, fur, tiles, paper and wood grain compete with the faces. Thin contours combined with soft material modelling make each panel resemble a finished illustration. Uniform contrast and equally sharp clutter give incidental objects the same importance as a gesture. Static, fully furnished cinematic shots recur where a close reaction or a simpler dialogue panel would communicate more clearly.

Chapters 1–20 are the closer project baseline. Compare the three tests side by side with actual Chapter 1–16 source panels, while treating their identities, costumes and story situations as separate from the rendering comparison. The user's stricter rules override soft lighting, texture or blur that may remain in an early sample.

## Drawing and rendering

1. Build the image from clear silhouettes and confident digital contours. Use darker, tapered outer and overlap contours with finer facial marks and interior detail. A face, hand, cloak edge and important prop must read at 25% of original size.
2. Use a base colour plus one or two hard-edged shadow tones. Keep highlights small and crisp. Organize hair into drawn masses; describe cloth with a few selected angular folds. Avoid continuous soft modelling, glossy skin and glossy ribbons of hair.
3. Gradients are reserved for skies and magic glow. Skin, ordinary hair, cloth, wood, stone and metal use grouped graphic shapes rather than sprayed transitions or photoreal surface response.
4. Use a limited palette per scene with strong value separation between the focal figure, an important prop and the background. Let secondary forms lose detail. Do not render every rivet, grass blade, stone pore, frost crystal, fur strand or paper fibre.
5. Establishing shots may carry selective architectural or landscape detail. Dialogue and reactions use simple, faded or plain backgrounds. Convey place with a few relevant shapes rather than a complete inventory. Aim for roughly one third quiet, low-detail space when the composition permits; this is a flexible house target, not a rigid area quota.
6. Frame emotion through close-ups, eyes, hands, head angle and shoulders. Vary shot distance and angle. Crop figures at the panel edge when it helps the beat. Do not turn every panel into a centered cinematic illustration.
7. Make acting specific through brows, eyelids, mouth shape, head tilt and shoulder tension. Comedy can simplify or briefly use chibi proportions while retaining recognizable hair, costume and identity. Preserve the scripted emotional beat.
8. Use focus lines sparingly for shock. Glow belongs to aether and rimeglass. Avoid decorative bloom, god rays, dust motes, haze, depth-of-field blur and light effects that obscure faces or hands. Snow, smoke or weather remains only when the scene needs it and must be drawn simply.
9. Generate no text, captions, bubbles, sound-effect lettering, signatures or watermarks. Document pages contain only illegible squiggles, with no recognizable letters or numbers. Lettering is added later by the layout pipeline.

## Test review rubric

Record a separate result for each panel, then compare the treatment across all three:

- Hard pass: exact required dimensions, intended cast and identities, correct anatomy/laterality, Ash scale where present, correct scene action and no generated text.
- At 25% size, the figures and important props remain led by contours and grouped shadows. Facial acting and the gesture must read without zooming.
- Inspect five representative shadow boundaries across the focal face, hair, cloth and prop where present. At least four should be hard-edged. Record the samples rather than selecting only convenient areas; inspect at original size as well.
- Check quiet space and detail hierarchy against the flexible roughly-one-third target. Faces and hands must remain free of decorative effects and clutter.
- p07 reads as dry character humour, p12 as a simplified comedic reveal and p17 as a restrained boundary landscape. All three must share the same line/shading treatment despite different shot types.
- Compare each test with the selected actual Chapter 1–16 panels and its original Chapter 29 counterpart. Report concrete differences, remaining defects and the exact output hashes. No single automated score or successful generation substitutes for pixel review.
