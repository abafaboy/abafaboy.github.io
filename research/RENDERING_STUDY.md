# Rendering study: how real Korean webtoons are drawn and coloured (panel level)

**What this adds.** `VISUAL_SURVEY.md` recorded lettering, UI and colour habits in episode 1 of 103 titles. This study looks closer at the drawing itself: line, shading, light, colour, backgrounds, effects, faces and monsters. It is the evidence behind `codex/MANHWA_STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

**Method.**
- Claude read the episodes on WEBTOON (official English site) in Chrome on 24–25 October 2026.
- Every image slice of an episode was laid out in a grid, then chosen slices were shown at about 360 px wide and zoomed.
- Slice numbers below count from the top of the episode.
- Nothing was downloaded or saved. No image from these titles is ever given to image generation, and Codex never sees them.

**Titles studied.**

| Title | Episodes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| *The Lone Necromancer* | 1, 2, 6 | modern Korea, a summoner-type lead |
| *Omniscient Reader* | 0–3 | modern Seoul apocalypse |
| *Eleceed* | 1 | modern action comedy |
| *My S-Class Hunters* | 1 | modern Seoul hunters, gate monsters |
| *I'm the Max-Level Newbie* | 1 ("The Top of the Tower") | hunter and game world |
| *The Return of the Disaster-Class Hero* | Prologue | modern Korea, calamity monsters |

## 1. What each title does

### The Lone Necromancer
- **Line.** Bold, tapered black contours with a thick outer line. Hair is cut into angular clumps.
- **Colour.** One or two hard cel tones and saturated colour, with warm/cool complementary flips (cyan glow against hot orange). Action and close-ups sit on solid or gradient colour backgrounds.
- **Effects.** White focus and speed lines. Figures and SFX break the thin black panel borders.
- **Faces.** Exaggerated: gritted teeth, sweat, wrinkles.

### Omniscient Reader
- **Line and shading.** Crisp line, hard flat shadow shapes.
- **Colour.** A muted cool grey-blue palette with cyan particles. Horror close-ups go red and white with halftone dots.
- **Name card.** A portrait on an olive gradient, with the name in white serif type.
- **Broadcasts.** Glitch with RGB-split edges.

### Eleceed
- **Line and shading.** Thin coloured (brown) line, soft painterly shading, a pastel palette.
- **Backgrounds.** Painted and photo-based.
- **Effects.** Heavy motion blur; sparkle accents for comedy.

### My S-Class Hunters (episode 1)
- **Slice 11 (talk).**
  - Thin-to-medium black line, slightly sketchy and tapered. Hair in clumps with one dark tone.
  - One soft-edged shadow tone, a warm light bloom from the upper left, blush hatching on the cheeks and a sweat drop.
  - The corridor behind is soft and desaturated blue-grey.
  - Three layers of depth: a figure cut off by the frame in the foreground, the lead in the middle, a small figure deep in the corridor.
  - The lead's saturated red work uniform is the only strong colour.
  - The bubbles break out of the panel border into the white gutter.
- **Slice 33 (monster reveal).**
  - A giant black crow-like monster: red mask around the eye, white eye without a pupil, rows of teeth, drool.
  - Its feathers are drawn with rough dry-brush strokes, rougher than the people.
  - Lit by a cool blue-violet gradient.
  - The lead is small in the lower right, his back to us.
- **Slice 40 (flock over the city).**
  - A high-key white sky with grey speed lines from the upper left.
  - The apartment towers are pale grey and low-contrast, pushed back.
  - Near monsters are dark navy with red patches; far ones are pale blue silhouettes (atmospheric perspective).
  - A red glowing gate with pink crackle lines.
  - A giant brush-italic purple SFX runs down the left third of the panel.
- **Slice 51 (impact).**
  - A fighter grabs the monster's beak.
  - Behind: an ice-blue and white energy burst, with black radial focus lines closing in from every edge of the frame and white crackle shapes like broken glass.
  - The red uniform against the icy burst is a complementary clash.
- **Slice 55 (aftermath).** A painted Seoul skyline from a bridge at sunset: pink-orange sky, a huge black smoke column, embers and black flecks in the air. Soft painterly background, narration box on top.
- **Slice 61 (power reveal).**
  - A man seen from behind in a white shirt with lavender shadows and many small fold lines.
  - Purple-black fire curls round his forearm, with purple lightning lines and black ink spatter.
  - The fiery painted orange sky behind is complementary to the violet power, and a purple rim light runs along the arm.
- **Page.** History is told in narration boxes on a white page (slice 47). The logo card comes at the very end of this prologue episode (slice 64 of 65).

### I'm the Max-Level Newbie (episode 1)
- **Page rhythm.** Much white page, a dark-navy system window every few slices, a boss fight early (slices 9–18 and 29), then daily life.
- **Slice 11 (action).**
  - A horned axe warrior with a knight leaping above, against a hot orange-yellow radial burst with speed lines.
  - Metal is glossy: white specular shapes on the helmet and armour, hard cel shadows with soft gradients on the metal only.
  - Thick black outline.
- **Slice 14.**
  - Two panels split by a slanted gutter.
  - Below, a battering log hits with a white flash. Tiny thrown silhouettes give the scale, and orange sparks fly.
- **Slice 29 (impact).** Rock debris drawn as faceted dark slate polygons with light edges, flying out of a white-blue burst with radial speed lines.
- **Slice 43 (lead close-up).**
  - Clean black line, heavier on the jaw and the hair silhouette. A heavy upper-lid line and a half-lidded smirk.
  - The skin has one hard-edged cool lavender-grey shadow plus a soft blue light gradient from the right.
  - Black hair with navy shine bands and a cool rim light.
  - A blue gradient background with soft white specks.
  - A system window floats in the scene in front of him, so we read it mirrored from behind.

### The Return of the Disaster-Class Hero (prologue)
- **Page rhythm.**
  - The first ~36 slices are an apocalypse montage on a black page: sepia-orange fire, grey smoke, a red key word in the narration ("CALAMITIES."), and giant red or white SFX.
  - The title card comes at slice 37 of 97 (about 38%), then the present day at a press conference.
- **Slice 5 (monster face).**
  - A demon close-up: a third eye glowing red with a black slit, white-hot eyes with red veins, and horns.
  - Dark brown face with hard cel shadows, lit from below by fire, with floating ash.
- **Slice 27 (hero walk).**
  - From behind, a low angle, the figure large in the frame.
  - A black cape with a bright white rim of backlight along its edge.
  - Sepia haze, a ruined city and embers, with a giant red-eyed monster faint in the haze behind.
- **Slice 35 (speed).** A painted motion smear of a moving figure in blue-white light.
- **Slice 41 (power).** A shaggy-haired figure with his face in shadow and glowing white-blue eyes. Navy palette with blue glow. A white SFX with a blue neon glow outline.
- **Slice 13.**
  - A protest: fists and heads in the foreground as dark silhouettes framing the panel.
  - A man shouts into microphones with hard black shadow masses over half his face.
  - The two panels are split by a slanted black bar.
- **Slice 52 (press).** Camera flashes drawn as white cross-shaped star flares. Clean line, cel shading, slightly simplified faces.
- **Slice 55.** An extreme close-up of a mouth and chin with a sweat drop, on a black page ("GULP").
- **Slice 57.** A shouting man with big hard black shadow shapes under the jaw and collar, cool blue-white light and horizontal lens-flare streaks.

## 2. Patterns that repeat across titles

| Pattern | Seen in | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Black, tapered line, heavier on the outer silhouette.** Hair is cut into clumps. | LN, ORV, MLN, MSCH (thinner, sketchier) | Keep the G-pen ink of method B. Make the silhouette and the jaw/hair mass visibly heavier. |
| **One cel shadow on forms, usually hard-edged.** Soft gradients appear mostly as light from a source. | ORV and LN (hard only); MLN 43 (hard shadow plus a soft blue light wash); MSCH 11 is the exception, with a soft-edged shadow and a warm bloom from one side | Keep "one hard shadow per material", and add a light layer from the scene's real light source. |
| **Rim light.** | MLN 43 (cool rim on the hair), Disaster 27 (a white backlight rim on the cape), MSCH 61 (a purple rim from the power) | A thin, crisp line of light along the figure's edge, from a light behind or beside it. It separates the figure from a dark background. |
| **Big dark value masses in dramatic beats.** | Disaster 13, 57, 5; ORV black backgrounds | Drama comes from large black shapes, not from more detail. |
| **The background steps back.** | MSCH 40 (pale towers), MSCH 11 (soft corridor), MLN 43 (gradient), LN (flat colour in action) | Establishing shots are detailed. Everywhere else the background is simpler and lower in contrast than the characters. |
| **One saturated signature colour.** | MSCH (red uniform), ORV (cyan in grey-blue) | Each lead owns one colour, and the rest of the panel is muted around it. |
| **Power in the complementary colour.** | MSCH 61 (violet on orange), MSCH 51 (icy blue on red), LN (cyan on orange) | When power appears, the background turns the opposite hue. |
| **Hits and action peaks on hot or white fields.** | Impacts: MLN 11 (orange burst), MSCH 51 and MLN 29 (white-blue burst). An action peak: MSCH 40 (a white sky behind the flock) | The hit panel drops the scene and uses a colour field plus radial lines. |
| **Radial lines that close in from the frame edges.** | MSCH 51, MLN 29, LN | Focus lines point at the contact point or the face. |
| **Graphic debris and particles.** | MLN 29 (faceted polygons), MSCH 61 (ink spatter), Disaster 27 (embers) | Particles are drawn shapes, and they appear only where something breaks, burns or glows. |
| **Monsters rougher than people.** | MSCH 33 (dry-brush feathers), Disaster 5 (cracked, under-lit) | Monster texture is rough and the people stay clean; the contrast tells them apart. |
| **One red accent for danger.** | MSCH 33 (red eye mask), Disaster 5 and 27 (red eyes), ORV (red horror) | A red eye or a red lamp is the warning. |
| **Depth layers and cropped figures.** | MSCH 11 (three layers), Disaster 13 (foreground silhouettes), MSCH 33 (the lead's back in the foreground) | Most panels have a foreground element or a crop. Centred, uncropped full figures are rare. |
| **Scale shown with tiny figures.** | MLN 14, MSCH 40 | A big thing is big because something small is beside it. |
| **Slanted gutters and black bars between panels.** | MLN 14, Disaster 13 | Use them in action and shock, not in conversation. |
| **Extreme close-ups.** | Disaster 55 (mouth); ORV ep 0 (eye close-ups in the flash-forward sword clash, from the corpus notes) | Eyes, mouths and hands get their own panels. |
| **Manhwa face marks.** | MSCH 11 (blush hatching, sweat drop), LN (gritted teeth, sweat) | Short diagonal blush strokes, sweat drops and teeth shine are part of the acting. |
| **A cleaner look for talk, a heavier one for peaks.** | MLN (clean close-up vs glossy action), Disaster (painted montage vs cleaner press scene) | Rendering effort follows the beat. The peak panel gets the most. |

## 3. Our old art against these patterns

These are our own images on the laptop:
- `style_test/abcd/compare.png` (methods A–D);
- `style_test/p3_nib_reveal.png`;
- the chapter 1 and 10 contact sheets.

| Pattern | Our old panels |
|---|---|
| Stated light and value masses | Even, all-over light. Everything sits in the middle values, so nothing pops at phone size. |
| The background steps back | The shop and the flat are as detailed and contrasty as the characters (compare.png image 1, p3). |
| The camera has a position | p3 is a centred, one-point-perspective full figure at eye level, with no foreground layer and no crop. |
| Power in the complementary colour | The summon ring is cyan, but the room keeps its ordinary colours and the faces are not lit by it. |
| Graphic effects at peaks | A thin ring and seam, and two small motion ticks. |
| Faces that act | Mild expressions. The p3 summon reveal has a calm frown. |
| Monsters | A cute rock golem. |

What was already right, and stays:
- clean ink;
- one hard shadow tone;
- no airbrush;
- the cyan summon circle and seam;
- the anti-AI checklist.

The owner chose method B for those qualities on 21 October. The new style guide keeps them and adds the light, value, camera, colour and effect rules above.
