## Synthesis (counted across the 103 episodes above)

- **Speech bubbles:** white, slightly flattened ovals with a THIN even outline (about 1.5–2.5 px at 800 px) are the default in essentially every title. Tails are short-to-medium curved wedges; long thin straight tails crossing borders were rare. Bubbles live in white gutter space next to the panel they belong to and often overlap the panel edge.
- **Intensity is shown by the OUTLINE, not the colour:** jagged white bursts for shouts (most action titles); a thick FUZZY/HATCHED black ring for an intense inner vow, panic or exasperation (≈25 titles, the most common "special" bubble); wobbly scalloped outline for stammering/nervous speech; dashed outline for whispers; hexagon/polygon for counting, announcements or a streamer greeting.
- **Menace / non-human voices:** black bubble + white text (≈15 titles); black + red text with red outline (ORV, Urek Mazino); coloured glowing bubbles for supernatural voices (navy+teal in Mage Again, neon purple in See No Evil).
- **Thought:** three conventions coexist — (a) oval + 2–3 shrinking circles toward the head (Corporate Punch, Constellations, Odd Girl Out); (b) plain floating text in white space with no bubble (Lookism, Study Group, World is Money and Power); (c) narration rectangle (most). Intense thoughts switch to the fuzzy ring.
- **Narration:** thin black-bordered white rectangle (most common), black box with white text (ORV, Copy-Paste, Killer Peter), white text straight on black for prologues (≈20 titles), one red key word (Disaster-Class Hero, Sweet Home). Footnotes in small text under the box (Social Coin).
- **System windows — four families seen:** (1) bright cyan holographic, chamfered, glowing double frame, desktop controls (ORV, Questism); (2) dark navy/teal HUD with thin cyan border, chevrons, scanlines, raised top tab with "!" badge (Max Level Newbie, Constellations, I Stole the First Ranker's Soul); (3) flat bright blue rounded panel with inset item boxes, red variant for alerts (Greatest Estate Developer, The Gamer, Gardener in mint green); (4) themed: old paper/tablet (Fatal Reset), frosted glitch glass with serif text (Nightmare Realm Summoner). Shared rules: [square brackets], short lines, centred headline, the window exists IN the scene (perspective, mirrored from behind), every pop-in gets a DING/DU-DUN, achievements cascade with older windows fading.
- **Phones:** never a flat screenshot dropped onto art without a decision. Either (a) a drawn phone in a drawn hand in its own framed panel with a readable but simplified UI (Social Coin, True Beauty, Kittalk), or (b) the panel BECOMES the app, full width and flat, with app chrome — header bar, avatars, name labels, timestamps, ticks (Random Chat, Pyramid Game, Viral Hit, Dungeon Cleaning). Messengers are invented parodies (KITTALK). Reactions are lettered on top of the chat in the series' bubble style.
- **Public-information devices:** TV news with channel bug and lower-third (Genius Grandson, ORV), newspaper/infographic montage (44th Period), job-site page (Death's Game), forum/news comment pages (Dungeon Cleaning), stat/score cards for social judgement (Gangnam Beauty).
- **SFX:** hand-lettered, outlined, scaled to the action and coloured to the scene palette in every modern title; stacked vertically down the scroll for long sounds (THUMP THUMP, WHOP WHOP, B-A-N-G). Small life sounds are lettered too (DING, JINGLE, TAP, POKE, BEEP). No title typed an SFX in a plain font with a full stop.
- **Page background as mood:** black page for dread or grief (≈20 titles), navy for magic (Mage Again), white for daily life; transitions by gradient across the gutter.
- **Colour:** one dominant hue per scene; a single saturated accent (red in Bloodhounds, The Boxer, The Horizon, Money Game) is the strongest repeated device for real-world grit.
- **What reads as fake:** photoreal 3D (Caster), uniform glossy rendering on every panel (System Universe, Grand Duke's Third Son at their weakest), and photographic backgrounds with pasted figures (My Life as a Loser) — readers accept these only when line art and acting are strong.
