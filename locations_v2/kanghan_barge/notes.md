# Kanghan Marine barge — L24 candidate keys

Prepared for Claude review. These images have not been marked approved on the review board.

## Layout

Black rectangular crane barge, 80 m long in the brief, with a broad open foredeck. The retained key uses a dark-red lattice crane amidships. Its boom projects over the outboard water and carries two drill rigs suspended above the surface. Aft are a low white control cabin, grey/navy shipping containers and a compact rack of black harpoons beside the winches and chains. The rack is aft in the retained image; do not put it in the foreground as in the rejected attempts. The near hull carries the silver double-hexagon emblem. Black rubber fenders hang from the sides; simple rails and bollards mark the deck edge.

## Light and views

key.png: elevated bow-quarter establishing view at nightfall; visible cold-white floodlights are the key light. The crane, hull and deck are visible together. far.png: opposite broadside from the concrete breakwater across the offshore water, with the crane and suspended rigs silhouetted under the same floodlights. Treat this as a view with a long lens; the story distance is two kilometres. The anchored position is before the ch 39 grounding. Rigs are hanging ready here; lower them only for ch 38 drilling.

## Retained limitations

key.png is the best of five tries under the 27 October rule. Harpoon barbs, shackle pins and the precise chain gauge are too small to verify in the retained wide view. Its marine surfaces are still rendered more densely than the preferred broad cel planes. Use the brief's 2 m harpoon length and arm-thick chain specification for later close views; this image is not a prop detail sheet. Four earlier attempts are saved in _rejects/ and must not be used as references.
