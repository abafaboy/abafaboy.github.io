# Busan fish market — L22

Candidate location keys for Claude review, completed 27 October 2026. Design: CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md 5d and ASSETS_ARC5_BRIEF.md L22; scale supplemented by ch034 staging. All three PNGs are native 1536×1024.

- `key.png`: view down the broad central aisle of the covered market. Waist-high tanks on both sides hold fish, crabs and octopus; adjacent stainless ice trays, red tubs and white boxes leave the aisle clear. Parallel drains follow the stall fronts. Two small adult workers are scale accents, not cast designs. The hall is a huge single-storey volume, approximately football-pitch size and 15 m high in the script; preserve that height in panels. The insulated double door is on the back wall at the aisle's vanishing point.
- Key light: visible cold-white fluorescent strips on the structural beams. Slate and grey-blue steel/concrete, blue-green tank water, red tubs; wet highlights belong to water, floor and metal. No supernatural light in the ordinary market key.
- `cold_store.png`: from the market side through the open heavy double door into the freezer. Thick leaves, gaskets and attached hinges frame the sides. Frosted steel racks line both walls, holding plain white/blue boxes of frozen silver fish. The central aisle leads to a plain insulated back wall with a high two-fan evaporator and attached drain pipe. This is the room before the gate appears. It pairs with `../cold_store_gate/slit.png`, which places the gate in that aisle.
- `roof.png`: open flat concrete roof at dusk, a clear streaming stage behind the sea-facing parapet. Stair access and ventilation plant occupy the left/landward side; the harbour and boats lie below the far/right parapet. Warm low sunset from picture-left, cool evening shadows; an orange tent is small at the market edge below and a distant red lighthouse terminates the breakwater. This establishes dusk; chapter 39 adds its scripted night and violet-break lighting when staging panels.

Later panels should preserve the chosen view's fixture positions. Doors, boxes and boats have no readable markings. No named characters appear. No unapproved Wave C asset was a reference; another chat's breakwater and stall keys remain separate assets.
