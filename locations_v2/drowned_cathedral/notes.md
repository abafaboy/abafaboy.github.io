# L16 — Drowned cathedral layout and light

- Chapters 16–21. Ash-grey Far Side Gothic nave, 60 m long, 30 m to the rib vault; paired pillar rows, recessed side chapels and branching side passages.
- `key.png`: view along the nave toward the boss doors. Aisles are knee-deep; the lower side chapels are chest-deep. Floating wooden pews and silent bronze bells hang on chains attached to the vault. Two tiny generic adults establish scale.
- Main light is pale daylight through broken abstract cream, pale-blue and pale-pink glass. Water is dark slate/navy with crisp reflections; cool violet-grey shadows. Bells do not glow.
- Far-end stack, bottom to top: closed pointed bronze double doors, small red iron warning lamp, one hanging bronze bell, round rose window. `doors.png` gives the closer front view; door sill remains underwater. Door leaves have a central seam and one pull ring each.
- The left nave junction descends into `crypt.png`: low rib vault, squat stone piers, plain sarcophagi, entrance steps at the front-left, deep rising black water and a mostly drowned side-passage arch at rear-right. Some tomb lids remain above the water.
- The crypt is lit by pale light from the entrance at left and a small breach above. No electric or magical lighting is added. No people in the crypt or door detail.
- Story route beyond the high boss-room service exit connects through flooded passages/crypt toward the gate. A scout-sized crack behind the side-chapel altar is script continuity, not a new full-size entrance.
- Approved source images were used only for V2 rendering; none of this entry's new images was passed back as an unapproved reference. No named cast, boss, lettering, stake or sealing web is included.
