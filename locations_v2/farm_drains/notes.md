# L19 - farm drain tunnels

Chapters 28-30. V2 cinematic cel. Both kept images are native 1536x1024 PNGs, pending Claude's review.

## Route and scale

- `key.png`: old Far Side round stone tide channels, 5 m across, climbing in long switchbacks. Distinct arch ribs have shell-and-wave keystones at their crowns. The black-water channel occupies the left/lower side, with a continuous narrow grated steel walkway on the right. Low pumps and connected discharge pipes drive the water uphill. The route bends left into the next rising run. Two generic adult workers provide scale; their tabards carry no text.
- `shaft.png`: the final straight 120 m shaft, intended to climb at 30 degrees to the pump room below the rim. It continues straight past the round sludge mouth on its left wall, halfway up. The side opening shows dim violet-grey canyon light; it is not a gate. The right-hand walkway remains continuous to the top. Overlapping black round shields on a steel frame barricade the distant pump-room landing. No guards or acting characters are included.
- Maintain the canonical measurements in chapter panels. Perspective alone cannot serve as a measured slope or distance diagram. The final shaft uses additional receding ribs and smaller distant fittings to distinguish it from the nearer switchback tunnels.
- Roofs are intact in both keys. Falling slabs, keystone movement, overload effects and the crew's escape actions are later chapter events.

## Light and finish

Visible cold-white/blue caged lamps on stone arch piers; deep slate, navy and violet shadow planes. Selected ink cracks and damp stains. The canyon side mouth supplies weak violet-grey light. Metal and water carry crisp wet highlights; no fog or red alarm wash. The engraved shell/wave motifs are pictures, not lettering. No readable signs, numbers or interface windows.
