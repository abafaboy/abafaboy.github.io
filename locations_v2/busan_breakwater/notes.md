# L21 — Busan breakwater

Production reference for chapters 34–40. V2 cinematic cel. Native 1536×1024 PNGs from built-in image_gen; no resizing, retouching or re-encoding.

- `key.png`: dawn master view from the harbour, the kilometre-long concrete breakwater running toward the small red lighthouse. Two tiny adult figures establish scale. The sheltered boats and market are on picture-left, exposed sea and tetrapods on picture-right. Geum's orange tarp stands at the market waterfront.
- `night.png`: wide night view of the same place. The lighthouse's bounded cold-white beam sweeps right over the navy sea. Bare bulbs warm the orange tent and quay; concrete remains in cool shadow.
- `neck.png`: landward working area for chapters 39–40, with market, concrete pier, sea wall and the slender lattice light tower behind. Keep the market roughly 200 m behind the neck. In this view family, the sheltered harbour is left and exposed water/tetrapods right; the lighthouse is outside the frame.
- `end.png`: the outer lighthouse end, surrounded by open sea. Keep the market and harbour light tower out of close lighthouse shots: they are about a kilometre away. Two rods are supported at the low parapet and a bucket rests on the walkway.
- `break.png`: the neck during the break. The violet boiling sea lights the seaward edge and small crab/eel crawlers climbing through the tetrapods. White tower worklights and warm market bulbs remain grounded sources. The walkway stays mostly open for later action. One tiny fleeing generic adult supplies scale; no named cast or Deep Lantern is included.

Scale locks for subsequent panels: walkway 6 m wide; low parapet about 0.9 m; car-sized tetrapods about 3 m; red lighthouse 12 m tall, its access door about 2 m; harbour light tower 25 m; market roof about one storey. A brine crawler has a body 80 cm long and 40 cm high, six walking legs, two claws and an eel tail about 1 m long. Keep ordinary adults 1.6–1.9 m with full-length legs and feet on the same ground plane.

The neck, break and flashback references share the market-left / seaward-right composition. Keep that staging coherent across cuts. Lighthouse-end shots use `end.png` for local tower scale rather than enlarging the distant lighthouse from a wide key.

No readable signs, UI or logos. Do not copy any rejected candidate. Only approved Arc 1 rooftop/acting art supplied house-style references; parallel Busan and monster entries were not used before approval. These new outputs await Claude's review.
