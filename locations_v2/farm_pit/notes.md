# L19 - core farm pit

Chapters 26-28. V2 cinematic cel. All four kept images are native 1536x1024 PNGs. These are L19 deliveries pending Claude's review, not review-board approvals.

## Layout and cameras

- Canon dimensions remain 150 m across and 80 m deep. The Tide Warden is 30 m standing and roughly 21 m kneeling. Keep those measurements in later panels; the wide image is a perspective reference, not a measured elevation.
- `key.png` looks down from the old wave-carved north stone balcony toward the south wall. West is picture-left: the tall curved observation glass and extraction-tank terraces. The control room is across the pit on the south wall, upper centre/right. The freight elevator and the drain route are east, picture-right. The restrained Warden kneels at the centre, with wrist, neck and waist restraints, connected extraction pipes and his trident at his knees. Three elevated catwalk runs and the floor perimeter give working access.
- `control_room.png` reverses the view from inside the south control room. Consoles run beneath the observation glass; the monitor bank is on the left. The north balcony is opposite, the east elevator is to viewer-left, and the captive is far below. Ceiling work strips are visible. Every screen stays blank chroma green for later lettering.
- `drain_pipe.png` is the route reference: the open U-section trough follows the east wall past the Warden's cropped, cuffed arm to one round hatch. A narrow grated walkway follows its outer lip. The discharge pump, support brackets, open circular door and carved stone tunnel mouth are visible. Use this image for close route staging.
- `alarm.png` is a lighting edit of the kept key, retaining its camera, captive, architecture and two scale figures.

## Lighting and materials

Normal: deep violet/slate ambient, visible cold-white work lamps, smoky violet extraction light confined to glass sections and tanks. Matte stone, coral and skin; crisp reflections only on wet steel, water and glass. Tank caps should carry the silver double-hexagon and small crescent shapes without letters. Control screens are plain green.

Alarm: visible danger-red beacons become the main accent, with red bands on catwalks and wet floor. Keep violet extraction light and green screens. The Warden's eyes remain closed in these keys, before the chapter 27 eye-opening beat. No fire, blast damage or added smoke.

## Review limitations after the five-try key limit

The fifth key was kept as the best completed attempt. It improves the captive's scale, west glass wall, two scale figures and screen colours. Its west tank terraces are visually flattened behind the broad glass treatment; individual tank caps and their two emblems are not clearly resolved. It also shows a second east-wall round port, which must not establish a second canonical escape route. The overhead cranes are not clearly established, and the trident's break is not clearly readable. Some fine surface texture remains denser than the cleanest house-style examples. `alarm.png` inherits these limitations. Use the written design for those details and the single-hatch `drain_pipe.png` for escape geography.

M9 had no local folder when this job began and was not approved in the review board; the Warden was drawn from the approved written design, without an M9 image input. The approved Arc 1 boss-room key supplied rendering style only. Active images created in this chat were edited for corrections and the alarm variant; rejects were never passed from their archive folders.
