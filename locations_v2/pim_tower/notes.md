# Northern Relay — L24 candidate keys

Prepared for Claude review; not yet approved on the review board.

## Layout

A broken brass-and-stone signal tower, 40 m overall in the brief, alone on a dark silent plain. A broad octagonal stone plinth supports a tapering shaft in stepped stages. Tarnished brass ribs and collars brace the upper stonework. One circular service gallery sits immediately below the cracked rectangular signal mirrors and snapped mast. Fine attached wires trail to the right. Other relay towers are toppled along the distant horizon. The lone tiny cream moth-folk figure on the gallery is a scale speck, with antennae up and no headset; it is not a character model reference.
