# Busan PC bang — L24 candidate key

Prepared for Claude review; not yet approved on the review board.

Cramped basement café, roughly 6 by 10 m with a low ceiling. Two banks of gaming PCs face their chairs along the central aisle. The snack counter is at the rear, left of the open back doorway; the broom stands beside that doorway. The sleepy owner is behind the counter and one small customer sits at a far workstation. Both are scale figures. Keep desk, keyboard, monitor and chair positions usable and on one floor plane.

Visible off-white fluorescent ceiling tubes provide the key light. Warm-white keyboard lights and green monitor spill are local secondary sources. All PC cases and headset light rings are unlit. Every visible display is a flat strong-green replacement surface; sampled screen pixels are strongly green and suitable for keying, rather than exact #00FF00. Do not draw game content, icons or UI. All cups, cans, kraft bags and boxes are unprinted. No signs or product logos.

The third try is kept. Earlier tries are in _rejects/: the first had label-like snack marks, and the second introduced reserved cyan case lights.
