# Tower gate: layout notes

Approved by Claude, 27 October 2026 (`codex/reviews/L8.md` on `msgf-bible`).

## Outside (`outside.png`)
- **The view:** from across the street at dusk, an unfinished 11-storey concrete frame:
  - bare floors with no walls;
  - square columns on a regular grid;
  - rebar sticking up from the roof slab.
- **The site:**
  - scaffolding with green safety mesh and loose plastic sheeting runs up the right-hand side;
  - a blank grey site hoarding along the pavement hides the ground floor.
- **Around it:**
  - ordinary low buildings, trees and street lights on either side;
  - N Seoul Tower on its hill in the far right distance;
  - the sun setting at picture-right.
- **The gate:**
  - a narrow, jagged, vertical slit of black-violet light with a thin pale rim;
  - about the height of a door, left of centre;
  - on the sixth floor, counting the ground floor as the first.
- **Light:** orange dusk from the right. The lit faces of the concrete are warm and the shadows cool violet.
- **Scale:** each floor is about 3.2 m. Borro (2.4 m) is three quarters of a floor tall.

## Inside the gate (`key.png`)
- **The layout:** the view looks diagonally from an intact near-left slab toward the open far right side.
  - An intact perimeter route runs along the left edge, and two anonymous adults stand well back on it.
  - The central and right-hand slab has collapsed. Two thick surviving beams span the hole and meet surviving supports.
  - The lower level shows below.
  - Concrete columns and upper beams frame the unfinished storey.
- **Light:**
  - dusty orange dusk comes in through the right-hand openings;
  - large cool violet shadows separate the concrete planes;
  - green mesh lines the open edge, and the distant city lies below.
- **In panels:** keep the beam supports coherent, give adults full proportions with their feet on the slab, and keep one horizon.
- **The long beam** across the hole is 60 cm wide (chapter 10).
