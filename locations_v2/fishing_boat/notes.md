# Old fisherman's boat — L24 candidate key

Prepared for Claude review; not yet approved on the review board.

The boat is the small 9 m blue-and-white wooden fishing boat from ch 38, about 3 m across. The wheelhouse occupies the forward third, with a blue roof, salty windows and a door facing the open aft working deck. The stern is in the near foreground. The deck has an engine hatch, exhaust pipe, coils of rope and plain blue/red plastic fish crates. Tyres are hung on visible ropes as fenders; the hull has limited chipped-paint marks. Keep it a modest working boat.

key.png is an elevated stern-quarter view under cool clear autumn afternoon sunlight from upper left. Wet hull and water catch small bright reflections; shadows remain grey-blue. The fisherman by the door is an incidental scale figure, not a character reference. Chapter 38 later loads the team aboard, including Borro, so the boat then rides lower. The first attempt is retained in _rejects/ for its photographic surface finish; the second attempt is the kept image.
