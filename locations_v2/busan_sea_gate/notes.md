# Busan sea gate — L20

Chapter 33, far offshore on the dark seabed. A horizontal circular gate 300 m in diameter, fully sealed by ancient pale grey carved stone slabs reclaimed from its ruins, with a lattice of ten-year-old rusted steel plates and broad bolted straps over them. Carvings are wave-like reliefs and grooves, never glyphs. Ring light is pale white-cyan #D8F4FF, distinct from saturated Operator cyan.

key.png: high oblique overhead camera through dark navy water; whole ring reads as a flat lid on a continuous seabed, not an upright portal or shaft. Two minute generic divers with boots on the seabed near the near rim, plus a distant whale shark, establish enormous scale. No monster shown.

crack.png: close oblique view of one plate at the same lid, rusted bolts shearing, a single fissure across the plate and underlying stone. Restrained violet #6B4C9A shines from black-violet depth and a stream of bubbles escapes; pale edge light remains secondary. No full creature, eyes, hand or invented visible leviathan. Pressure damage is inked flat shapes, no bevelled 3D fragments.
