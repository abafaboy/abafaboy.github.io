# L14 — Han River gate flashback chamber
Date: 27 October 2026. Delivered for Claude review; approval remains in reviews/STATUS.md.

- key.png is attempt 2, selected after the five-try cap. A wide rectangular dark masonry chamber with square stone piers, a plain shadowed ceiling and flagstone floor. Closed double iron door sits in the far wall just right of centre. Riveted straps, central seam and attached heavy hinges give it a repeatable design.
- One caged white dungeon lamp is attached to the wall left of the door. Its neutral-grey wash picks out the jamb, rivets and a floor patch; the sides of the chamber stay in large dark grey masses. The image reads in greys, without orange costumes or coloured gate light. Tiny figures are anonymous scale markers, never Minseok or young Jaeha.
- CANONICAL SIZE REMAINS 4 m HIGH. Accuracy fault: in the selected drawing the door is about 2.7 adult heights, so it visually reads around 4.7–5 m for ordinary adults. Do not propagate that larger size into chapter panels. Eye level also reads lower than the intended camera height of 1.6 m. These remain unresolved after five attempts.
- The key is visually monochrome; a sampled grid found channel differences up to 9/255, so it is not mathematically pure R=G=B grey. No conspicuous coloured element is present.
- Flashback version is shut, without cables, wedge, machinery, inscriptions, the rusted lamp or the orange cloth. In chapter 26's PRESENT-DAY use, the script adds the steel wedge, cables and violet light; the letterer adds all writing. Do not draw letters into the location reference.
- Four discarded originals are ordinary PNG files in _rejects/. Attempt 2 was promoted to key.png and is not duplicated in _rejects/. The other versions drift farther in scale or make the figures too large in the frame.
- Attempts 1–2 used the approved A1_P11 for ink/cel technique only; attempts 3–5 used the written V2 block without image inputs. No new key or reject was ever used as a reference.
- Native 1536×1024 PNG copied byte for byte from built-in image_gen. No resampling or re-encoding; no saved previews or temporary project images.
