# L18 — Farm canyon continuity notes

Chapters 23–25 and 30–31. These are location drawings for review, not an update to the review board.

## Route and layout

- The inner gate rim sits on the high exit ledge at the head of a 400 m Far Side canyon. The narrow river runs far below. The canyon is sheer dark rock, with retrofitted steel walkways, cables and huge pipes bolted to it.
- `key.png` looks down-canyon from the ledge beside the rim; its cropped steel support is at picture-right. Descending pipe runs and zig-zag stairs follow the walls. The grey engineers' cabin is on a first landing; the second small service hut in the drawing is incidental. A locked grille closes a distant cleft toward the deeper route. The one anonymous distant worker is a scale figure, not a named cast member.
- `walkway.png` looks along a wall-side catwalk to a natural rock landing. The square spiral trap plate is flush in that stone landing; do not place it on top of steel grating. The outer guardrail borders the drop; the large flanged pipe follows the rock side.
- `relief.png` shows the 40 m ancient stone relief of a coral-armoured sea hero bracing against a wave. The crown, beard, armour and wave are all attached grey bas-relief carved into the wall, not a living figure. A narrow maintenance catwalk passes beneath it.
- `pipe_gallery.png` is the old vaulted tide tunnel near the canyon floor. Three separate parallel 4 m pipes rest on cradles and continue through three far arch openings. The service aisle follows the left wall. Its higher entrance-mezzanine camera is intentional: it makes all three runs visible. Wave motifs are carved into the vault ribs and wall bands.
- `junction.png` shows a round wave-carved chamber. A supported main pipe enters from the foreground/right and splits through a Y-manifold into two wall passages. One inspection-glass section on the left outgoing branch leaks violet light. The railed service route remains passable.
- `glass_wall.png` closes the gallery route with a 30 m broad curved glazed barrier. Steel mullions, fogged panes and condensation conceal the farm beyond. A huge pipe feeds through its lower left edge. The brass plaque has a nested-hexagon emblem and crescent only, with no lettering. Keep the contents hidden before the reveal.
- `rim_ledge.png` reverses the key's viewpoint: from the approach side, across the 40 m missing span, toward the exit ledge. The gate's vertical ellipse is 8 m tall, inside an open steel iris with overlapping blades, hydraulic actuators and wall anchors. The old crossing is broken at both ends; there is no intact bridge. The river lies far below.

## Scale, light and rendering

- Walkway clear width: 2 m. Guardrails: 1.1 m. Main pipe outside diameter: 4 m. Ordinary cabin door: about 2.1 m. Keep one camera horizon and full adult proportions for any later figures.
- Dim violet-grey sunless twilight fills the open canyon; shadows are violet/slate. Visible neutral-white work lamps are the key light, creating local pale pools and clean hard cel shadows. The tunnel lamps are caged or mounted directly to stone piers.
- Only the junction leak emits saturated Hush violet. The rim's thin ellipse emits white light with a very pale cold white-cyan edge; its interior stays black-violet. Do not replace it with saturated Operator cyan. No broad bloom or fog over the scene. The fog in the glass view is condensation on the glass surface.
- Use V2 crisp tapered black ink, restrained hand-drawn stone texture, flat local colour and hard shadow shapes. These are full-detail establishing keys; simplify their backgrounds for later dialogue/action panels.
- All seven kept PNGs are native 1536×1024, copied byte-for-byte from the built-in generator. No resizing, recompression or post-processing. No people in the six detail views.
