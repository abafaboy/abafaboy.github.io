Rings

Every Vessel is measured once, at sixteen. One ring is a commoner, three a knight, five a true lord. Seven rings belonged to King Aldric I alone. They built a cathedral on the spot.

Greyhollow

The last settled fief before the Pale. It has a keep with half a roof, a mill that won't turn, and frost that never fully leaves. Until now.

The Order of the Measure

Blue robes and silver lenses. They keep the Stones and the ring order. For three hundred years they have hunted the people they call hollow-born.
