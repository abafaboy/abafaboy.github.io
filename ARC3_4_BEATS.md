# Arcs 3 and 4 (chapters 13–33): chapter beats

**What this is.** `STORY_BIBLE_v4.md` gives chapter-by-chapter beats for chapters 1–12 and arc-level beats after that. This file turns arcs 3 and 4 into chapter beats, so the scripts for chapters 13–33 can be written. It follows the bible's arc summaries and power milestones exactly; where the bible leaves a gap, the choice made here is marked **(decision)**. The owner said "keep working. make all the decisions."

**Why chapter 33 matters.** The owner asked for the first ~30 chapters as one public long-form video. Chapter 33 is the arc ending nearest 30: Jaeha's body breaks, and Auntie Geum's line closes the season.

**Rules every chapter keeps** (`SCRIPT_FORMAT_v2.md`): danger, comedy or strangeness in the first five panels; the title card 15–40% of the way in; two turns; one smart move by Jaeha; one or two short laughs; a window with numbers for every rule, reward and limit; a fight that adds something new and that the enemy can really win; and a closing hook whose type differs from the previous chapter's.

## New faces (decisions)

- **Han Sora (27): Kanghan's eyes inside Black Hound.** She is the chapter 1 crossbow hunter (hunter 5 in `codex/CHARACTER_DESIGNS_v2.md` 2.5), who survived Gate 0912 with Mok. She reports to Director Baek. She carries a "core compass", a brass compass whose needle is a sliver of boss core and swings toward Operator signals. She gets her name card in ch 14. She is the fan-service woman of Arc 3: flirtatious, amused and dangerous. She is never Jaeha's love interest; she is testing him.
- **The Association hearing panel:** three evaluators in grey Association uniforms, chaired by Inspector Ryu (50s, tired, fair). A background role with no name card.
- **Kanghan engineers** (Arc 4): grey coveralls, the hexagon logo as an emblem with no letters, respirators.

## ARC 3 · BLACK HOUND (13–22): justice for Minseok

Power milestones: PASS I (15); Lyra (18); "Borrowed Thunder" (19); Nib's seal 3/5 (20).

### 13. The Hearing
- **Cold open:** a viral clip loops on every screen in the Association lobby: "HIS SUMMONS GO FIRST!" A guard checks Jaeha's "husks" at the door. Nib, masked, has to stand perfectly still while a guard pokes his cheek.
- **The hearing:** Black Hound says Jaeha set off the hidden boss at Gate 0912. Mok testifies, solemn, with a lawyer at his side.
- **Choi testifies:** the boss room lamp was red, the survey called it C-grade, and an F-rank and a rookie walked out of a gate that killed four B-ranks.
- **Yerin testifies:** "I watched him close that door." The room goes quiet. Mok's knuckles whiten on the table.
- **The re-test:** the Association measures Jaeha (F-rank; the protocol hides his class) and tests his "unusual husks" against a stone training construct.
  - Nib and Borro must fight like husks: silent and stiff. They are hilariously bad at it.
  - **Smart move:** Jaeha reads the test rules. The test ends when the construct's core is touched, not when it is destroyed. So he sends Nib straight through its legs to tap the core and wins in four seconds, showing almost nothing.
- **Verdict:** licence kept; summons registered as "unusual husks". [REGISTERED: 2 UNUSUAL HUSKS · OWNER KANG JAEHA · F-RANK]
- **Hook (threat):** in the gallery, the Black Hound crossbow woman smiles at him. In her lap, a brass compass needle swings and points straight at Jaeha.

### 14. The Compass
- **Cold open:** at a convenience-store table at midnight, Han Sora drops into the seat beside Jaeha, too close, and buys him a coffee (glamour beat for an adult woman; Jaeha flustered, Nib "......").
- **Name card:** HAN SORA (27) · BLACK HOUND · CROSSBOW. She invites him to the B-grade raid "for heroes" in person and asks innocent questions about his husks. He answers every one without saying anything.
- **Smart move:** he notices the compass needle swings toward Nib whenever Nib moves in field form. He realizes it tracks summons in field form, not him, and decides to keep the summons in rest form whenever she is near.
- **Investigation:** Bomi digs through Black Hound's old raid records. Four years ago, a Han River raid; one trap-tester dead: Oh Minseok. Choi brings kimbap and the incident report, which says "accident" and is signed by Mok.
- **Jaeha's ghost (short flashback):** Minseok pushing him out of a sealed room. "Everyone comes back, kid." The door closing.
- **Laugh:** Seoa pays Nib in snacks to fold laundry; Borro, under his rooftop tarp, has planted lettuce in the gutter.
- **Hook (personal):** Jaeha writes on a clean page: MOK. MINSEOK. HAN RIVER. Then he closes the notebook.

### 15. Pass
- **Cold open:** training in an E-grade gate Choi found for them: a ruined aqueduct full of ash hounds. Borro takes a hound's charge on his shield; the impact rattles uselessly into the ground.
- **Problem:** Nib is fast but light; Borro is strong but slow. Neither can finish the pack leader, an ash hound twice the size of the rest.
- **Idea:** while CARRYing for both, Jaeha feels the impact land on Borro and pass through his own body. What if he could send it on, instead of keeping it?
- **PASS I unlocks:** [NEW SKILL: PASS I · MOVE FORCE BETWEEN TWO LINKED SUMMONS · EACH HAND-OFF LOSES 30%]. Borro blocks the leader's charge, Jaeha PASSes it, and Nib's knife lands with a giant's weight behind it.
- **Cost:** PASS runs through his body; his hands shake and his ears ring.
- **Laugh:** Nib, after the hit: "I like this. Do it again." Borro: "Then you take the next charge."
- **Hook (power):** [ROUTE ALIGNMENT 60%]. Jaeha does the arithmetic: one more new boss type, and he can CALL again.

### 16. The Hero Raid
- **Cold open:** a press line outside a B-grade gate in an old dam spillway. Black Hound in clean jackets, Mok smiling for cameras, Bomi streaming live: 31,000 viewers.
- **Inside:** a drowned cathedral of the Far Side, half underwater, with bells hanging silent in the dark. The raid moves deep. Jaeha is "the hero trap-tester" at the front, as always.
- **Smart move, set up quietly:** at every junction, Jaeha marks the floor with chalk the way trap-testers do. Nobody notices that some marks point the wrong way.
- **Danger:** a trapped nave floods; Borro holds the doors while the raid gets through.
- **Sora's test:** she shoots a crossbow bolt into a trap trigger on purpose, to see what Jaeha's "husks" do when he's in danger. The compass needle spins.
- **Hook (threat):** at the boss chamber, Mok smiles at Jaeha: "After you, hero." The door behind them starts to close, again.

### 17. Thirty Seconds
- **The trap springs:** Mok's men seal the boss chamber with Jaeha's team inside, exactly as at Gate 0912.
- **Jaeha's trap springs too:** the wrong-way chalk marks sent Mok's sealing team down the flooded side passage, and Nib, scouting through LINK minutes earlier, found the chamber's second exit. The door Mok sealed is the one Jaeha wanted sealed: now Mok's own men are cut off, and Mok is inside with him.
- **The boss wakes:** the Drowned Bell-Ringer, a hushed giant with a cathedral bell for a head (B-grade, a hushed hero; its window offers HARVEST only).
- **Mok must fight beside the trap rat to live.** His shield takes a hit meant for Jaeha, and Jaeha CARRIES part of it for him, without being asked.
- **Laugh:** Nib to Mok: "You first." Mok: "What?" Nib: "Doors. You like closing them. You go first."
- **Hook (countdown):** [BOSS ENRAGED · 00:30]. The bell-head starts to ring, and the chamber's walls start to crack inward.

### 18. Lyra
- **The fight turns bad:** the ringing shatters stone; Borro is buried to the waist; Nib's field form is at 00:40.
- **[ROUTE ALIGNMENT 100%] [A CALL IS AVAILABLE].** Jaeha calls in the middle of the raid. The window opens on a river under a silver sky.
- **The call is a negotiation:** a voice laughs at him. She wants applause, she wants money, and she wants someone to find her little brother, Ilan. Jaeha points at Bomi's drone: "Thirty-one thousand people are watching. Make it a good entrance."
- **Lyra arrives (full-height splash):** the river archer, longbow drawn, silver braid flying, landing on the bell-head's shoulder. The glamour beat: Jaeha flustered and blushing, Nib flat: "......". Bomi's chat explodes.
- **Name card:** LYRA · RIVER ARCHER OF THE FAR SIDE · SUMMON NO. 3. [SEAL 1/5 · FIELD FORM 05:00]
- **Hook (power):** her first arrow bounces off the bell. "Count the arrows if you want. I only need the one." She doesn't have the one. Not yet.

### 19. Borrowed Thunder
- **The problem:** nothing they have can crack the bell-head; Lyra's arrows are too light at 1/5 power.
- **The plan (smart move):** Borro blocks the boss's hammer blow; Jaeha PASSes the impact through himself into Lyra; Lyra fires it. The first try fails: Jaeha drops the force (his nose bleeds).
- **The second try:** Mok, without being asked, puts his shield beside Borro's to take part of the hit, so there is more to pass.
- **"Borrowed Thunder":** the arrow leaves the bow with the sound of the bell-head's own hammer. It goes clean through the bell. **Payoff.**
- **Windows:** [BOSS DEFEATED: HUSHED BELL-RINGER (B)] [HARVEST CORE · EST. ₩900,000,000] (HARVEST only; plant for ch 50). He harvests it: the rent for a year, Seoa's art school fees.
- **Laugh:** Lyra poses for Bomi's drone and asks what her cut is.
- **Hook (threat):** in the dark, Sora lowers her crossbow, and her compass needle points at Lyra, then at Jaeha.

### 20. Three of Five
- **Mok turns:** with the boss dead and witnesses cut off, Mok decides to finish what he started four years ago. His second team arrives through the flooded passage.
- **The fight:** Black Hound against Jaeha's team in the ruined nave. Lyra is out of field form; Borro is spent.
- **Nib holds the line alone,** and Jaeha CARRIES everything that hits him until he can't stand.
- **[NIB HELD THE LINE FOR HIS OPERATOR] [SEAL 2/5 → 3/5]:** scout-captain armour (design pending, ch 20). Nib dismantles Mok's team in a page of fast, clean panels without killing anyone.
- **Mok's shield breaks** under Nib's knives. Inside the shield's hollow grip, wrapped in plastic: an old phone. Jaeha recognizes the cracked orange case. Minseok's.
- **Hook (mystery):** the phone's screen lights up: one video file, four years old.

### 21. Ask Him
- **Cold open:** Bomi's drone has been streaming the whole time: 214,000 viewers.
- **Smart move:** Jaeha doesn't accuse Mok. He asks him quiet, polite questions, the way he always has, and lets Mok's pride answer them on stream: the lamp was red, Kanghan paid for the C-grade survey, and four years ago Kanghan told him to seal the Han River door.
- **Minseok's recording plays** to the whole stream: Minseok, in the same orange vest, laughing nervously in a gate under a bridge: "If I don't come out, kid, it wasn't an accident."
- **The chat flips:** "trap rat" becomes "TRAP-TESTER", and the clip spreads before Black Hound can stop it.
- **Mok breaks:** he drops to his knees. Jaeha doesn't hit him. "You closed the door on him. I'm not going to close it on you. The police will."
- **Sora slips away** in the confusion.
- **Hook (threat):** Kanghan Tower. Baek watches the stream end. "Bury Black Hound. And find out what else that phone recorded."

### 22. The Last Seconds
- **Aftermath:** Mok arrested on the news; Black Hound dissolved; Yerin, Bomi, Choi and Seoa celebrate on the rooftop with tteokbokki. Jaeha visits Minseok's niche at the columbarium.
- **Laugh:** Borro's lettuce harvest; Nib refuses to eat it until Seoa tastes it first.
- **The phone:** at night, Jaeha watches the recording to the very end. In the last seconds, after the line everyone heard, Minseok turns the camera: behind him, through the gate's rim, the concrete pier of a Han River bridge, with the same orange sodium lights.
- **He knows that bridge.** Through Nib's eyes in ch 8, he saw an unregistered gate under it, guarded by Kanghan.
- **Hook (mystery):** the gate where Minseok died four years ago is still open. A gate stays open as long as its boss lives.

## ARC 4 · THE KEPT GATE (23–33): the core farm

Arc beats from the bible: inside, Kanghan keeps a boss chained alive and drains it (a core farm). Moon Ahin has come in alone to learn why her family's guild hires trap-testers for an unregistered gate, so the rivals must work together. They escape with proof; Kanghan's people inside the Association bury it, but Choi keeps a copy. Ends: Jaeha's body breaks under CARRY. Auntie Geum: "Your head is S-rank. Your body is garbage."

### 23. Under the Bridge
- **Cold open:** night, the Han River. Through LINK, Jaeha rides Nib's eyes along the bridge girders above the Kanghan enclosure: guards, cables, a gate slit glowing under the pier.
- **The way in (decision):** Kanghan's contractor hires trap-testers for "maintenance surveys" of the gate, paid in cash, no names. Jaeha signs up under a false name. Choi hates the plan. Seoa makes him promise to come home for dinner.
- **The crew:** five trap-testers, all F-rank, all desperate. Jaeha sees himself in every one of them.
- **Inside:** steel walkways bolted into a Far Side canyon; pipes as thick as trucks running down into the dark.
- **Hook (arrival):** a column of forty flawless white husks marching in perfect step down the walkway, and the woman in white conducting them: Moon Ahin.

### 24. The Puppeteer
- **Moon Ahin's intro** (glamour beat, tall full-figure panel; Jaeha flustered, Nib "......"): she has come in alone to find out why her family's guild pays trap-testers to walk into an unregistered gate.
- **Name card:** MOON AHIN (22) · A-RANK SUMMONER · "THE PUPPETEER" · HEIR OF KANGHAN'S GUILD.
- **Rivalry:** she despises trap-testers ("You sell your lives for less than my husks cost"). He calls her husks "very brave husks". She does not laugh.
- **Danger:** canyon wyverns (decision: new monster, small cliff-dwelling wyverns) attack the walkway; her husks fight in perfect formation and still lose three to a trap she didn't see.
- **Smart move:** Jaeha reads the canyon's old trap stones and steers her formation around a fall-plate. She hates that he was right.
- **Hook (mystery):** at the bottom of the canyon, a sound like a heartbeat as big as a building.

### 25. Same Kind
- **The truce:** a collapsed walkway forces the two teams together. Forced cooperation: Nib and her lead husk scout side by side, neither speaking.
- **Character:** she asks why he still calls his summons husks in public. He asks why her husks have numbers instead of names.
- **Fight:** a pack of ash hounds in a pipe gallery. Her husks hold the line; his summons strike; the first combo between rivals.
- **Laugh:** Lyra critiques Moon Ahin's outfit; Moon Ahin critiques Lyra's aim.
- **Hook (reveal, half):** the pipes end at a wall of glass. Behind it, something enormous breathes.

### 26. Core Farm
- **The reveal:** a hushed giant, a fallen Far Side hero (decision: the Tide Warden, a sea-giant in coral armour), chained alive in a pit of machinery, pipes driven into its body, draining smoky violet light into rows of tanks. Kanghan engineers work around it like dairy farmers.
- **[BOSS: HUSHED TIDE WARDEN · STATUS: RESTRAINED · CORE YIELD: 4 YEARS]** The window reads like an inventory.
- **Rule 7 on the page:** a gate stays open as long as its boss lives. Kanghan keeps this one alive to keep the cores flowing.
- **Moon Ahin's face** as she sees her family's logo on the tanks.
- **Plant:** the chains are the same factory steel, with the same stamped mark, as on the knight in chapter 1.
- **Hook (threat):** an engineer looks up from a monitor: "Unregistered summon signatures on level four." Alarms.

### 27. Alarm
- **Escape begins:** Kanghan security in black tactical gear; the facility's shutters slam.
- **Han Sora returns**, now in Kanghan colours, with her crossbow and a squad.
- **Fight:** a running battle along the drain pipes; Lyra and Sora trade shots across the pit (two archers, two styles).
- **Smart move:** Jaeha uses the facility's own safety protocol (flooding a section to stop a fire) to cut off the squad.
- **Hook (choice):** the Tide Warden's eye opens, and through the drain pipes, it speaks to the summons in their own language.

### 28. Not Equipment
- **The choice:** the Warden begs for death or freedom. Freeing it would close the gate (with everyone inside); killing it would do the same.
- **Proof first:** Jaeha decides they get out with proof first, and swears to come back. Nib: "Do you say that to everyone?" "Everyone comes back."
- **Proof:** Moon Ahin films the farm with her own phone: her family's logo, the tanks, the chains.
- **Borro's anger:** he hates the word "equipment", and here a hero is being used as one. He tears a pipe out with his bare hands.
- **Hook (threat):** the facility triggers a lockdown: [GATE RIM SEALING · 12:00].

### 29. Sandbags
- **The tunnels:** the way out runs through the drain tunnels, which start to collapse.
- **Borro holds the roof** like he once held a bridge, with the crew of desperate trap-testers running under him.
- **CARRY for three:** Jaeha takes the weight with him. Nosebleed. His vision greys at the edges. [CARRY LOAD: 180% · WARNING: OVERLOAD]
- **Laugh:** a trap-tester asks if this is normal. "Usually there's less... no, actually, this is normal."
- **Hook (countdown):** [GATE RIM SEALING · 06:00], and the last tunnel is blocked by Sora's squad.

### 30. The Rim
- **The final stretch:** a gap across the canyon to the gate's rim, with the walkway gone.
- **Moon Ahin's moment:** she conducts all her husks into a living bridge. Her husks break one by one under the team's weight.
- **Teamwork:** Lyra covers the crossing; Nib clears Sora's squad; Jaeha CARRIES for Moon Ahin's husks too, which no one has ever done for them.
- **Moon Ahin, quietly:** "Why are you carrying my husks?" "Because they're carrying us."
- **Hook (threat):** Sora steps onto the far end of the bridge and aims at Jaeha's heart.

### 31. Two Pulses
- **Duel:** Sora against Jaeha's team on the collapsing husk bridge. She is the best shot in Seoul, and she is fast.
- **Smart move:** Jaeha reads her compass: it points at summons in field form, so he drops Nib into rest form at the right moment, the needle swings to Lyra, and Sora's shot goes where the needle points.
- **Borrowed Thunder II:** Borro blocks Sora's last bolt, the force PASSes to Lyra, and Lyra shoots the compass out of Sora's hand, not Sora.
- **Sora falls back** into the dark, laughing. "Director Baek is going to love you."
- **Hook (cost):** they reach the rim with seconds left. Jaeha's hands are shaking too hard to hold the phone.

### 32. Buried
- **Outside:** dawn under the bridge; the gate rim seals behind them. Everyone came back.
- **The proof goes to the Association,** and within a day it's "insufficient evidence": Kanghan's people inside bury it. The footage is "inconclusive". The trap-testers' testimony "unreliable".
- **Choi** copies everything onto an old USB stick before handing it over, and hides it in a kimbap box.
- **Moon Ahin confronts her uncle.** Baek, gently: "Do you want the hospitals dark, Ahin?" She leaves his office without answering. (Baek and Ahin; not yet a break.)
- **Hook (threat):** Baek, alone, to Sora on the phone: "Don't touch the Operator. Make him come to us."

### 33. Garbage (ARC 4 FINALE)
- **Cold open:** a family dinner on the rooftop, everyone there, finally. Jaeha lifts his chopsticks, and his hand won't close.
- **The break:** his body gives out from months of CARRY: he collapses. Hospital. [OPERATOR BODY: CRITICAL · CARRY CAPACITY EXCEEDED 14 TIMES]
- **The diagnosis:** the doctors find nothing wrong. Choi brings someone who might.
- **Auntie Geum Okran** (name card: GEUM OKRAN (62) · RETIRED S-RANK · "THE WALL OF BUSAN"), apron over a tracksuit, one prosthetic arm, a ladle. She pokes his chest with the ladle and reads him like a menu.
- **The line:** "Your head is S-rank. Your body is garbage."
- **Hook (power, season end):** she hands him a takeaway cup of tteokbokki and a training schedule: "One more set. Then just one more." [NEW QUEST: THE WALL OF BUSAN] Somewhere in the ocean, a sea gate's seal begins to fail.
